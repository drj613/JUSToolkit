- **Same kit, different look**: Don Patch/Super Patch (18 entries each)
- **Same animations, enhanced hitboxes**: Bo-bobo/Shinsetsu (24 vs 33 entries)
- **Completely different movesets**: Ichigo/Bankai, Luffy/Gear 2, Naruto/Kyuubi, Nami/PCT

### Characters Without Base Forms
- Gohan: Only SSJ and SSJ2 exist (no base form)
