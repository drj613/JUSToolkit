# Arale Norimaki (Dr. Slump) — Character Guide Notes

- Source: "ARALE CHARACTER FAQ", Ver. 0.7
- Author: Kristian Inola, a.k.a. "Summonmaster"
- Version: 0.7 (dated 10/01/2007 per version history)
- GameFAQs FAQ id: gf-47894

## Koma Forms / Evolution Chart

Three branches on Arale's evolution chart, unlocked from her Help koma:

- **Support branch** (top): 2 koma, then 3 koma.
- **Battle branch** (middle): 4 koma, then 5 koma, then 6 koma (Laughter), then 6 koma (Power) (requires Obocchaman's evolution path also bought), then 7 koma (requires Norimaki Senbee's evolution path also bought).
- **Bottom branch**: cosmetic only (quick question, JUMP stage, music tracks) — no combat numbers.

No koma shapes are given numerically in this guide.

## Normal Attacks (no damage numbers given for most; author focuses on range/frame behavior, not numeric damage)

- **B**: turn-and-bum-smack. Short range, slow. Always chains into >+B. Author notes some autoguard-like clashing against other normal hits (unconfirmed mechanism).
- **>+B**: double poke with a stick. Good range/speed, minimal startup/recovery. Nothing chains after it without a support.
- **^+B**: detaches head upward. Links into ^+Y. Bad recovery if whiffed/blocked.
- **air B**: downward hand spike. Knocks opponent down; described as an instant ring-out on stages with gaps/pits (spike move).
- **Y**: poke with poo on a stick. Slower startup than >+B; doesn't chain to/from other normals.
- **>+Y** ("key move"): flings two poo pieces in an arc. Drains SP from the opponent on hit (does not return SP to Arale — no amount specified). Ricochets off walls; poo lingers briefly on the ground after landing. Cancels enemy beams prematurely on contact.
- **^+Y**: launches Arale into the air, sets up air Y / air B mixups.
- **air Y**: flings two poo piles downward at ~45°. Same SP-drain property as >+Y (amount unspecified). Ricochets off walls; disappears after a certain travel distance.
- **d+B** (Forced Switch): fast, long-range dash-attack version of forced switch. Has "some degree of autoguard" while running (author unconfirmed exact frames). Passes through Dr. Mashirito's projectiles and short-duration beams if timed correctly.
- **d+Y** (Guard Break): horizontal electricity shot (with Gatchan). Hitbox covers jump-ins and short range in front, plus slightly behind Arale.

## Special Attacks (X = "N'Cha Cannon" mouth beam, same base move scaled per koma)

- **4 Koma X**: weakest of Arale's 4 beam versions (no exact number given).
- **4 Koma ^+X ("Car Toss")**: slow startup, brief explosion after landing (can hit dashing opponents).
- **5 Koma X**: more damage than 4 Koma version (no exact number).
- **5 Koma ^+X ("Poo Toss")**: causes **Support Seal** status and drains **2 SP** from the opponent; does **no actual damage**. Large arc, some startup lag; ricochets off walls; lingers on screen.
- **6 Koma X**: more damage than 4/5 koma versions (no exact number); described as "thick and yellow."
- **6 Koma ^+X ("Earth Rate")**: hits both sides of Arale; described as weak ("not very much" guard-threshold damage); has startup time.
- **7 Koma X**: strongest N'Cha Cannon version — Obocchaman fires a beam too, plus Gatchan's electricity; "large damage" (no exact number).
- **7 Koma ^+X ("Everyone in Penguin Village Parade")**: near-instant startup (this move's key strength); comparatively weak raw damage vs. other characters' specials. Guaranteed to activate during the invincibility frames after a no-roll wakeup (press Up while down). Tends to break guard if opponent doesn't twitch-block start to finish; sometimes knocks far away on hit. Very poor recovery if it whiffs or is twitch-blocked at close range.

## Support Koma Forms

- **Arale 2 — "Poo Toss"**: identical effect to her 5 Koma's Poo Toss — causes Support Seal, no damage on hit. Slightly more startup lag in support form than the special version (author observation, unconfirmed by number).
- **Arale 3 — "N'Cha Beam"**: laughter-element mouth beam, similar to her X specials; noted as effective against Power-type characters due to the Laughter > Power damage relation used elsewhere in the game (not explicitly numbered by the author here).

## Help Koma Form
- Grants immunity to the **Judgment** status effect (countdown timer starting at **10** [seconds, implied] to KO) for whichever character she's linked to.
- Judgment can be cancelled early by inflicting another negative status within the 10-count, or by switching to a character (e.g. Dio) who is immune.

## Boosts
- Boosts give **+8 bonus to J-Soul** when specific characters are adjacent in the deck.
- Arale boosts: Goku (DBZ).
- Arale is boosted by: Norimaki Senbee, Obocchaman, Goku.

## Guard
- Arale blocks by covering her ears with her fingers. Does not stop Guard Breaks (as with all characters presumably).

## Passive Effects
- Naturally immune to Judgment status (making the Help-koma immunity redundant on her own character).
- Takes **more damage from lightning-based attacks** — combined with her Knowledge weakness (she is Laughter-typed, except her 6 Koma Power variant), this is cited as giving a stated **minimum of 2.25x damage taken** from characters who are both lightning-based and Knowledge-typed against her (author explicitly states "2.25 times more damage" for the worst-case matchup: Perfect Climatact Nami [Nami 6], Killua, Sasuke). This implies base type mismatch (~1.5x, matching the standard nature triangle referenced in other guides) stacked multiplicatively with a lightning-vs-Arale multiplier (~1.5x), consistent with 1.5 × 1.5 = 2.25 (author unconfirmed — the guide states the total figure, not the breakdown; the 1.5×1.5 derivation here is inferred by the summarizer, not stated in source).
- Other lightning-classified attacks to watch for (non-numeric): Edajima's forced switch, Mashirito's ^+B, Arale's own guard break (d+Y), Piccolo's guard break, Hamuu 3 support.

## Ultimate Action
- Select: Arale crouches and pokes a pink poo with a stick in front of her.
- The poo itself, on hitting the opponent, gives the **opponent** SP (amount unspecified) — the poking stick motion itself does not register as a hit; only contact with the poo counts.
- Can be repeated to hit the poo up to **3 times**.
- Turns the opponent around if the poo appears behind them. If the poo's outer edge hits, opponent is pushed back; if the inner edge hits, opponent is turned around and pulled closer (per "Playing As Arale" section).
- Misses entirely if opponent is at point-blank range (since only the poo registers as a hit, not the stick).

## Mechanic-Revealing Observations / Tactics
- **Instant Parade RO tech**: If Arale is standing within (or slightly inside) a stage's marked edge-guard boundary, a guard break into 7 Koma's Parade (^+X) special will reliably ring out the opponent, even if the opponent is behind Arale when the guard break lands — implies the Parade special has a wide/tracking hitbox or travels along a fixed path regardless of Arale's facing (author unconfirmed mechanism, described only via observed effect).
- Guard breaks in general: the standard follow-up pattern cited is guard break -> immediate N'Cha Cannon (X) beam, since players tend to dash in or re-guard after a break, walking into beam startup.
- Push blocking exists as a defensive option against the >+Y arc (mentioned as a way to avoid the poo threat while it's airborne toward a blocking character), and specifically against beam extension setups.
- Damage note from a discussion of Gon's Guard Up ability: cites Gon+Kon assault knockback reduced by half via Guard Up, referencing a specific combo knockback value of **150 dmg** as the baseline being halved (context: opponent-side observation, not an Arale number per se — included because it's the only concrete damage figure in the whole guide).

## Recommended Support Koma (non-numeric effect descriptions; no damage numbers given for any of these)
- **Franky 2**: delayed table-flip attack, large arc, covers much of the screen.
- **Kon 3**: repeated arcing microphone throws at (seemingly) random intervals; hard to escape/interrupt.
- **Lambo 3**: delayed counter-explosion of lightning if struck; causes the "shock" status effect.
- **Poo 3** ("Poo"): instantly-appearing poo that follows Arale horizontally at the height summoned; functions as both chip damage and a projectile shield/autoguard object.

Note: this guide is overwhelmingly strategy/matchup analysis (extensive "Playing As/Against Arale" and character-by-character matchup sections) with very little hard numeric data — most specific numbers found were SP-drain amounts (2 SP), a +8 boost stat, a 10-count Judgment timer, a 2.25x damage multiplier claim, and one incidental 150-damage reference from an opposing character's move.
