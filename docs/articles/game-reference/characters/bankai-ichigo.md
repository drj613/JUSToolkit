# Bankai Ichigo Kurosaki (Bleach)

- Source guide: "Bankai Ichigo Kurosaki Character Guide" (JUMP! ULTIMATE STARS)
- Author: Prottain (contact listed as Eric Siu, 2007)
- Version: not stated
- GameFAQs FAQ id: gf-47060
- Unverified community source — numbers and mechanics below are as reported by the guide author and have not been independently verified against the game.

## Koma forms / evolution

Evolution tree: `[H] -> [2]-[3]` (support branch), `[H] -> [4]-[5]-[6]-[7]-[8]` (battle branch).

- [H] Help, [2] Support, [3] Support, [4] Battle, [5] Battle, [6] Battle, [7] Battle, [8] Battle.

Koma 7 and 8 (Bankai form) require an upgrade item from a separate koma, Zangetsu.

Koma shapes given for the Bankai forms:
- 7 Koma: row of 1, then row of 2, then row of 4 (staircase, 7 cells total)
- 8 Koma: two rows of 4 (8 cells total, rectangle)

No HP/J-Soul numeric values given for any koma in this guide.

## Moveset (7 Koma unless noted) — damage listed as Power/Laughter vs Knowledge nature targets

Movement/utility:
- Double-tap side direction: Dash ("flashstep") — Ichigo passes through the enemy during the dash.
- Down: Block.
- A+Down (air): fast fall.
- A: Jump; A again: Double jump.
- A + double side directional: Jump then flashstep in air.
- Select: pull off mask, regains some SP; ~1 second delay to fully finish.

B-button (ground normals), no listed delay unless noted:
- B: horizontal slash, moves Ichigo forward slightly. Damage: 9 (Power/Laughter) / 13 (Knowledge).
- Side B: downward arcing slash, steps forward, decent range. Damage: 9 / 13.
- Up B: diagonal stab, lifts enemy slightly. Damage: 9 / 13.
- Down B: ~1 second delay; black shockwave forces a character switch (if a second battle character is in the deck). Damage: 9 / 13.

Y-button:
- Y: multi-hit slash flurry with black aura; hits everyone nearby, launches them; damage scales with number of slashes landed (mashable), more slashes the closer the target. Max damage: 20 (P/L) / 30 (K).
- Side Y: travels dash-distance, stops and turns to slash if enemy in path (does not hit grounded enemies); range approximates a Side B slash. ~0.5s delay after use regardless of hit/miss. Damage: 18 / 27.
- Up Y: flashsteps up (higher than a regular jump), slashes down any enemy in the traveled distance; no delay regardless of connect. Pressing a direction immediately after a hit chains into Down Y (air). Costs one jump from the jump counter. Damage: 18 / 27.
- Down Y (air): flashsteps straight down, then horizontal slash both sides, same horizontal range as Side B; ~1s delay after slash. Damage: 18 / 27.
- Down Y (ground): stabs sword into ground, shockwave guard-break, radius same as Down B shockwave; ~1s delay (typical of guard breaks). Damage: 18 / 27.

X-button specials, 7 Koma:
- X (Getsuga Tenshou): black energy arc, roughly half-screen range. ~1s delay to fire + ~1s after = ~2s total delay. Damage: 50 (P/L) / 75 (K).
- X (Random variant): same move but with half-mask on; must remove mask after firing. Larger arc, more damage, more delay (~2.5s total), and applies an "energy degeneration" status that drains SP over time (author notes: "not very much"). Damage: 75 (P/L) / 112 (K).
- Up X: diagonal flashstep, multi-hit slash along travel path (author: "insane speed"). If enemy blocks, they are not hit and Ichigo ends up roughly 1/4 screen forward and 1/3 screen up (this is the travel distance). Damage depends on hits landed; ~1s delay after combo finishes (Ichigo stands in air); costs one jump. Max damage: 49 (P/L) / 73 (K).

X-button specials, 8 Koma (larger/slower versions):
- X: same as 7-koma but ~2/3 screen range; +1s delay (must don Hollow mask first); no random-X mask-removal variant exists at 8 koma (mask is worn, not removed). Damage: 55 (P/L) / 82 (K).
- Up X: same as 7-koma but travels ~2/3 screen vertically, just over 1/4 screen horizontally; +1s delay (mask donning). Damage: 65 (P/L) / 97 (K).

Author's opinion: 8 Koma X-moves not recommended due to delay despite higher damage/range; "purely for style."

## Combos (numeric, listing max damage; P/L = Power/Laughter, K = Knowledge)

- Combo 1: B, Side B, Up X — 67 max (P/L) / 100 max (K).
- Combo 2: B, Side B, Y, Up X — 87 max (P/L) / 130 max (K). Fails if Y throws the enemy behind/above Ichigo.
- Down Y + Combo 1: 85 max (P/L) / 127 max (K).
- Down Y + Combo 2: 105 max (P/L) / 157 max (K).
- Y (maxed hits), X: 70 max (P/L) / 105 max (K); if the "random X" mask variant triggers: 95 max (P/L) / 142 max (K). X can be replaced with Up X.
- B, Side B, Side Y (no special): 36 max (P/L) / 54 max (K). Not a perfect link — delay between Side B and Side Y.
- B, Side B, Y, Up Y, directional immediately after (no special, finishes with a ground slash instead of Up X): 72 max (P/L) / 108 max (K).

## Mechanic observations

- Dash/flashstep passes through the target (Ichigo doesn't stop at collision).
- Up Y and Up X each consume one of Ichigo's available jumps.
- Guard-break moves (Down B, Down Y) share roughly a 1-second recovery delay.
- Blocking an Up X special prevents the multi-hit from connecting; the move still displaces Ichigo the same fixed travel distance.
- "Stop" status (a stun mechanic implied but not defined in this guide) can reportedly still be escaped using B attacks/Side Y to move, or Up X if SP is available (author unconfirmed on exact mechanism — guide only says these moves "move you forward").

## Help/Support Koma effects mentioned (non-Ichigo koma being used to support him)

- Eve (Black Cat) and equivalents (Devil Bat, Hanamichi Sakuragi, Gajira Norimaki, Sipuxiang): each grants one extra jump; stacking multiple does not exceed a max of three total jumps (author states this as an observed mechanic).
- Yoruichi (Support): gives an "ally boost" to Ichigo (specific numeric boost not given).
- "Big Three" supports mentioned by name only (3 Koma Trunks, 3 Koma Seiya, 3 Koma Sena) — no numbers given, flagged in the community as being frowned upon competitively.
