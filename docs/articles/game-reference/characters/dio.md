## Basic moveset (shared across Dio battle koma forms)
Damage is listed per opponent nature: "power/laughter" value and a separate "knowledge" value (knowledge takes more damage — 1.5x in every case below).

| Input | Move name | Dmg (power/laughter) | Dmg (knowledge) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| B | Sideways punch | 10 | 15 | Combo starter |
| B+side | Vampire beam | 10 | 15 | Good combo ender, hard to land |
| B+up | Backflip kick | 10 | 15 | Launches Dio airborne; aerial combo starter / dodge |
| B+down | The World: sideways punch | 10 | 15 | Combo starter |
| B in air | The World: downward punch | 10 | 15 | Aerial move for falling |
| Y | The World: 1-2 punch (single input) | 20 | 30 | Strong combo move |
| Y+side | The World: sweeping punch | 18 | 27 | Best combo move (strength + distance) |
| Y+up | Blood-steal jab | 14 | 21 | Stand-alone move, recovers Dio's HP |
| Y+down | Turn-and-punch (The World) | 20 | 30 | Good strength/speed/distance |
| Y in air | Knife throw | 15 | 22 | Aerial ranged move |

Other inputs (no damage numbers): Over-twice dash (red aura dash forward); A = jump / double jump in air; A+down in air = air-jump cancel, useful against aerial moves; L/R = supports/character switch (configurable).

Select = Ultimate Action: Dio pulls out a book; if attacked while reading, teleports behind the attacker (counter-teleport). Author notes it as situational/hard to use.

## Koma forms — Specials (X button)

### 4 Koma Dio
- X: "Muda Muda" — 5 kicks, only the first kick actually connects/hits opponent. Damage: 15 (power/laughter) / 22 (knowledge). Weakest special in Dio's kit; described as an "okay" combo ender.
- X+up: "Circle Knives" — Dio teleports knives around the opponent to hit them. Damage: 30 (power/laughter) / 45 (knowledge). Works well at long range and decently in normal combos; the only worthwhile 4-koma special per author.

### 5 Koma Dio
- X: "Muda Muda Muda" (tap X rapidly for more hits) — Damage range: 19–43 (power/laughter) / 28–64 (knowledge). Best combo ender; usable as crowd-clearer and ringout tool.
- X+up: "Road Roller" — Dio drops a steamroller, crushes foe, then bashes it until it explodes. Damage: 40 (power/laughter) / 60 (knowledge). Falls slowly, easy to dodge if seen coming, can self-KO the user; author warns not to use if Dio is your last battle koma (self-KO risk).

### 6 Koma Dio
- X: "Muda Muda Muda" (same as 5-koma, slightly weaker) — Damage range: 19–41 (power/laughter) / 28–61 (knowledge).
- X+up: "The World" (ZA WARUDO, Ultimate Action-tier special) — Time-stop mechanic:
  - Startup: about 1 second (faster than some other specials).
  - Duration: time frozen for approximately 2 seconds.
  - During freeze: opponent cannot be moved by attacks but still takes damage; if opponent was mid-attack, that attack is cancelled once they take damage.
  - Match/round timer does not tick down while time is frozen.
  - Player cannot use supports or switch characters while time is frozen.
  - Opponent can audibly hear "The World" activate and may block in response to negate damage during the freeze (noted weakness). Because of this, the move is described as more effective in 4v4 matches where opponents are distracted and less likely to react/block in time.
  - Author rates this one of the best moves in the whole game.

## Support/Help koma

### 1 Koma (Help)
- Effect: regenerates SP gauge if the player doesn't change characters or use support characters. Described as good for 1-battle-koma decks, still useful for decks with more.
- Compatible help koma synergies named (buff effects, no numbers given): Giorno (SP gain on attacking/blocking a support character), Jonathan (HP increase on revive), Rohan (immunity to battle/support seal), Gyro Zeppili (+1 max SP gauge), Taizo (SP gain on KO), Frieza (SP gain on multi-hit).

### 2 Koma (Support)
- Name: "Flip Kick" — causes Blindness status effect.
- Koma shape: 2 cells (1x2, "[ ][ ]")
- Damage: 22 (power/knowledge) / 33 (laughter) — note: for this move the "off-nature" grouping flips (power+knowledge vs. laughter), unlike the basic-move table which groups power+laughter vs. knowledge. (author unconfirmed — could be a typo in the source, flagging discrepancy)

### 3 Koma (Support)
- Name: "Niku no Me" — targetable/selectable support.
- Koma shape: 3 cells, L-shape ("[ ][ ] / [ ]")
- Damage: 36 (power/laughter) / 54 (knowledge)
- Effect: teleports to target, attacks once, no additional effect.

## Mechanic-revealing observations
- Damage against Knowledge-type opponents is consistently 1.5x the power/laughter value across every basic move and special listed (10→15, 18→27, 20→30, 14→21, 15→22, 15→22, 30→45, 40→60, 19-43→28-64, 19-41→28-61) — strong evidence of a fixed type-effectiveness multiplier (1.5x) that Knowledge-nature characters take from at least this character's kit.
- The World's time-stop: damage still applies to a "frozen" opponent, but knockback/hitstun-launch response is suppressed (opponent doesn't fly backward) — implies damage and knockback application are decoupled systems.
- Attack-cancellation rule surfaced: taking damage while mid-attack cancels the attack (observed specifically during The World's freeze, but likely a general hitstun/interrupt rule).
- Time-stop explicitly does not pause the round/match timer countdown — timer and "gameplay tick" for other state (movement) are apparently separate systems, or timer is deliberately excluded from the freeze.
- Blocking can occur reactively to an audio/visual cue at the very start of a special's activation (mentioned specifically for The World) — suggests specials have a distinct start-up tell window during which guard input remains possible.
- "Muda Muda Muda" explicitly supports rapid-tap input scaling hit count/damage within a single special execution (min-max range given: 19-43 / 28-64 for 5-koma, 19-41 / 28-61 for 6-koma).
