## Koma forms & evolution

- 1 Koma [Help]: Effect: Increase SP Gauge when using multiple Special Attacks in a short time.
- 2 Koma [Laughter Support] "Nandokakantokapatoroonamu": Removes status ailments from whoever is touched by a beam (can accidentally hit and heal the caster's opponent, or even remove the user's own beneficial statuses if too close).
- 3 Koma [Laughter Support] "Bakabooru" (Idiot Ball): No damage; throws a ball forward causing confusion, leaves target unable to react for a fraction of a second.
- 4 Koma [Laughter Battle]: Shape: 2x2 block.
- 5 Koma [Laughter Battle]: Shape: 3-wide top row + 2 squares centered under (`[][][]` / `  [][]`).
- 6 Koma [Laughter Battle]: Shape: `[][][]` / `  [][]` / `  []`.
- 7 Koma "Ikarin Patch" [Laughter Battle]: Shape: `[][][]` / `  [][]` / `  [][]`.

7-Koma (Ikarin Patch) HP: 192 (author states "his 7 koma has 192HP").

## Passive abilities

- Attack up when health is low (author rates this low-value).
- Gain more SP energy when using multiple Special Attacks in a row — roughly a quarter of an SP bar back for chaining specials.
- Take more damage from Special Attacks (i.e., damage reducers don't fully mitigate incoming special damage — still takes "neutral" damage from specials).

## Supports recommended alongside Don Patch (boost/synergy notes)

- Bobobo 2-koma: boosts Don's 4/5/6-koma forms; throws 3 weapons (scimitar, broadsword, axe) for good damage especially vs. Power characters. Bobobo 3-koma usable in some combos but hard to connect.
- Hekkun 2 or 3-koma: also boosts Don's 4/5/6-koma. 2-koma causes confusion and knocks opponent off the ground; 3-koma does slightly more damage and knocks opponent backward. Both usable in combos.
- Tennosuke 2 or 3-koma: third booster for Don 4/5/6. 2-koma drains 2 SP bars from opponent and holds them in place (setup for specials). 3-koma slower startup, inflicts a stop effect via a "BBB" (big blue blob) latch-on that deals damage.
- Vegeta 2-koma: boosts Ikarin Patch (7-koma); long-range beam, usable mid-Ikarin's-X without knocking the target down.
- Brocken Jr. 2-koma: fast karate-chop support, good damage for a 2-koma, does not boost Don.

## Mechanic-revealing notes

- Multi-hit attacks (notably on Don's 7-koma) synergize with SP-gain-on-multi-hit help koma effects (e.g., Ko Patch for 7-koma, Bobobo for 4/5/6-koma).
- Aerial Y is a multi-hit move that does not stop until hitting solid ground or a solid object — can hit up to 3 characters in theory, and is a self-ring-out risk.
- Y (grounded 5-hit) knocks the opponent over on the last hit, preventing further combo without support or a wall assist.
