## Dr. Mashirito — basic info

- Nature: Laughter-based (basic attacks). 1.5x damage to Power-based opponents.
  - Exception: on his "Knowledge alternative" (4A Koma), basic attacks instead do 1.5x to Laughter-based opponents (i.e., the 4A koma's nature is Knowledge, per author's implication — author unconfirmed on exact mechanism).
- Passive ability: gains more SP when KOing opponents of a weaker nature.
- Ally boosts: Hiruma (Eyeshield 21), Dio (JJBA), Bo-bobo (Bobobo-bo Bo-bobo).

## Battle Koma Specials (X / ^X), by Koma version

All Specials listed are Laughter-based (author notes even the 4A/"Knowledge alternative" Specials remain Laughter-based).

- **4 Koma / 4 Koma Alt** ([] shape 1x4, or 2x2 for Alt):
  - X: 36 damage — giant robot throws a big rock; rock breaks on hit.
  - ^X: 28 damage — giant robot pounds ground, shockwave hits anyone caught.
- **5 Koma** ([] shape 1x5):
  - X: 44 damage — throws giant explosive blue poo; high chance of overshooting at close range, better at mid-range.
  - ^X: 36 damage — bigger shockwave than 4 Koma's.
- **6 Koma** (2x3 shape):
  - X: 42 damage — fires a laser; pulls hit opponent (even guarding) to the edge of the screen and holds them there, damaging/pressuring until attack ends. If it pushes opponent off-edge, they float and keep getting hit until attack ends (unless blocking, in which case they stop at the edge without falling off). Opponent hit by the attack is knocked down when it ends — if carried off the edge this way, it's described as an instant KO. Author calls it "arguably his best Special."
  - ^X: 41 damage — 5-hit combo, ends with upward knockup.
- **7 Koma** (4+3 shape):
  - X: damage varies (not given) — robot charges then fires a standard beam.
  - ^X: 33 + 22 damage (two components) — same knockup as 6 Koma's ^X, then fires the arm for extra damage; opponent can jump away to avoid the final hit.

## Support koma guide (numeric mentions only)

- Bo-bobo 3: nose-hair attack, 36 damage, hits Mash's preferred mid-range.
- Katsura 3 (author's top recommendation): sweep does 0 damage but huge knockback (can send opponent to the ceiling); bombs thrown backward do 8 damage each; support has no startup lag, is "practically invincible" once out (only destroyed by hazards, and only if it's a guard break).
- Other supports (Cerberos 3, Dakki 2, Dio 2, Gintoki 3, Hah Hah Brothers 3, Hibari 3, Hitsugaya 3, Kagura 2/3, Rouji 2, Satsuki 2, Zoro 3) mentioned with no numeric data — qualitative recommendations only.

## Caramelman J (8 Koma — final boss form / alternate character)

Shape: 4 rows of 2 blocks ([][] x4, 8 total).

- Passive abilities: takes more damage from Lightning-based attacks; gains more SP when KOing opponents of a weaker nature.
- Ally boosts: Frieza (Dragon Ball), Papillon (Busou Renkin), Luffy (One Piece).
- Nature: Laughter (basic attacks do 1.5x to Power-based opponents). Author notes Specials are unusually Power-based instead, doing more damage to Knowledge-based opponents.
- Note: even though the Koma name is "Caramelman J," the in-battle name display still reads "Dr. Mashirito" (author observation).

### Specials

- X: 80 total damage — robot fires a laser at the floor that raises debris; the laser itself does 3 damage, and the debris impact does the other 80 damage and launches the opponent high. (Note: laser 3 + debris 80 as separately stated by author — total effective damage on debris hit is 80, laser alone 3.)
- ^X: 65 damage — robot grows legs and walks around; anyone touching any part of Caramelman J during this state takes damage and is spun away; movement is controllable but can't leave the current platform or jump; guard breaks. If Caramel himself (not the robot) is attacked, he's knocked out of the move. Some attacks reportedly always knock Caramelman J out of this regardless of hit location (author-cited examples: Dr. Mashirito's own >Y, Luffy's ^Y).

## Mechanic-revealing observations

- "Poo-based" attacks (Mashirito's >Y) are stated to be unstoppable/unaffected by hits from other attacks or moves that would normally interrupt (author examples: passes through 8-Koma Seiya's X, hits Caramelman J out of his ^X).
- Certain characters/attacks can knock Caramelman J's ^X user-controlled state out prematurely even from behind (author example: Ichigo's Y) — suggests the ^X armor/priority isn't fully omnidirectional.
- 6/7 Koma laser-type Specials carry opponents to the screen edge and can float them off-edge for a guaranteed KO if not blocked — an edge case combining hitstun/knockdown state with stage boundaries.
