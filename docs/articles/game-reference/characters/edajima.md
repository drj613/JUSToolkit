## Koma forms / evolution

- EH = Edajima's Help Koma (unlocked free, in D.Gray-man/Boss Tower-adjacent world per guide's "Planet X: Boss Tower" mission 9 — no specials used, more wins than other team).
- EB = Edajima's 8 Koma (Battle).
- WH = Wan Taren's Help Koma (unlocked free; required prerequisite for Edajima's battle koma).

Koma chart: `EH-EB`, `WH` (linear, two separate small trees).

Edajima has only two "real" Koma (Help + one Battle 8-Koma), plus a "hacked beta koma" mentioned but not detailed.

### Help Koma
- Shape: 1 block.
- Effect: increase maximum number of Special (SP) bars by 1.

### Support 2 Koma (author notes: requires Action Replay code to unlock/use, including on Wi-Fi)
- Shape: 2 blocks.
- Effect: Edajima performs his A-Special animation (text bubble + aura burst).
- Damage: 30 neutral, 45 super-effective. Steals 2 SP bars.

### Battle 8 Koma
- Shape: 4 rows of 2 blocks (8 total).
- Type/Nature: Power.
- Base Health: 224 (stated as the highest base health in the game, per author).
- Passive ability ("Principal"): gains more SP when KOing; hard to knock back.
- Ultimate Action (Select): sits and eats ramen; regenerates health slowly.

## Passive / Help Koma boosts (ally effects)

- Jaki: increases special gauge when attacking or blocking while health is low (author rates this "useless").
- Wan Taren: lets you see invisible characters (author rates "useless").
- Momotaro: increases SP slowly while on the field.

## Support recommendations (qualitative, no extra numbers beyond what's above)

- Gokudera 3 recommended to guarantee the A-Special connects.
- Yugi 2 noted as "abusable" to hit twice (no numeric detail given).
- Satsuki 2 (health regen status) and Seiya 2 (small heal + cures all status, different Koma footprint) recommended for sustain.
