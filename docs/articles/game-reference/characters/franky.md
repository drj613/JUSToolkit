## Character/type info
- One Piece character. Two of his koma are Laughter type; one 5-Koma variant is Power type.
- Unlocked at Boss Tower Battle X, Mission 9, by completing 5 sub-goals culminating in "Get 8 more points than your opponent" (final goal unlocks Franky).

## Koma forms
- **Help Koma**: Shape `[]`. Effect: decreases damage taken from blades. Author rating 1/5 — situational (only useful vs. blade-using opponents).
- **2-Koma**: Shape `[]`/`[]`. Support summon: Franky appears in front of the player's character and throws a circular table like his Down-B, but this version carries 3 objects on the table; if the table itself connects it forcibly character-changes the opponent. About a 1-second delay before it triggers.
  - 1 projectile: 5 dmg (knowledge/laughter), 6 dmg (power)
  - 2 projectiles: 10 dmg (knowledge/laughter), 12 dmg (power)
  - 3 projectiles: 15 dmg (knowledge/laughter), 18 dmg (power)
  - Table itself: 15 dmg (knowledge/laughter), 17 dmg (power)

  - Author rating 2/5.
- **3-Koma**: Shape `[][]`/`[]`. Support summon: Franky appears almost directly in front, loads for 0.5–1.5 seconds (variable hold), then charges and punches. Longer hold = more distance and damage.
  - No load: 25 dmg (knowledge/laughter), 31 dmg (power)
  - 1 second load: 38 dmg (knowledge/laughter), 47 dmg (power)
  - 1.5 second load (max): 50 dmg (knowledge/laughter), 62 dmg (power)
  - Does not break guard. Author rating 4/5 — considered his best support.
- **4-Koma**: Shape 2x2 `[][]`/`[][]`. J-Soul: 160. Type: Laughter.
  - Passive abilities: takes less damage from blades; takes more damage from lightning attacks; gains SP when attacked by opponents.
  - Ultimate Action: drinks cola to power up his Y attacks — 1st cola raises attack power, 2nd raises it further (2 is the cap). Non-cola drinks (identifiable by steam color — colas are white) give no boost. Can randomly fail to pick a cola.
  - Special A "Coup de Vent": loads air in arms, launches it forward. 30 dmg (knowledge/laughter), 45 dmg (power). Moves Franky back slightly on use; strong knockback. Author rating 4/5 (5-Koma version is better).
  - Special B "Ultimate Hammer": 0.75s knuckle-crack startup, diagonal forward jump-grab (compared to "B. Ichigo's Up X, but slower"), punches opponent down from the air. 52 dmg to Knowledge, 35 dmg to Power/Laughter targets. Author rating 3/5.

## Full moveset (Basic Moves — base/Laughter koma; author says to "switch the damage ratio for power and knowledge type enemies" to get numbers for the Power koma variant)
- **B (Regular)**: head-butt, moves him forward slightly, close range. 8 dmg (knowledge/laughter), 12 dmg (power).
- **Forward/Back B**: straight punch, moves forward slightly, close range. 9 dmg (knowledge/laughter), 13 dmg (power).
- **Down B**: picks up a circular table and throws it, decent range (nearly full screen). 10 dmg (knowledge/laughter), 15 dmg (power).
- **Up B**: fire breath aimed diagonally into the air, direction depends on facing, close range. 8 dmg (knowledge/laughter), 12 dmg (power).
- **Jump/Aerial B**: jumps and double-axe-handles opponent downward, moves forward slightly, close range (extended slightly by the forward movement). 9 dmg (knowledge/laughter), 13 dmg (power).
- **Y (Regular)**: boxer-stance multi-punch; hit count rises after each UA use, close range.
  - Base: 16 dmg (knowledge/laughter), 24 dmg (power)
  - After 1 UA: 27 dmg (knowledge/laughter), 40 dmg (power)
  - After 2 UA: 36 dmg (knowledge/laughter), 54 dmg (power)
- **Forward/Back Y**: finger-bullet projectile traveling to screen edge; after 1 UA fires 3 cannonballs, after 2 UA fires one big cannonball.
  - Base: 14 dmg (knowledge/laughter), 21 dmg (power)
  - After 1 UA: 24 dmg (knowledge/laughter), 36 dmg (power)
  - After 2 UA: 33 dmg (knowledge/laughter), 49 dmg (power)
- **Down Y**: 1-second stationary wind-up then straight-punch (Franky doesn't move); after 1 UA the punch detaches and flies further, after 2 UA it flies to screen edge.
  - Base: 14 dmg (knowledge/laughter), 21 dmg (power)
  - After 1 UA: 21 dmg (knowledge/laughter), 31 dmg (power)
  - After 2 UA: 36 dmg (knowledge/laughter), 54 dmg (power)
- **Up Y**: ~0.5s windup, then fart-propels diagonally into the air (direction depends on facing); decent range (about twice Up-B's range at base), UA increases range substantially.
  - Base: 16 dmg (knowledge/laughter), 24 dmg (power)
  - After 1 UA: 24 dmg (knowledge/laughter), 36 dmg (power)
  - After 2 UA: 30 dmg (knowledge/laughter), 45 dmg (power)
- **Jump/Aerial Y**: airborne diagonal-down fire breath (like Up-B but from the air, facing down), slightly more range than Up-B. Max 6 hits; UA raises damage per hit but not max hit count.
  - Base, by hit count: 1 hit = 2/3 dmg (k/l, power); 2 hits = 5/7; 3 hits = 8/12; 4 hits = 11/16; 5 hits = 13/19; 6 hits (max) = 16/24.
  - After 1 UA: 1 hit = 4/6; 2 hits = 8/12; 3 hits = 12/18; 4 hits = 16/24; 5 hits = 20/30 (6-hit value not given in source).
  - After 2 UA: 1 hit = 6/9; 2 hits = 13/(power value omitted in source); 3 hits = 19/28; 4 hits = 26/(power value omitted); 5 hits = 32/48 (6-hit value not given in source).

## Basic combos
- `B, ->B, Y, ->Y`: 47 dmg (knowledge/laughter), 70 dmg (power). Cannot use UA boost with this combo because the first Y launches the opponent away.
- `B, Up B, Up Y, B`: 41 dmg (knowledge/power — likely a typo in source for knowledge/laughter), 61 dmg (power). Also can't benefit from UA due to the extra distance created.
- `After UA, B, ->B, Y`: After 1 UA: 44 dmg (knowledge/laughter), 66 dmg (power). After 2 UA: 53 dmg (knowledge/laughter), 79 dmg (power). Can be extended into a support for more damage.
- `After UA, B, Up B, ->Y`: After 1 UA: 40 dmg (knowledge/laughter), 60 dmg (power). After 2 UA: 49 dmg (knowledge/laughter), 73 dmg (power). Good for ring-outs, but author notes only the 2-UA version adds enough distance.
- `After 2 UA, B, ?B, Y + Shishio 2 (support, used about halfway through the Y), X`: For 4-Koma: 125 dmg (knowledge/laughter), 152 dmg (power). For 5-Koma: 135 dmg (knowledge/laughter), 167 dmg (power). (Condition: "if all hits connect and it's done right.")
- `After 2 UA, B, Up B, ?Y + Shishio 2, X`: For 4-Koma: 121 dmg (knowledge/laughter), 144 dmg (power). For 5-Koma: 131 dmg (knowledge/laughter), 161 dmg (power).

## Mechanic-revealing observations
- Ultimate Action stacking: Franky's UA can be triggered up to 2 times, each application permanently boosting his Y-move damage and hit counts (and increasing ring-out potential) for the remainder of the stock/life, until reset. This is an explicit "power-up meter via item consumption" mechanic tied to a specific drink item (cola) versus decoy items that visually differ (steam color).
- Certain combos explicitly become unusable after taking a UA boost because the added launch distance/knockback breaks the combo's spacing (`B, ->B, Y, ->Y` and `B, Up B, Up Y, B` both called out as UA-incompatible for this reason) — indicates hit knockback scaling interacts with UA-based power buffs to break combo timing.
- Jump Y (multi-hit projectile-like fire breath) has a hit-count-dependent damage table separate from a flat "per hit" value, and UA affects the per-hit damage scaling without changing the maximum hit cap (6) — an example of a move where UA affects only per-hit magnitude, not hit count, unlike Regular Y where UA changes hit count too.
- 3-Koma support explicitly ties charge duration (0.5s to 1.5s hold) to both travel distance and damage magnitude in three discrete tiers — a charge-move damage/range scaling mechanic.
- Type match-up damage: nearly every move in this guide lists a lower "knowledge/laughter" damage value versus a higher "power" damage value for the same hit, confirming the game's type-based damage modifier system operates per-hit, not just per-move.
