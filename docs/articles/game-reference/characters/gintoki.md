## Basic Moveset (Battle Character — Koma unspecified for base moves, apply to all/most Koma)

Damage is listed as two values per move: first number is the higher damage, second is lower, without the guide always specifying which nature gets which except where noted ("or" pairs — read as Power-target-damage or Laughter/Knowledge-target-damage per the two listed number sets).

- **Neutral+B** ("Foot Thrust"): 1 hit. To Power: 10 or 7; To Laughter: 7; To Knowledge: 7 or 10.
- **Forward+B** ("Forward Swing"): 1 hit. To Power: 12 or 8; To Laughter: 8; To Knowledge: 8 or 12.
- **Up+B** ("Bomb in MY face!!!!"): 1 hit. To Power: 13 or 9; To Laughter: 9; To Knowledge: 9 or 13.
- **Down+B** ("Word Bubble" — Force Change): 1 hit. To Power: 10 or 7; To Laughter: 7; To Knowledge: 7 or 10. Causes forced character switch.
- **Jump+B** ("Side Swing"): 1 hit. To Power: 10 or 7; To Laughter: 7; To Knowledge: 7 or 10.
- **Neutral+Y** ("Construction Worker", hits both sides): 1 hit. To Power: 28 or 19; To Laughter: 19; To Knowledge: 19 or 28.
- **Forward+Y** ("Bokuto Throw"): 3+5 hits (multi-part). To Power: 19+7 or 13+4; To Laughter: 13+4; To Knowledge: 13+4 or 19+7. Bokuto thrown, travels up then comes back down.
- **Up+Y** ("Bomb Toss"): 1 hit. To Power: 21 or 13; To Laughter: 13; To Knowledge: 13 or 21. Edge-guard move.
- **Down+B listed twice** ("Headbutt", guard break): 1 hit. To Power: 24 or 16; To Laughter: 16; To Knowledge: 16 or 24. (Guide lists this as "Down+B" a second time — likely meant Down+Y; possible transcription error in source, author unconfirmed.)
- **Jump+Y** ("Mini Bombs"): 1+1 hits. To Power: 7+19 or 5+13; To Laughter: 5+13; To Knowledge: 5+13 or 7+19.

## Help/Support Koma

- **Help Koma**: attached character's special (SP) gauge increases when they block at the last moment (perfect-guard mechanic).
- **Support Koma 2** ("Oi Oi, Abune Jyaneka" / Counter-attack), shape: vertical 2-cell, Type: Laughter.
  - 1 hit. Damage: 0 to Power, 0 to Laughter, 0 to Knowledge (listed as 0 across the board — pure utility/status move, author unconfirmed if this is literal 0 damage or an omission).
  - Effect: Gintoki hides in a trash can, bursts out on being hit, applies Attack Seal to opponent.
- **Support Koma 3** ("Temakakesasenjaneyo!!"), shape: horizontal 3-cell, Type: Laughter.
  - 2 hits. Damage: 37 to Power, 25 to Laughter, 25 to Knowledge.
  - Effect: appears on scooter, hits opponent twice.

## Battle Koma

### Ultimate Action (all Koma)
- Gintoki stops, pulls out Jump Magazine and reads (sometimes falls to floor depressed). Regains SP.

### Passive Effect
- None listed.

### 4 Koma (2x2 shape, Laughter)
- **Special A** "Temakakesasenjaneyo!!": 2 hits. Damage: 46 Power, 25 Laughter, 25 Knowledge. (Same move name as Support 3 but higher Power-damage value here — 46 vs 37.)
- **Special B** "MY PARFAIT!!" (counter-attack): 3 hits. Damage: 46 Power, 31 Laughter, 31 Knowledge.

### 5 Koma (2-then-3 stacked shape, Laughter)
- **Special A** "Okaayy Neexxt!!": 6 hits, vacuum-effect rush attack. Damage: 63 Power, 42 Laughter, 42 Knowledge.
- **Special B** "Tansuika Futsutoaino!!" (counter/heal): 4 hits. Damage: 43 Power, 31 Laughter, 31 Knowledge. If not hit while countering, heals instead of attacking.
- Author rates this his best Koma: "Special A is really fast and does good damage."

### 6 Koma — Laughter variant (3x2 shape)
- **Special A** "Kiyaaaaaa!!": 9 hits, scooter dash-and-explode. Damage: 76 Power, 47 Laughter, 47 Knowledge.
- **Special B** "AAAAAA!!": 7 hits, scooter launches straight up. Damage: 72 Power, 48 Laughter, 48 Knowledge.

### 6 Koma — Power variant (3x2 shape)
- Same two specials, same hit counts, but damage-vs-nature values are mirrored: Special A: 47 Power, 47 Laughter, 76 Knowledge. Special B: 48 Power, 48 Laughter, 72 Knowledge.
- (Notable: two separate 6-Koma forms exist for Gintoki, differing only in Nature typing and which opposing nature takes bonus damage — same numeric magnitudes, different nature assignment.)

### 7 Koma (1-then-3x3 shape, Laughter)
- **Special A** "Sadaharu, Sanbo no Jikanda": 4 hits. Damage: 82 Power, 55 Laughter, 55 Knowledge. Rides Sadaharu, rams opponent 3 times then Gintoki hits once more.
- **Special B** "...Butsutakiru": no damage numbers given — grants SP and "Deck Power" gain (buff, not an attack).

## Combos (mechanic-revealing notes)

- **Combo 1**: Neutral B -> Forward+B -> Neutral B. Short, "good damage especially on the last hit" (no numeric total given).
- **Combo 2**: Neutral B -> Up+B -> Neutral B. Similar to Combo 1.
- **Combo 3** (5 Koma only): Neutral B -> Forward+B (or Up+B) -> Neutral+Y -> Special A, chained immediately. Described as easy, fast, high damage, "possibility of a ring out" (no exact numeric total given).
- Author notes Gintoki has "almost no combos even with his 4 and 5 koma," relying instead on constant pressure/keep-away with projectiles (bokuto throw, bombs, mini bombs).

## Support Synergy Notes (non-numeric, strategy — kept only where it reveals mechanics)
- Shishio (Rurouni Kenshin) 2 Koma: raises grabbed opponent up first, creating an extra window to land hits — implies a distinct "hold/juggle" state usable for follow-ups.
- Sena (Eyeshield 21) 3 Koma: stops opponent movement and drains SP.
- Satsuki (Ichigo 100%) 2 Koma + Gintoki 5 Koma Special B: used together for extra healing (stacking heal-on-hit effects).
