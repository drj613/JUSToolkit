## Koma Forms

**Help Koma**: Increases SP gauge when multi-hitting. Author notes a specific mechanic detail: multi-hit attacks grant a chunk of SP at the start of the hit sequence, then the per-hit SP gain drops off sharply for subsequent hits, and SP gain stops entirely after a certain number of hits regardless of how long the attack continues. This Koma only increases the initial SP chunk, not the SP for later hits, and does not extend the point at which SP gain stops.

**Support Koma 2** — shape: 2 tiles side by side. Type: Power. Effect: Kamehameha wave attack.
- vs Power/Laughter: 20 damage
- vs Knowledge: 30 damage

**Support Koma 3** — shape: 2 tiles top + 1 below (3 total). Type: Power. Effect: Masenko fired upward at 45-degree angle.
- vs Power/Laughter: 28 damage
- vs Knowledge: 42 damage

**Battle Koma 4 (SSJ1)** — HP: 136. Type: Power. Shape: 4 tiles in a vertical column.
- Passive: when KO'd, Max SP and Max HP/SP increase (no exact amounts given; author unconfirmed).
- Ultimate Action: Hold Select to regain SP.
- Special A — Kamehameha Wave: lasts about 1/2 second.
  - vs Power/Laughter: 36 damage
  - vs Knowledge: 54 damage
- Special B — Masenko, fired upward at 45-degree angle.
  - vs Power/Laughter: 28 damage
  - vs Knowledge: 42 damage

**Battle Koma 5 (SSJ2)** — HP: 152. Type: Power. Shape: 3 tiles top row, 2 tiles bottom row (5 total).
- Special A — Father & Son Kamehameha: strong Kamehameha wave that pushes the opponent back; also hits characters hanging on ledges.
  - vs Power/Laughter: 42 damage
  - vs Knowledge: 62 damage
- Special B — Burst of Anger: increases damage dealt by 25%, lasts 5 seconds. Note: does NOT affect Gohan's Forward+Y.

## Combos (listed for movement/hitstun implications, not strategy)

- B, Up+B, Up+Y — simple hit chain into launcher into upward Ki blast.
- Forward+B, Y — kick pushes opponent forward then rapid punch; author notes this whiffs on some characters because Forward+B's knockback distance varies by character weight/hitstun, sending some too far for Y to connect. Reported to connect for up to 2 (sometimes 3) repeated Forward+B inputs depending on opponent.
- Up+B, Forward+B, Y — launcher into juggle into finisher.
- "Chain Combo": Forward+B, Y, Up+Y, repeat — can loop and keep the opponent airborne, limited by stage space and whether attacks push the opponent too far. Author reports achieving the loop 3 times in a row before losing the opponent.

## Ally Boosts

- **Son Goku (Dragon Ball)**: Help Koma increases max HP when returning from a KO (no exact amount given). Support 2 fires a Kamehameha wave in the facing direction — near-instant, pairs well with Gohan's Special A for extra damage/guard break. Support 3 (Super Saiyajin 3 Goku): player picks a target, Goku teleports behind them and fires a Kamehameha; has a delay between target selection and the attack landing.
- **Nico Robin (One Piece)**: Help Koma causes auto-guard (a shield appears right before being hit, preventing HP loss but costing SP each activation; works even while already blocking). Support 2 requires target selection, applies a blindness status — useful for guard breaks in 1v1. Support 3 causes hands to rise from the floor and push all opponents to the edge of the player's screen — useful for setting up ring-outs near a ledge.
- **Kuririn/Krillin (Dragon Ball, 4-koma only)**: Help Koma increases SP gauge when using a support to attack or block. Support 2 activates Solar Flare (blinds opponents, affects all nearby enemies, works faster than Robin's blind). Support 3 uses Destructo Disk (guard breaks, has a delay, knocks the opponent back and does not allow a follow-up).
- **Piccolo (Dragon Ball, 5-koma only)**: Help Koma steadily increases HP over time (regen, no exact rate given). Support 2 stretches an arm to drag the opponent in front of the player — most useful near a ledge combined with Y into "summon Piccolo" into Y again to knock them off; possible mid-stage but requires precise timing. Support 3 fires Special Beam Cannon, but has a delay; author considers other supports better damage-for-space.

## Supports (general testing notes)

- **2-koma Goku/Vegeta**: both fire a beam blast combo-able with Special A. Vegeta's beam does +2 damage over Goku's and is slightly faster.
- **2-koma Gotenks (Dragon Ball)**: beam blast, Laughter-type attack — deals more damage to Power-type opponents than Goku/Vegeta's beam, and the same amount to Laughter/Knowledge types as Goku's beam does to Power types.
- **2-koma Shishio (Rurouni Kenshin)**: runs up, grabs the opponent, inflicts a burn status; can combo into Kamehameha wave while opponent is stunned by the grab.
- **2-koma Satsuki Kitaooji (Ichigo 100%)**: grants health regeneration effect to the player's character — useful given Gohan's low health; stacking with Piccolo's help-koma regen slightly increases healing rate.

## Aerial Tactics

- Aerial Y (Ki blast, 45-degree downward) can be fired again almost immediately after the first shot connects/misses/is blocked — allows rapid follow-up blasts while airborne.
- Aerial B (kick) is riskier: whiffing leaves Gohan open, but hitting an opponent near a ledge can knock them off the stage entirely (setting up edge guard). If Aerial B connects, the opponent is knocked too far away for a follow-up Ki blast to also land.

## Guard Breaking

- 5-koma Special A combined with a 2-koma Goku/Vegeta/Trunks beam support can force a guard break: use the 2-koma support first to wear down the opponent's guard/shield, then the Special to break guard and deal damage. Near a ledge this can deal 22-28 damage and force the opponent off-stage.
- 2-koma beam support immediately followed by Gohan's Down+Y can also force a guard break, since the opponent is distracted by the incoming beam and doesn't react to the guard-break input in time.

## Edge Guarding

- Forward+Y hits characters hanging on the ledge and can continue hitting them if they try to climb back up, preventing recovery. If Gohan stands exactly on the edge, the beams will miss ledge-grabbers, exposing Gohan to an attack from behind. There is a delay/pause after the 16-hit beam sequence ends.
- 5-koma Special A (Father & Son Kamehameha) also covers the stage floor, combos with a 2-koma beam support for ring-outs; costs 2 SP bars per the author.

## Passive/Misc Numeric Notes

- Help Koma "Increase Max Health" (generic, non-character-specific koma): +8 HP, same amount as one ally boost step (author unconfirmed exact universality, but directly stated as equal to an ally boost increment).

## Mechanic-Revealing Observations

- Multi-hit SP gain mechanic (from Help Koma description): SP gain per hit front-loads at the start of a multi-hit attack and tapers off, then stops entirely after some threshold number of hits — a general SP-gain rule, not unique to Gohan, revealed via his Help Koma's effect description.
- Down+B (and Down+Y at Battle Koma level) are guard-break/character-swap-on-hit inputs, consistent with other characters' documented Down+B/Down+Y behavior in this source set.
- Forward+B's knockback distance is stated to vary by opposing character, which changes whether a follow-up Y in a combo will connect — evidence that different characters have different hit-stun/knockback-distance values (weight-class-like behavior).
- 5-koma Special B ("Burst of Anger") explicitly excludes Forward+Y from its +25% damage buff — indicates per-move exception flags exist within buff systems rather than a single global damage multiplier.
- Auto-guard (Nico Robin help koma) triggers even while manually blocking and costs SP per activation — reveals SP is consumed by defensive/passive procs, not just offensive actions.
