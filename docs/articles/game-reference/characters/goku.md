## Evolution chart

```
       |- [S2]--[S3]
       |
[H]-| -|- [B4]--[B5]--[B6]--[B7]--[B8]
       |
       | -[B4L]
```

- B4L is unlocked via a path in Kaiousama's chart.
- B8 is unlocked via a path in Vegeta's chart, after Vegeta's 6 Koma.

Goku 6/7 koma = Super Saiyan form. Goku 8 koma = Vegetto (fusion of Goku + Vegeta).

## Koma stats

Support:
- 2 Koma Support — "Kamehame ha": shape 2 cells horizontal. Damage: 29 (Power/Laughter), 43 (Knowledge).
- 3 Koma Support — "Warp kamehame ha": shape 2 horizontal + 1 below. Damage: 29 (P/L), 43 (K).

Battle koma:

- 4 Koma Goku: Type Laughter and Power (has two shape variants). Health: 152.
  - Special A "Kamehame ha": no special effect. Damage: 28 (P/L), 42 (K).
  - Special B "Dashare – Cheap Joke": causes freeze effect and drains 1 SP bar. Damage: 0.
  - Shape (Power variant): row of 3 + 1 centered below. Shape (Laughter variant): row of 3 + 1 below-left.

- 5 Koma Goku: Type Power. Health: 168.
  - Special A "Kaio Ken x20 kamehame ha": no special effect. Damage: 40 (P/L), 60 (K).
  - Special B "Kaio Ken": no listed status effect; can hit up to 5 times by pressing X repeatedly. Damage: 36 (P/L), 54 (K).
  - Shape: 2x2 block + 1 cell below.

- 6 Koma Goku: Type Power. Health: 184.
  - Special A "Chou kamehameha": no effect. Damage: 43 (P/L), 64 (K).
  - Special B "Genki Dama": attack power increases with the number of characters in your deck. Damage: 50-70 (P/L), 75-105 (K).
  - Shape: 2x3 block.

- 7 Koma Goku: Type Power. Health: 200.
  - Special A "Warping chou kamehameha": press forward or back to teleport before firing. Damage: 56 (P/L), 84 (K).
  - Special B "Genki Dama": attack power increases with number of characters in deck. Damage: 55-80 (P/L), 82-120 (K).
  - Shape: bottom row of 4, top row of 3 offset right.
  - Author note: the Super Saiyan transformation adds delay to kamehameha, making some combos harder/impossible at 7 koma.

- 8 Koma Goku (Vegetto): Type Power. Health: 216.
  - Special A "Warping Kamehame ha": tap any direction to teleport before firing. Damage: 72 (P/L), 108 (K).
  - Special B "Vegetto Sword": no listed effect. Damage: 50 (P/L), 75 (K).
  - Shape: 2x4 block (4 rows of 2).

## Ally boosts / passives

- Son Goku (base): Ultimate Action — hold Select to recover SP gradually. Ally boosts: Kamesennin, Gohan, Arale. Passive: when KO'd and returning, max health and SP gauge are increased (no numeric amount given).
- Super Saiyan Son Goku: Ally boosts: Gohan, Kuririn, Vegeta. Passives: gains more SP when health is low (no numeric threshold/rate given); when KO'd and returning, max SP gauge increases (no amount given).
- Vegetto: Ally Boosts: Kamesennin, Mr. Satan, Gotenks. Passive: gains SP energy when blocking (no rate given).

## Combos (move sequences; damage totals not quantified in the guide except the FAQ note below)

General combos (usable on any Goku koma, use Special A only):
- B, Forward B, Up Y, kamehameha
- B, Up B, Y, Forward Y
- "Can't touch this" (equip 2 Koma Kurapica): B, Forward B, Up Y, activate Kurapica, kamehameha — result: Attack Sealed opponent
- "Shield shatter" (equip 2 Koma Keigo Atobe): B, Forward B, Up Y, activate Keigo, kamehameha
- "Sudden Death special": B, Forward B, Y, Forward Y x3 — works well with help koma Sanosuke; even on block, some SP is gained
- "Motionless pain" (equip 3 Koma Shun): B, Up B, activate Shun, Y, X
- "BoBo kill" (7 Koma Bo-bobo + 2 Koma Gyro Zeppelin): B, Forward B, Y, activate Gyro, dream combo starting with Bo-bobo
- "Ultimate Breaker" (3 Koma Mello): B, Forward B, Y, activate Mello, Forward Y, X — designed to break guard

4 Koma specific:
- "SP Drain" (3 Koma Shun or 2 Koma Nube): Combo 1: B, Up B, Up Y, Shun/Nube, Up X, repeat. Combo 2: B, Forward B, Up Y, activate Shun/Nube, Up X, repeat. Drains a lot of SP from both sides (author claim).

5 Koma specific:
- "Kaio Ken combo" (2 Koma Rei): B, Forward B, Forward Y x3, Up X (all 5 hits), activate Rei
- "Abyss Death" (2 Koma Nube): B, Forward B, Y, activate Nube, Up X (5 hits), Up X (5x) — abuses the "paralysis glitch" (see below) to potentially double the combo

6 Koma: guide refers reader to 7 koma combos (no separate list).

7 Koma specific:
- "Genki Dama finisher" (3 Koma Shun): B, Forward B, Y, activate Shun, Up X — needs 6 komas total in deck (besides Goku) to charge Genki Dama to its second stage
- "Critical Genki Dama" (2 Koma Nube): B, Forward B, Y, activate Nube, Up X — needs 10 komas total besides Goku for Genki Dama's third charge level
- "Ultimate kame hame ha" (7/8 koma only, 3 Koma Seto Kaiba): B, Forward B, Forward Y x3, activate Seto Kaiba, X (teleporting forward)
- "No escape" (2 Koma Nube): B, Forward B, Forward Y x3, activate Nube, X, X — abuses paralysis glitch, big knockback; best on 7/8 koma for longer-lasting kamehameha

8 Koma (Vegetto) specific:
- "Maniac swing": B, Forward B, Up X
- "Up and Away": B, Forward B, Up Y, Up X — timing note: Vegetto Sword cannot be used immediately after Up Y or it teleports up instead
- "Ultimate Breaker" (3 Koma Mello): B, Forward B, Y, activate Mello, Forward Y, Up+X

FAQ answer on max combo damage: author states damage varies by attack and nature, but claims to have exceeded 100 damage in a combo (author unconfirmed, no breakdown given).

## Mechanic observations

- "Paralysis glitch" (author's term): if a multi-hit attack (e.g., kamehameha) is already in progress when a paralysis effect (e.g., from Shun) would apply, the opponent remains paralyzed after the multi-hit attack finishes — i.e., paralysis status is not consumed/cleared by ongoing hitstun from the attack that was already landing. Used for guaranteed ring-outs and for repeating combos.
- Genki Dama (Special B, 6/7 koma) has multiple charge tiers based on total koma count in the deck: implied stage 2 at "6 komas besides Goku," stage 3 at "10 komas besides Goku" (author-specific claims, unconfirmed).
- 7 Koma's Super Saiyan transformation adds startup delay to kamehameha relative to other Goku komas (author claim).
- Vegetto Sword (8 Koma Special B) cannot be chained immediately after Up Y — doing so causes a teleport-up instead of the sword swing, implying an input-timing/state conflict between the two moves.
- Special B "Kaio Ken" (5 Koma) can be hit up to 5 times via repeated X presses (mashable multi-hit, same as noted for Genki Dama combos referring to "5 hits").
