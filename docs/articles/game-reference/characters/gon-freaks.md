- Name: "Jail and Jajaken: Rock"
- Koma shape: 3 cells (1x3)

## Mechanic-revealing observations
- Support koma have an explicit windup delay mechanic: Jail (3 Koma) hides for 10 seconds before executing, during which other supports are sealed (implies a support-lock/cooldown state tied to an active deployed support).
- Upward Y (Lure Cast) is chargeable via holding the button, increasing range — confirms a charge-input mechanic distinct from the taunt-charge system.
- Taunt-based Nen Charge is a stacking buff with 2 discrete levels affecting X-move damage specifically (not B/Y moves).
- Forward B causes self-launch (Gon goes airborne after use) — noted as a mechanic to "be aware of," implying it affects Gon's own hurtbox/state, not just the opponent's.

## Combos noted (illustrate hit-linking/juggle rules, no extra numbers given)
- Launch combo: B, Up B, Aerial B
- Corner combo: B, Forward B, Aerial B (requires opponent pinned against a wall/stage edge)
- Jajaken combo: Y, Forward Y, 4 Koma X (author notes hits "don't link well together" — a pseudo-combo, useful vs. crowds and for guard chip damage)
