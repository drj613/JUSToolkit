## Controls reference (general, not Gotenks-specific)

A = Jump; A,A = Double Jump; Down+A = drop through platform / fast-fall in air; dash = double-tap direction; Side Attack = direction+B/Y; Down+B = Forced Change (forces opponent to switch character); Down+Y = Guard Break; Down+X = Push Attack; Select = Ultimate Action; L/R = character hotkeys.

## Acquisition (Dragon Ball world missions, credited to Genroh for objectives)

- Mission 2: KO all opponents; KO with Special Attack; don't get KO'd; KO 2x with Special Attack → unlocks Shishio; KO 2x by ring-out → unlocks **Gotenks**.

## Evolution branches

Note: Gotenks has two distinct 5-koma forms with different natures (Laughter and Power) — evolution branches rather than a single linear chain.

## Help koma

- Effect: Increases SP gauge when using multiple Special Attacks in a short time (exact rate not given).

## Support koma

**2-koma:**
- Super Saiyan 3 Gotenks appears and fires a mouth energy beam; described as "pretty much identical to his five-koma X special."
- Damage: 32 to Knowledge/Laughter, 48 to Power

**3-koma:**
- Super Saiyan 3 Gotenks jumps and fires eight blasts of energy diagonally downward. Usable in the air.
- Damage: 32 to Knowledge/Laughter, 48 to Power (same numbers as 2-koma support — author unconfirmed whether this is accurate or a copy error, since it's 8 blasts vs. a single beam)

Damage values given are for **Laughter-nature Gotenks**. Author notes: for Power-nature Gotenks, swap the damage — the higher number goes to Knowledge characters, and the lower number goes to Power and Laughter characters. Gotenks also receives an HP/stat boost from Goku, Vegeta, Vegetto, and Bobobo boost koma (no exact boost value given).

## Ultimate Action (Select)

Gotenks powers up (traditional DBZ power-up pose), slowly gaining SP while Select is held. No exact SP rate given.

## Specials by koma form

**4-koma Gotenks:**
- X — "Galactic Donuts": goes Super Saiyan, fires five rings forward; if all hit, opponent becomes temporarily immobile inside a golden ball. Ball keeps opponent off the ground, preventing combo-breaking via most ground-based supports.
  - Damage: 30 Knowledge/Laughter, 45 Power
- Up+X — "Super Hitsusawazafurukosu": 11-hit combo, starts up quickly, stronger than Galactic Donuts. Cannot be canceled once started — a miss leaves Gotenks fully exposed.
  - Damage: 35 Knowledge/Laughter, 52 Power

**5-koma Gotenks:**
- X — "Saiko No Kogeki" (Victory Cannon): Super Saiyan 3 transformation, mouth energy blast. Standard beam special; fast startup, good knockback.
  - Damage: 36 Knowledge/Laughter, 54 Power
- Up+X — "Super Ghost Kamikaze Attack": Super Saiyan 3 transformation, spits out a self-resembling ghost that seeks an opponent and explodes on contact.
  - Chargeable: holding X spits out up to 3 additional ghosts (max 4 total).
  - Ghost count by charge/release timing:
    - Release immediately → 1 ghost
    - Release after cheeks expand once → 2 ghosts
    - Release after cheeks expand 2–5 times → 3 ghosts
    - Full charge / hold until auto-release → 4 ghosts
  - Long startup time; author calls close-range use "insane" (i.e., impractical/risky).
  - Damage: 12–48 Knowledge/Laughter (scales with ghost count), 18–72 Power (scales with ghost count)

## General strategy notes (mechanic-revealing)

- Only the 4-koma form can combo into a special without requiring a counter-hit setup — implying the 5-koma special has stricter hit-confirm requirements or startup that prevents direct linking from normals.
- Cannot link the basic B, forward+B, Y, forward+Y string (author states this explicitly as a combo gap/whiff, unlike some other characters where this string reportedly links).
- Multiple selectable natures: 5-koma has both Power and Laughter nature variants, letting the player choose type matchup (useful vs. common Power-nature opponents since Laughter deals bonus damage to Power).
