# Hiei (Yu Yu Hakusho) — Jump Ultimate Stars Character Guide Notes

- Source: "Jump! Ultimate Stars" Hiei Character FAQ
- Author: Adam Miller (AKA Actawesome)
- Version: 1.0 (1/04/07)
- GameFAQs FAQ id: gf-46926
- **Unverified community source** — numbers and mechanics below are as reported by the fan author, not verified against the game or ROM.

## Moveset (general, koma tier not specified except where noted)

- Y (neutral): "Strong Attack" — barrage of punches, combos into another attack. No damage number given.
- Forward/Back + Y: quick lunge + flame sword attack — **14 damage**.
- Up + Y: quick glide upward, allows follow-up with regular B or Y. No damage number given.
- Down + Y: guard break — quick sword/uppercut motion; described as not a strong guard break but still deals some damage (no number given).
- B (neutral): weak sword attack, pushes enemy back. No damage number given.
- Forward/Back + B: lunge + sword, described as weak, usable in combos. No damage number given.
- Up + B: "one of Hiei's worst attacks," quick sword motion in air. No damage number given.
- Down + B: "ALMOST Guard Break" — quick flame punch. No damage number given.
- A: jump.
- Select (Ultimate Action): Hiei turns around, gains "very little SP" (no exact number). If hit while turned around, Hiei teleports behind the attacker (counter/reversal mechanic — no numeric data).

## Specials

### 4-Koma Hiei
- X: quick purple lightning-like sword lunge attack, described as good for finishing combos. No damage number given.
- Up X: described as a "crappier version" of the 5-Koma Up X special; situational (best used against opponents recovering from an edge). No damage number given.

### 5-Koma Hiei
- X: a series of quick slashes that launch the enemy into the air; also described as a good finishing combo. No damage number given.
- Up X: "Dragon of the Darkness Flame" — lasts for an extended duration, good for edge-guarding/chasing. Notable mechanic: if this attack hits Hiei himself, it powers him up, turning his attacks purple and "twice as strong" (author unconfirmed — no numeric multiplier given, described qualitatively as "TWICE AS STRONG").

No damage numbers are given anywhere in this guide for any of Hiei's attacks except the Forward/Back+Y lunge (14 damage).

## Help Koma effects

- Immunity to poison status effect.
- Immunity to blindness status effect.
- Grants ability to see invisible characters.
- While not actively in battle and standing still: slow passive SP regeneration.
- Also noted to boost (ally-boost) Kakashi Hatake (Naruto), Kurama, and Yusuke — presumably via help-koma placement (author does not specify the exact boost effect for these characters).

## Support Koma

- 2-Koma support: Hiei dashes forward, quickly steals a bar of SP from the opponent. Good against "spammers" (SP-heavy playstyles). No exact SP amount beyond "a bar" given.
- 3-Koma support: Hiei removes his bandage, revealing his Jagan (third) eye; quickly stuns everyone around him except the summoning player. Takes away "a little" SP from those hit (exact amount not given).

## Passive / mechanic observations (author's strategy notes, not hard numbers)

- Hiei has a fast dash that the author describes as useful for evasion/mix-ups ("run in circles around computers... nearly invincible to them" — this is a player-skill observation, not a stated invincibility frame mechanic).
- Author recommends ending combos with regular Y attacks and starting with regular B attacks.
- Up+B (edge-guard tool) is described as able to combo opponents into the Dragon of the Darkness Flame's damage-over-time/hangtime effect when they are near a stage edge — implies the darkness flame effect can persist/chase, but no numeric duration given.
- Deck-building note: Kakashi Hatake (Naruto), Yusuke Urameshi, and Kurama are called out as characters that "power up" Hiei (ally-boost mechanic), no numeric bonus given.
