## Koma tree / evolution

```
  -2-3
H-
  -4-5-6-7-8
```

Support koma occupy 2-3 spaces; battle koma are playable. All Ichigo battle koma share: air dashing, SP gain while fighting, and a UA.

- Help koma effect: increases SP gained on attack/block when adjacent to a Battle Koma.
- 2-Koma support shape: horizontal. Summons Ichigo to throw Kon (stuffed animal) — 0 damage. Prevents opponent from switching Battle Koma for a short time.
- 3-Koma support shape:
  ```
  [][]
  []
  ```
  Reduces damage taken by half for a short time (self-buff, no damage dealt).

## Passive / UA

- Koma 4–6 UA: a shout that increases potency of Y-Attacks.
- Koma 7–8 UA: rips off Hollow mask — gains energy but loses some health.

## Moveset — 4–6 Koma (pre-Bankai)

Damage format: (Power/Laughter damage, Knowledge damage)

B-Attacks:
- B (neutral): horizontal sword slash — 10 / 15 damage
- Forward/Back B: vertical slash — 10 / 15 damage
- Up B: wide sweep hitting around him — 9 / 13 damage
- Down B: energy wave around him, forces opponent to change Battle Koma on hit — 10 / 15 damage

Y-Attacks:
- Y (neutral): spinning sword over head, rapid-press for more hits — 6-14 / 9-21 damage (range)
- Forward/Back Y: slash + small Getsuga Tenshou projectile, travels short distance — 15 / 22 damage
- Up Y: slash + small Getsuga Tenshou at upper diagonal — 15 / 22 damage
- Down Y: small shockwave via ground thrust, breaks guard — 18 / 27 damage

### 4-Koma specials
- X: Getsuga Tenshou — 30 / 45 damage
- Up X: Getsuga Tenshou shot above head — 33 / 49 damage

### 5-Koma specials
- X: bigger, longer-range Getsuga Tenshou — 38 / 57 damage
- Up X: attack-power buff for a short time (locks Battle Koma switching until it wears off) — no direct damage stated

### 6-Koma specials
- X: Zangetsu assists for a giant Getsuga Tenshou — 45 / 67 damage
- Up X: longer-lasting attack boost, no lockout side effect — no direct damage stated

## Bankai moveset (post-6-Koma form change; "entire moveset changes, faster")

B-Attacks:
- B: horizontal slash — 9 / 13 damage
- Forward/Back B: step + slash — 9 / 13 damage
- Up B: stab above, slight launch — 9 / 13 damage
- Down B: shockwave, forced Battle Koma change — 9 / 13 damage

Y-Attacks:
- Y: slashes all around, multi-hit on repeated press, launches — 11-20 / 16-30 damage (range)
- Forward/Back Y: flashstep forward + single slash if enemy found — 18 / 27 damage
- Up Y: flashstep up, slashes enemy down to stage (no damage number given)
- Down Y: stab into stage, guard-break energy release — 18 / 27 damage

### 7-Koma specials (Bankai/Tensa Zangetsu tier)
- X: Tensa Zangetsu Getsuga Tenshou variant, travels farther — 50 / 75 damage. Random chance: inner Hollow takes over, gigantic version hits 75 / 112 damage but inflicts an "energy degeneration" status effect on the user. Each use refills some special energy.
- Up X: diagonal flashstep + aerial sword combo hitting a wide area, damage depends on hit count — 51 / 74 damage.

### 8-Koma specials (Hollow mask form)
- X: Hollow-mask Getsuga Tenshou — 55 / 82 damage
- Up X: Hollow-mask version of 7-Koma's Up X; can also hit a grounded foe directly in front (7-Koma version is air-only) — 65 / 97 damage
- Both 8-Koma specials noted to have long start-up time (author recommends 7-Koma over 8-Koma for Bankai play).

## Combo notes (Bankai)

- B, Up-B, Up-X(7-Koma) — two slashes into aerial special; links easily; Forward-B can substitute for Up-B.
- Down-B, Up-X(7-Koma) — guard break launches opponent into aerial special; same total damage as above but slower due to guard break startup.
- Y, Up-X(7-Koma) — slash frenzy into aerial special, slightly more damage than the above; author notes only a partial Y string should be used, not the full string.
- B, Forward-B, Y, Up-Y, Aerial Y — full launcher combo, ends with opponent slammed to the ground.
- B, Forward-B, Y (single tap), Forward-Y — forward-and-back slash combo.

These combo notes imply forced-hitstun chaining between B/Y strings and specials in Bankai form (author unconfirmed on exact hitstun/cancel windows — no frame data given).
