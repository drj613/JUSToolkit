## Koma details

### Boost koma
- Piyohiko, Hammer, Hamii: each gives +8 J-Soul (HP) boost. Shapes given as 1, 2, or 3 block (not specified per-boost).

### Help koma
- 1 square `[]`: Immunity to battle and support seal effects.

### Support koma
- 2 square (`[][]` stacked vertically as shown, "Hahahaha!"): Sends out a flying man who stays near ground; if opponent is hit, their attacks are sealed.
- 3 square ("Jaguar counter", L-shape `[][]` over `[]`): Summons sleeping Jaguar; if hit by enemy, he gets up and does a large recorder slash for 54 damage.

### Battle koma

**4 koma** — shape 2x2 block (`[][]` / `[][]`)
- J-Soul: 144
- X special "Jaguar Fart": lags, then hits enemies directly in front and back 1 time. 54 damage to power, 36 to knowledge/laughter.
- Up X special "Jaguar Healing Dance": heals ~1/4 of Jaguar's HP after a long delay (author unconfirmed exact fraction).

**5 koma** — shape `[][]` top, then 3 single blocks stacked below
- J-Soul: 160
- X special "Jaguar Dash Attack": dashes forward; on hit grabs opponent, headbutts, then two white creatures appear on both sides and shout, launching opponent sky high. 2 hits. 60 damage to power, 40 to knowledge/laughter.
- Up X special "Jaguar Dance": long dance; ends with either 3 bombs or 3 J-Coins appearing (author calls it "worthless").

**6 koma** — shape `[][]` / `[]` / `[][][]`
- J-Soul: 176
- X special "Summon Drummer": summons a kid with drums moving forward slowly, hitting with drumsticks. 12 hits. 69 damage to power, 46 to knowledge/laughter.
- Up X special "Summon Drooler": summons a kid who drools onto platforms below Jaguar, causing speed-down debuff on enemy. 4 hits. 120 damage to power, 80 to knowledge/laughter.

## Mechanic-revealing observations / combo notes

- Combo: Up Y + Jump Y — knocks opponent airborne, then can be followed by 2 (or 3 with a triple-jump help koma) Ramen Swirl hits, totaling ~100 damage. Implies triple-jump help koma affects available airborne combo extensions.
- Guide recommends pairing Jaguar with support koma for burst damage since his own specials are considered weak:
  - Trunks 3-koma support: usable "at any time," ~30-something damage.
  - Seiya 3-koma support: same as Trunks 3-koma.
  - Satsuki 2-koma (Ichigo 100%): if 4-koma Jaguar's Up X (healing dance) is activated immediately after Satsuki's support, recovers 450 HP (author unconfirmed — stacking/interaction specifics not detailed).
  - Sena 3-koma support: immobilizes opponent, leaving them open to attacks from behind.
