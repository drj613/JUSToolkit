# Kagura (Gintama)

- Source guide: "Kagura Character FAQ"
- Author: Jason Dorfman (GameFAQs handle: FFandMMfan), Version 2.0
- GameFAQs FAQ id: gf-46302
- Unverified community source — numbers are player-collected, not from game data files.

## Acquiring / evolving Kagura

- Acquired as a Help koma in the Gintama world, Mission 4, Goal #5 (requirement: complete
  the stage's "collect the Jump Magazine" objective without ever touching/switching your
  deck's battle koma or using a support koma).

Evolution chart (as diagrammed in source):
- H (Help) -> BL4 (Battle, Laughter, 4-koma) -> BL5 (Battle, Laughter, 5-koma) -> BL6
  (Battle, Laughter, 6-koma)
- H also branches to S2 (Support, 2-koma) -> S3 (Support, 3-koma)
- BL5 also branches to BP6 (Battle, Power nature, 6-koma) — but this branch is locked until
  unlocked via Sadaharu's own evolution chart (see below)

Unlocking the BP6 slot requires Sadaharu (Kagura's pet dog, a separate Help koma). To get
Sadaharu: reach Planet U, Mission 3 Special mission, and score 8 points via KOs (normal
clear only requires most points via KO). Sadaharu's Help koma's first evolution slot then
unlocks Kagura's Power-natured 6-koma (this only unlocks the slot — the BP6 koma itself
must still be separately obtained via Kagura's own chart). Sadaharu's next evolution slot
after that unlocks a Gintoki koma (not covered further in this guide).

## Help / Support koma

**Help koma**: Increases Special (SP) Gauge when breaking item boxes.

**Support Koma 2** (shape: 2 panels horizontal, per diagram `[][]`): Kagura appears riding
Gintoki's scooter and drives forward, dealing damage.
- Damage: 24 to Knowledge/Laughter characters, 36 to Power characters.
- Notes: Kagura falls off the scooter partway through; while she's on it no separate damage
  is dealt from her directly, but the riderless scooter keeps sliding and still damages
  anyone it hits. If the scooter hits a ledge while she's still riding, she falls off at
  the edge.

**Support Koma 3** (shape: 3 panels horizontal, `[][][]`) : A table appears with Kagura
hiding under it, head peeking out; if an opponent touches her head she grabs and pulls them
under the table, dealing 8 hits of damage before releasing them.
- Damage: 33 to Knowledge/Laughter, 49 to Power.
- Notes: If she doesn't catch anyone immediately, she waits in place; after a few seconds
  she vanishes, and the longer she waits without catching someone, the fewer hits (and thus
  less total damage) the attack deals when it does connect.

## Basic moveset (Battle koma, normal attacks)

Numbers as given are for the Laughter-natured koma (strong vs Power types, normal vs
Knowledge/Laughter). Per the author, for the Power-natured koma, swap so the higher damage
applies to Knowledge characters instead of Power characters (P/K/L damage profile flips
between the two natures; normal-attack damage values are otherwise identical across all of
Kagura's battle koma sizes per the 4-koma writeup).

- **B, B+left/right, B+Up** (basic hits): 15 damage to Power, 10 to Knowledge/Laughter.
- **B in the air**: downward swing that thrusts the enemy to the ground. 13 damage to
  Power, 9 to Knowledge/Laughter.
- **B+Down**: creates a text bubble that forces a character switch on the enemy. 15 damage
  to Power, 10 to Knowledge/Laughter (same as basic B — the switch is an added status
  effect, not extra damage).
- **Y**: grabs the enemy and swings them in circles; damages all foes within swing range.
  Damage to the grabbed enemy: 27 to Power, 18 to Knowledge/Laughter.
- **Y+left/right**: dashes forward, swings umbrella, sends enemy in the opposite direction.
  30 to Power, 20 to Knowledge/Laughter.
- **Y+Up**: uses umbrella as a spring, dives at an upward angle to kick. 30 to Power, 20 to
  Knowledge/Laughter.
- **Y+Down**: creates a text bubble that breaks the enemy's guard. 30 to Power, 20 to
  Knowledge/Laughter. (Guard-break flag confirmed on this move.)
- **Y in the air**: diving kick. 30 damage to Power, 20 to Knowledge/Laughter.

### Combos (basic moves)

- **B -> B+Up -> B in the air**: 43 damage total to Power, 29 to Knowledge/Laughter.
- **Y in the air -> Y in the air**: on hitting an enemy with the flying kick, Kagura
  bounces back into the air, allowing a second aerial Y (hits same or a different target).
  30 damage to Power / 20 to Knowledge/Laughter per hit (so up to 60/40 total for 2 hits).
- **B+Up -> Y in the air near a ledge**: near-guaranteed ring-out setup; author warns you
  cannot stop the aerial Y once started, so a miss can self-destruct you off the stage.

### Ultimate Action

Kagura eats a piece of "celery or something green," which slowly recovers her life. No
numeric HP-restore value given.

## Battle koma stats

Kagura has 3 passive effects always active (author notes: don't give her Help/Support
komas with the same effect, since they'd be redundant/do nothing):
1. Restores her Special (SP) bar when attacking.
2. Restores her Special bar when she breaks treasure chests.
3. Slowly restores her Special bar passively at all times.

### Battle Koma 4 (Laughter)
- J-Soul: 152
- Nature: Laughter
- Koma shape: `[][]` / `[]` / `[]` (2-wide top row, then two single-panel rows below —
  vaguely L/T-shaped, 4 panels total)
- **Special Attack A**: fires energy blasts from her umbrella; tapping X repeatedly fires
  more shots, up to 16 shots total. Max damage: 48 to Power, 32 to Knowledge/Laughter.
- **Special Attack B**: eats a bowl of food, restoring some health; can be interrupted by
  enemy attack. No numeric heal amount given.
- Author's rating: normal-attack damage is identical across all her koma sizes, so the
  4-koma is considered strong for its size mainly due to the self-heal special.

### Battle Koma 5 (Laughter)
- J-Soul: 168
- Nature: Laughter
- Koma shape: `[][][]` / `[][]` (3-wide top row, 2-wide bottom row, 5 panels total)
- **Special Attack A**: hops on Sadaharu (giant dog) and rides around, damaging anyone she
  runs into; direction controllable via D-Pad. Damage: 45 to Power, 30 to
  Knowledge/Laughter.
- **Special Attack B**: eats from a rice cooker, restoring HP (more than the 4-koma's heal,
  per author); can be interrupted by enemy attack. No exact heal number given.
- Author notes a combo with Gotenks 3-koma support dealing up to 93 damage to Power
  characters if every hit connects (see Support Combos below for the base numbers this
  refers to). Author calls this her best koma overall, citing the stronger heal and
  Sadaharu's crowd-control/edge-guard utility.

### Battle Koma 6 (Laughter)
- J-Soul: 184
- Nature: Laughter
- Koma shape: `[][][]` / `[][][]` (3x2 rectangle, 6 panels)
- **Special Attack A**: Sadaharu charges forward, bites the enemy on contact, and throws
  them forward — noted as a strong ring-out tool. Damage: 67 to Power, 45 to
  Knowledge/Laughter.
- **Special Attack B**: same table-hide grab as her Support Koma 3, but as a battle special;
  grabs, deals damage, and forces a character switch on release. Damage: 60 to Power, 40 to
  Knowledge/Laughter.
- Author notes: loses her healing specials at this koma size, but both specials here lock
  the opponent out of fighting back while taking the hit, making them good to combo with
  Support koma (see Support Combos). Both specials only hit one target at a time. Rated best
  for 1-on-1 matches (vs. the 5-koma's crowd utility).

### Battle Koma 6 (Power)
- J-Soul: 184
- Nature: Power
- Koma shape: `[][]` / `[][][]` / ` []` (irregular shape, 6 panels total — top row 2 panels,
  middle row 3 panels, bottom row 1 panel offset)
- Specials: identical to the Laughter 6-koma's specials — all of Kagura's specials are
  Laughter-element regardless of her own koma nature, so they deal the same damage to
  same-natured targets as the Laughter 6-koma does. Only her normal (B/Y) attacks flip to
  Power-type damage profile (higher damage vs. Knowledge instead of vs. Power).
- Author's take: useful to catch opponents who assume "Laughter Kagura" and swap to
  Knowledge counters; noted downside is the irregular koma shape makes deck placement
  harder. Author considers the two 6-komas roughly equivalent otherwise.

## Fighting notes / mechanic observations

- All of Kagura's Y attacks have startup and recovery lag ("small lag time before and
  after"), and none of her Y attacks combo into each other except **Y+Up -> Aerial Y**
  (noted as a near-universal option for most characters, not Kagura-specific).
- This lag window is called out as the primary punish opportunity when fighting her: block
  a Y attack (except Y+Up), then release guard and counterattack immediately.
- Movement-seal effects are noted as relatively ineffective against her: Y+Up lets her gain
  vertical distance and often counterattack from the air; B+forward and Y+forward let her
  move along flat ground; on her 5-koma, riding Sadaharu lets her move left/right (though
  she can't leave the current platform while riding).
- Interrupting her healing specials (4-koma food / 5-koma rice cooker) is explicitly called
  out as possible via a fast enough attack — author states "a majority of the life gain is
  on the very last burst of healing," implying the heal accelerates or front-loads its
  effect near the end of its duration (author unconfirmed exact heal curve).

## Ally boosts

Per the standard "3 boosting characters" system described (2 from her own series, 1 from
another):
- **Gintoki** (Gintama) — recommended as a battle character ally (his 5-koma); his 3-koma
  support is also usable.
- **Shinpachi** (Gintama) — Help koma gives 1 SP bar when Kagura is KO'd; his 2-koma grants
  a "Toughen" effect; his 3-koma inflicts Confusion on enemies (this last effect is on the
  enemy, not a boost to Kagura, but listed under his koma options).
- **Vegeta** (Dragon Ball) — cross-series booster. His Help increases SP when attacking or
  blocking while at low health. Author recommends his battle komas or 3-koma support
  instead if using Kagura's healing-focused 4/5-koma, since the Help's low-HP trigger
  conflicts with keeping her topped off via self-heal.

No numeric J-Soul boost amount is given for Kagura specifically (unlike the Taikoubou guide's
flat +8 HP figure) — author only describes what each booster koma does when adjacent.

## Support combos (numeric)

All Battle Koma (general):
- **Y -> 3-koma Gotenks (support)** [Laughter Kagura]: 75 damage to Power, 50 to
  Knowledge/Laughter. Noted as an excellent ring-out technique.
- **Y -> 3-koma Gotenks (support)** [Power Kagura]: 66 damage to Power, 59 to Knowledge, 50
  to Laughter.
- **Y+Up -> 3-koma Gotenks -> Aerial Y**: damage variable/unpredictable (hard to land every
  hit), but author states it can exceed 100 damage to weaker-natured targets and 60+ to
  neutral-natured targets.

6-Koma (Laughter) combos:
- **Y -> 2-koma Piccolo (support) -> X Special**: 110 to Power, 87 to Knowledge, 79 to
  Laughter.
- **Y -> 3-koma Arale (support) -> X special**: 139 to Power, 93 to Knowledge and Laughter.
  Noted as an excellent ring-out technique.
- **Y -> 2-koma Shishio (support) -> X Special**: 122 to Power, 105 to Knowledge, 91 to
  Laughter.

6-Koma (Power) combos:
- **Y -> 2-koma Piccolo (support) -> X Special**: 101 to Power, 96 to Knowledge, 75 to
  Laughter.
- **Y -> 3-koma Arale (support) -> X special**: 130 to Power, 102 to Knowledge, 93 to
  Laughter. Noted as an excellent ring-out technique.
- **Y -> 2-koma Shishio (support) -> X Special**: 113 to Power, 114 to Knowledge, 91 to
  Laughter.

(Note: X Special here refers to her 6-koma Special Attack A, i.e. Sadaharu's charge/bite,
listed above as 67/45 base damage — the combo totals above include the support koma's own
damage contribution plus this special.)

## Other support koma referenced (non-boosting, used in combos/strategies)

- Lenalee 2-koma (D.Gray-Man): timing tech — jump right before using her; she flies under
  and carries the enemy off the edge.
- Sanji 2-koma (One Piece): recommended healing substitute when using the 6-koma (which
  lacks Kagura's own heal).
- Satsuki (Ichigo 100%): heals slowly over time.
- Gotenks 3-koma (Dragon Ball): Laughter-type support, synergizes with any Laughter Kagura
  koma (see damage numbers above).
- Piccolo 2-koma (Dragon Ball): used in 6-koma combos (see above).
- Arale 3-koma (Dr. Slump): beam attack, best with Laughter 6-koma (see above).
- Shishio 2-koma (Rurouni Kenshin): grab + attack + burn; used in 6-koma combos (see above).

## Character use notes (non-numeric mechanic observations)

- Author explicitly notes normal (B/Y) attack damage values are identical across all koma
  sizes for Kagura — only the special attacks and passive utility (healing, Sadaharu, grab)
  differ by koma size.
- 6-koma specials are noted to disable the opponent's ability to fight back while being hit
  (a hitstun/lock state), which is why they combo cleanly into Support koma follow-ups.
