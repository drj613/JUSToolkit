# Kakashi Hatake (Naruto) — Character Guide Notes

- Source: "Jump! Ultimate Stars Hatake Kakashi FAQ", by MasterTurtle (aka Hyperslasher), version 2.0
- GameFAQs FAQ id: gf-46618
- Unverified community source — numbers are from a fan FAQ, not verified against game code.

## Type / affinity note

Kakashi is Knowledge type (in his standard forms); attacks deal 1.5x damage to Laughter-type opponents. In his 6-Koma "B" (Laughter type) form, his own attacks deal 1.5x damage to Power-type opponents instead. Note: even while in the Laughter-type 6b Koma, his Chidori and Mangekyou Sharingan specials are stated to still deal Knowledge-type damage.

## Obtaining Kakashi

- Unlock: Naruto world, Mission 2, Objective 5 — KO at least 2 opponents using special attacks (no premade deck required).

## Evolution chart

Tree: H -> S2 -> S3 (Help/Support branch), and H -> B4 -> B5 -> B6a, with B6a -> B6b branch (per the ASCII diagram, B6b branches off near B5/B6a).

## Basic attacks (shared across all Battle Koma forms)

### B attacks
- B: quick slap, hits both in front and slightly behind. Damage: 8.
- B + side (Forward): dash forward, kunai upslash then upward kick. Knocks enemy airborne (comboable into aerials, not grounded attacks). Two hits: 1st hit 2 damage, 2nd hit 6 damage.
- B + up: summons a clone that kicks upward. Less reach than side B; knocks target up less high (leaves them open to low-hitting supports). Damage: 8.
- B + down: charges briefly, fires Pakkun (a small dog). Damage: 8. Forces enemy to switch battle characters (has more range than most forced-switch attacks).
- Air B: downward slash in the air; can knock enemies down through thin floors. Damage: 8.

### Y attacks
- Y ("Kakashi Triple Kick"): summons 2 clones, 3-way kick. Damage: 24 at close range, less at distance (falloff, exact numbers not given).
- Y + side (Forward): throws up to 6 shuriken in a line, 3 damage each, total 18 if all hit. Notable startup/recovery lag; range less than expected.
- Y + up: jump + punch/slash/kick (3 hits). Will NOT hit an opponent unless they are above Kakashi. First two hits: 5 damage each; third hit: 8 damage. (Total 18.)
- Y + down: charges ~0.5 second, then summons a large dog. Breaks guard. Damage: 16.
- Air Y: throws a kunai downward-angled. Damage: 12. Usable as a follow-up after Up Y if not comboing into a support.

### Select (Ultimate Action, shared across forms)
- Creates a tree stump decoy. If Kakashi is hit within the next 4 seconds, the stump replaces him (attack deals no damage), and he can move a short distance after disappearing.

## Koma forms

- Help Koma (shape: single square): reduces damage taken from special attacks.
- Support Koma, 2-block (shape: 2 horizontal squares): Kakashi appears reading a magazine; if attacked, retaliates with a kick for 28 Laughter-type damage. Described as unreliable/outclassed by other counter supports.
- Support Koma, 3-block (shape: 3 vertical squares): Kakashi dashes, grabs enemy, summons a clone, performs "Thousand Years of Pain." Knocks enemy airborne, deals 28 Laughter damage, and drains one of the opponent's SP bars.

### Battle Koma 4-block (shape: 2x2 squares)
- J-Soul: 136.
- Special A: beam attack (DBZ-style). Deals up to 30 damage at point-blank range.
- Special B: summons ninja dogs that converge on nearby enemy. Deals 24 damage if all dogs hit; described as slow, weak, limited range.
- Author's overall assessment: this form has the best specials of all Kakashi Koma.

### Battle Koma 5-block (shape: 2 top + 3 bottom squares)
- J-Soul: 152.
- Special A: Raikiri — charges chakra, dashes forward, hits enemy. Damage is fixed at 36 regardless of charge time; charge time only changes dash distance. No shock status inflicted (unlike Sasuke's Chidori).
- Special B: ninja dogs again; deals 30 damage if all hit.
- Author's overall assessment: least useful of his forms.

### Battle Koma 6-block A (shape: 2x3 squares, Knowledge type, Mangekyou Sharingan art)
- J-Soul: 168.
- Special A: Chidori. Deals 44 damage total.
- Special B: Mangekyou Sharingan — creates a hovering red eye that damages all enemies on contact. Deals 48 damage total. Goes through walls; path is controllable, useful for edgeguarding.

### Battle Koma 6-block B (shape: 2x3 squares stacked as 3 rows of 2, Laughter type alt form)
- J-Soul: 168.
- Special A: Chidori (still deals Knowledge-type damage despite Laughter-type Koma).
- Special B: Mangekyou Sharingan (still Knowledge-type damage). Same numbers implied as 6a (44 / 48) though not explicitly restated.
- Author recommends either 4-Koma or 6-Koma (Laughter variant, 6b) as the best picks; 6b's value comes from applying Laughter-type damage via base form while specials remain Knowledge-type, giving two damage types from one character.

## Ally Boost / Deck support

- Kakashi receives Ally Boost from: Naruto, Sasuke, Sven (each presumably +8 HP style boost per convention noted in Sakura's guide, but exact HP value not stated in this source).
- Kakashi's innate abilities (do not stack Help Koma of the same effect): Wall-Jump, see invisible opponents, reduced damage from Special Attacks. Note: all three of his boosting characters (Naruto, Sasuke, Sven) grant abilities he already has natively, so their Help-Koma value is only useful when NOT pointed at Kakashi.

## Mechanic-revealing notes / combos

- Side B (Forward B) knocks enemies airborne, enabling follow-up into aerial attacks but not grounded attacks — indicates a distinct "juggle state" mechanic gated by knockback type.
- Push block mechanic referenced: pushing an opponent back on block can create spacing to safely use Y or set up an air-dash-behind mixup.
- Kakashi described as having the longest dash in the game (exact frame/distance data not given, author-unconfirmed qualitative claim).

### Basic combos (no exact damage totals given, only described)
- B, side B, up Y, (pause), aerial Y — described as the standard/best non-support combo. Aerial B substitutable near a ledge.
- B, up B, up Y, aerial Y — alt version; up B has less reach, no fast-fall+support insertion possible, but no pause needed mid-combo.
- B, up B, Y, up Y, aerial Y — the extra Y hit adds ~4 damage and keeps the enemy in the combo (may miss depending on opponent hurtbox size).

### Support combos
- Side B, up Y, Gotenks 3 (used in-air), fastfall, dash, repeat — a loopable combo.
- Side B, up Y, fastfall, Aya 2, repeat — "the infamous Kakashi infinite." Deals significant cumulative damage, pushes enemy toward the edge, and drains enemy SP each loop (via Aya's effect). Aya's support-seal effect prevents the opponent from breaking the loop with their own support, unless they have support-seal immunity.
- Rouji 2 works similarly to Aya but inflicts movement seal instead of support seal (unblockable by any immunity, per author).

### Team combo
- Kakashi (any Koma) + Kenshin 5-Koma: B, side B, up Y, switch to Kenshin, aerial Y, X — described as dealing "a pretty decent amount of damage for 1 SP" (no exact number given). Timing note: must wait for Kenshin to fully appear before pressing Y or Kakashi will perform his own aerial Y instead.
