# Kazuki Mutou (Busou Renkin) — Character Guide Notes

- Unverified community source — numbers are from a fan FAQ, not verified against game code.

## General mechanics notes (from this guide's Controls/Terminology sections)

- Koma affinity triangle: Power → Knowledge → Laughter → Power. Attacking a weakness deals 1.5x damage. All of Kazuki's Koma are Power-affinity.
- Koma deck: 20-Koma block, 4 (vertical) x 5 (horizontal) grid. Need at least one Koma of each size category to build a valid deck.
- Koma size categories: 1-Koma = Help Koma; 2-3 Koma = Support Koma (costs 1 SP bar when summoned, can be destroyed before its effect activates, wasting the SP); 4-8 Koma = Battle Koma.
- 1-Koma (Help) with no arrow: effect negated, or increases SP bar by 1 (author's description, somewhat ambiguous).
- Each Special (X) attack costs 1 SP bar.
- B cancels into Y or Forward+B; Y usually cancels into Forward+Y (but author notes this cancel isn't useful for Kazuki specifically).

## Obtaining Kazuki

- 1 Block: KO all opponents in Busou Renkin Mission 1.

## Koma forms

- 1 Block (Help Koma): passive slow HP regen.
- 2 Block (Support Koma): summoned above player, flies upward. Damage: 26 to Power, 39 to Knowledge, 26 to Laughter.
- 3 Block (Support Koma): summoned in front of player, charges (wraps sash around Sunlight Heart lance), then rushes forward. Damage: 28 to Power, 42 to Knowledge, 28 to Laughter.
- 4, 5, 6 Block: Battle Koma (movesets below).

Note: the 2-Block and 3-Block damage figures (Power/Knowledge/Laughter) look like they already include the 1.5x weakness multiplier for Knowledge listed separately, i.e., base damage is the Power/Laughter number and the Knowledge number is roughly 1.5x it.

## Mechanic-revealing notes / combos

- Directional/Aerial Y is a two-part attack that can be aimed in any direction except straight down while grounded.
- Tap attack (character tapped while already active, via L/R assignment) = half version of Forward+Y; near-zero startup; if it lands, deals 16 damage (equivalent to both hits of Forward+Y landing).
- Up+B combos out of B, launching opponent slightly airborne (author notes it "cancels B fairly quickly"); opponent cannot block mid-air, allowing a follow-up Support.
- Guard Break is called out explicitly on Down+Y (all Koma levels).
- Auto-HP regen listed as a passive trait for Kazuki's Battle Koma (also reflected in 1-Block Help Koma effect).

### Named combos (author-reported, base 4-Koma unless noted)

- B, Up+B, Y: 31 dmg to Power, 46 to Knowledge, 31 to Laughter.
- B, Forward+B, Y: 31 / 46 / 31.
- B, Up+B, Forward Y: 31 / 46 / 31 (last hit blockable but very tight window).
- B, Up+B or Forward+B, Y, Up+Y: 31 / 46 / 31 (escape combo).
- B, Up/Forward+B, Y, Forward Y: 47 / 70 / 47 (low chance to land final hit).
- Forward+B, Forward+Y: 24 / 36 / 24.

### Koma-specific combos into X (damage to Power / Knowledge / Laughter)

- 4-Koma: B, Forward+B, Y, Up+X (causes Blind): 51 / 76 / 51.
- 4-Koma: Down+Y, Up+X (Guard Break + Blind): 36 / 54 / 36.
- 4-Koma: B, Up+B, X: 43 / 66 / 43.
- 5-Koma: B, Up+B, X: 47 / 70 / 47.
- 6-Koma: B, Up+B, Up+X: 61 / 91 / 61.

## Koma Deck Support (Ally Boosts affecting Kazuki)

- Tokiko Tsumura (Busou Renkin): increases Guard strength.
- Papillon / Koushaku Chouno (Busou Renkin): nullifies Poison.
- Jonothan Joestar (JoJo's Bizarre Adventure): increases Max Health after returning from KO.

## Author's Koma-choice summary (strategy opinion, not extracted in detail per instructions, kept minimal for numeric context)

- 4-Koma: fast X (28 dmg), inflicts Blind via Up+X (20 dmg).
- 5-Koma: X charges to 32/40/52 dmg; Up+X (Papillon butterflies) 36 dmg with blast radius, can hit multiple targets.
- 6-Koma: X scales 40/48/56 dmg by deck size; Up+X (Tokiko) up to 46 dmg total across 6 hits, recommended for decks of 10+ Koma.
