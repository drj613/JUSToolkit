## General Mechanic Notes (from this guide)

- Nature type-advantage rule stated: Power beats Knowledge, Knowledge beats Laughter, Laughter beats Power, at a multiplier of 1.5x damage when attacker's nature beats defender's nature.
- Kurama is Knowledge nature.
- Special Attacks (X button) require the SP/energy gauge to fill completely at least once; the number next to the gauge indicates how many charges are banked/available before needing to refill.

## Help / Support Koma

- Help Koma (1 Block): regenerates the Special Attack gauge if you don't switch characters (Ally Boost self-effect).
- Support Koma (2 Block): sprays mist to the direct left and right of where Kurama stands, temporarily blinding enemies caught in it. No damage number given.
- Support Koma (3 Block): restores some health to your Battle Characters. No exact heal amount given.

## Strategy/Mechanic Observations

- Jump Canceling: pressing Down+A while airborne makes the character (Kurama or nearly any character) drop quickly back to the platform, skipping the "hover" period at the apex of a jump — reveals a general engine mechanic (jump has a brief hang-time/hover window that can be cancelled by a fast-fall input).
- Author notes Kurama's attacks mostly deal only 4-14 damage per hit, calling him "underpowered" relative to high-HP characters (cites Vegito and Bobobo as HP outliers, no numbers given for them here).
- Kurama's X attacks are noted as effective for ring-outs (implies knockback/positioning utility, no numeric knockback given).
- Kurama's Ally Boost: regenerates the Special Attack Gauge for allies as long as you don't switch characters or use a Support Koma; characters who receive this boost from him: Hiei, Robin.
- Characters providing Ally Boosts to Kurama: Hiei (adds another SP gauge bar; also nullifies Poison status), Kuwabara (regenerates SP gauge), Shun (adds another SP gauge bar; also regenerates SP gauge on a last-moment/just-frame block).

## Deck Grid Info

- Koma sizes: 1 block = Help Koma; 2-3 blocks = Support Koma; 4-8 blocks = Battle Koma. Deck grid is 4x5. A valid deck needs at least one Help, one Support, and one Battle Koma.
