## Koma forms

### 1. Help Koma
- Shape: single square `[]`
- Effect: enables one Battle Character to perform a wall jump (one of only 3 chars with this effect, alongside Naruto and Fusuke/Ninku)

### 2. Support Koma A (2-koma)
- Shape: `[][]` (2 squares, horizontal)
- Name: Onkyou no Tougi: Otokase ("Sound Stepping Skill: Sound Shackles")
- Nature: Knowledge
- Damage: N/A (grab move)
- Effect: appears behind the player's character, rushes forward at an upward angle, grabs first Battle character (friend or foe) touched and carries them. Usable in the air.

### 3. Support Koma B (3-koma)
- Shape: `[][][]` (3 squares, horizontal)
- Name: Enbu: Sarikaze ("Waltz: Misty Wind")
- Nature: Knowledge
- Damage: 32/48 (5 hits)
- Effect: kicks a small cyclone forward, damages and knocks back; long untechable time (author notes this is good for ring-outs)

### 4. Battle Koma A (4-koma)
- Shape: 2x2 square block
- Base HP: 128
- Nature: Knowledge
- Boosts: all D.Gray-man cast — Kanda, Allen, Komui
- Passive ability: Dark Boots — grants triple jump and air dash
- Specials:
  - Neutral-X: Enbu: Kirikaze ("Waltz: Misty Wind"). Nature: Knowledge. Damage: 36/54. Speed: Slow. Range: Moderate. Effect: horizontal wind tunnel hitting up to 5 times, knocks back; does not combo without support; hits opponents hanging from ledges.
  - Up-X: Enbu: Kuroshizuku ("Waltz: Black Droplets"). Nature: Knowledge. Damage: 2/3 (initial flip), 28/42 (ground hit), 20/30 (air hit) — assuming same elevation as opponent. Speed: Moderate. Range: Moderate (vertical range: very long). Effect: knocks back; damage/knockback increase the further Lenalee falls; airborne for the whole move; does not stop falling until hitting the ground floor — if the "ground floor" is actually a pass-through platform rather than a solid floor, Lenalee falls through and dies. Only special comboable without support (from Neutral-B).

### 5. Battle Koma B (5-koma)
- Shape: plus/T-ish 5-square shape (`  [][]` over `[][][]`)
- Base HP: 144
- Nature: Knowledge
- Boosts: all D.Gray-man cast — Kanda, Allen, Komui
- Passive ability: Dark Boots — triple jump and air dash
- Specials:
  - Neutral-X: Onkyou no Tougi: Otokase ("Sound Stepping Skill: Sound Shackles"). Nature: Knowledge. Damage: 40/60 (3 hits), 42/69 (4 hits). Speed: Slow. Range: Long. Effect: launches on first hit, knocks back on last hit; airborne for the whole move; cannot combo into it.
  - Up-X: Shittsui no Tougi: Tetsukase ("Falling Stepping Skill: Iron Shackles"). Nature: Knowledge. Damage: 2/3 (rising hit), 30/45 (falling hit) — assuming same elevation. Speed: Very Slow. Range: Close (vertical range: very long). Effect: launches on rising hit but rising does not combo into falling hit; damage/knockback increase with fall distance; airborne throughout; falls through pass-through-platform "ground floors" and dies (same hazard as 4-Koma Up-X); does not guard break despite appearance; smaller hitbox than expected, can whiff entirely.

Author's overall koma comparison: 4-Koma has lower HP (128) but faster/more useful specials; 5-Koma has more HP (144) but slower specials, one "next to useless."

## Support-combo notes (mechanic-revealing)

- Trunks (Dragon Ball) 3-Koma combo: Neutral-B > Up-B > Up-Y > call Trunks + Air-B simultaneously = 12 hits, 69/103 damage total (author-tested number).
- Gyro Zeppelli (JoJo) 3-Koma: Neutral-B > Up-B > call Gyro > Forward-Y, inflicts Guard Seal status; damage depends on summon timing.
- Ichigo (Bleach) 2-Koma: Neutral-B > call Ichigo > Up-B > Forward-Y inflicts Battle Seal status.
- Fuji (Prince of Tennis) 2-Koma: call Fuji + Neutral-B simultaneously > Up-B > Up-Y > Air-Y, inflicts Guard Seal (smaller koma than Gyro, harder to hit, less damage — author unconfirmed on exact numbers).
- Yuu Kanda (D.Gray-man) 2-Koma support is a guard break usable in the air.
- Allen Walker (D.Gray-man) 3-Koma support cannot be used in the air but launches on hit.
