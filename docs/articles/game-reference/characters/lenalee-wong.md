## General mechanic notes from this guide (not Lenalee-specific, but stated as background)

- Nature system: Knowledge > Laughter > Power > Knowledge (rock-paper-scissors). Advantaged attacker gets a **1.5x** damage multiplier (author's worked example: 10 base damage → 15 damage against a disadvantaged-nature target).
- Move-table format used throughout: separate "Normal" damage vs. "Laughter" damage — for Lenalee (a Knowledge-type character), "Laughter" damage = the 1.5x bonus number since Laughter is weak to Knowledge. "Normal" applies to Power and Knowledge-type targets.
- Distance categories used: Near (point-blank/a couple hits away), Mid (a bit further), Far (near edge of screen).

## Help Koma

- Effect: grants the equipped character the ability to Wall Jump.
- Note: Lenalee herself does not need this (she has an innate Triple Jump); this Help Koma is for giving Wall Jump to *other* characters.

## Support Koma

### 2-Blocks Support Koma — "Onkyou no Tougi - Otokase"
- No damage numbers given (utility move, not an attack).
- Effect: Lenalee is summoned and picks up either you or your opponent (target depends on positioning) and carries them to an extremely high area, in the direction Lenalee is facing.
- Can be used while airborne (mid-jump).
- Support Koma general rule (stated in guide): if a Support Koma is attacked upon being summoned, it is immediately canceled/interrupted, wasting the SP spent.

### 3-Blocks Support Koma — "Enbu Kirikaze" (support version)
- No damage numbers given.
- Effect: Lenalee performs a whirlwind attack with her Dark Boots that knocks nearby opponents flying — strong ring-out tool near the arena edge.
- Cannot be summoned while airborne (ground-only), unlike the 2-Block version.

- Reversed Crescent Kick (Up+Y): can combo into a B attack afterward; pushes opponent downward; useful at edge for ring-outs.

### Movement/traits (non-numeric)
- Triple Jump (innate).
- Super Jump, Air Dash also available.
- Wall Jump is NOT innate to Lenalee — must be granted via an equipped Help Koma (e.g., Fuusuke's or Naruto's Help Koma per the FAQ section), and stacking it with her innate triple jump gives a "fourth jump" (author-labeled trick, unconfirmed mechanically beyond this description).

## Battle Koma — 4-Blocks version

- Specials: Enbu Kirikaze and Enbu Kuroshizuku.
- Health note (comparative, from 5-Block section): 4-Blocks Lenalee has **128 HP**.

| Special | Ground/Aerial | Input | Dmg (Normal) | Dmg (Laughter target) | Max combo | Distance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Enbu Kirikaze | Ground | X | 2 to 36 | 3 to 54 | 1 to 6 | Near to Mid |
| Enbu Kuroshizuku | Ground→Aerial→Ground | Up+X | 2 to 28 | 3 to 42 | 1 to 2 | Near |

Notes:
- Enbu Kirikaze (X): whirlwind kick from Dark Boots; knocks caught opponents flying; usable to punish opponents re-entering the arena after being knocked off-edge.
- Enbu Kuroshizuku (Up+X): forward somersault then downward strike; damages/pushes opponents in path; always ends with Lenalee descending fully to ground level. Risk noted: landing-position miscalculation can cause a self ring-out.

## Battle Koma — 5-Blocks version

- Specials swapped for: Onkyou no Tougi: Otakase (replaces Enbu Kirikaze) and Shittsui no Tougi: Tetsukase (replaces Enbu Kuroshizuku).
- Health: **144 HP** (vs 128 HP on the 4-Block version — a +16 HP difference).

| Special | Ground/Aerial | Input | Dmg (Normal) | Dmg (Laughter target) | Max combo | Distance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Onkyou no Tougi: Otakase | Ground→Aerial | X | 2 to 42 | 3 to 63 | up to 4 | Near to Mid |
| Shittsui no Tougi: Tetsukase | Ground→Aerial→Ground | Up+X | 2 to 46 | 3 to 69 | 1 | Near |

Notes:
- Onkyou no Tougi: Otakase (X): dash-in kick starter that transitions into an aerial combo; final hit kicks the opponent downward — used off-stage this is described as an instant ring-out; can double as an escape tool rather than pure offense.
- Shittsui no Tougi: Tetsukase (Up+X): vertical jump then vertical downward strike. Author specifically notes the *maximum listed damage is NOT from a direct hit* — it comes from side blades conjured by the Dark Boots during the downward strike, meaning landing squarely on the opponent does not achieve full damage; must be positioned slightly farther than normal and on a platform above the target to land the higher-damage version (author unconfirmed exact hit breakdown between direct strike vs. blade hits).

## Mechanic-revealing observations

- Support Koma cancel rule: any Support Koma is interrupted/wasted if the summoned character is struck immediately upon appearing (general JUS mechanic, not Lenalee-specific).
- Nature multiplier is explicitly stated as exactly **1.5x** for advantaged matchups (worked example given: 10 → 15 damage).
- Several moves marked "flexible" damage ranges (Whirlwind Kick 2–13, Reversed Crescent Kick 15–23, and both Battle Koma Specials) reflect variable hit counts within a single combo string rather than random damage — i.e., the low end is a single grazing hit and the high end is the full combo landing.
- Shittsui no Tougi: Tetsukase's true maximum damage comes from secondary hitboxes (side blades) rather than the primary downward strike — an example of a move's headline "max damage" number not corresponding to its most obvious hitbox.
- DY-equivalent notes aside (not present here — see Edajima file for that pattern), Lenalee's Whirlwind Kick and Advancing Crescent Kick (Down+Y) are explicitly called out as capable of Guard Break.
