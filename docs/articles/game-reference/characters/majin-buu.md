## Koma forms

### 6 Koma: Power
- J-Soul: 192
- Koma shape: 3x2 grid ("[][] / [][] / [][]")
- Special A: Kamehame Ha — Nature: Power, Hits: 14, Damage: 44–90 (higher value against wall). Beam attack, comboable into other beams; author notes it's weaker than Goku's version due to lacking a teleport component.
- Special B: Henka Beam — Nature: Power, Hits: 6, Damage: 36–66. Beam turns opponent into food, Buu eats and spits them out; restores some of Buu's health; described as having decent ring-out potential.

### 6 Koma: Laughter
- J-Soul: 192
- Koma shape: 2x3 grid ("[][][] / [][][]")
- Same specials as Power form presumably (guide doesn't restate separately); author notes Laughter Buu still uses Power-nature specials, which the author says leaves it "with not much weakness at all" despite laughter's own type weakness to knowledge.

## Combos (numeric)
- Basic combo: B, >B, Y, >Y — Damage: 60 basic, 90 max.
- B, ^B, ^Y — Damage: 36 basic, 54 max. ^B is a launcher; ^Y is slow so best paired with an active support.
- B, ^B, X (Kamehame Ha) — Damage: 62 basic, 93 max. Hits opponent airborne then catches them falling with the beam.
- B, ^B, ^X (Henka Beam) — Damage: 70 basic, 105 max. Same setup but eats them as they fall; author calls this generally better than the X version.
- Tap Buu + basic combo — Damage: 69 basic, 103 max. Not a true combo (opponent can block in the gap); situational vs. non-blocking opponents.
- vY (guard-break), then X or ^X — generic guardbreak-into-special combo, no separate damage total given (sum of vY + special values above).

### Support-assisted combos
- B, >B, Y, [Piccolo support during Y], ^X or X — author prefers ^X here since it deals more damage on average, heals Buu, and executes faster.
- B, ^B, ^Y, [Piccolo support], ^X — Piccolo pulls opponent back to Buu after being launched by ^Y, chains into the "Infinite Combo" below.
- With Usopp 3-koma (One Piece) support: B, >B, Y, ^X — Damage: 118, Max: 150+. Usopp's firebird shot lands simultaneously with the spit-out from ^X, plus applies a burn (damage-over-time) status.
- With Takeshi Yamamoto 3-koma (Katekyo Hitman Reborn) support, activated during ^Y: B, ^B, ^Y, [Takeshi], X or ^X — Damage: 113, Max: 151.
- Semi-Infinite Combo: ^X + Piccolo — Damage: 68 per cycle, 102 max per cycle. Timing-dependent loop: summon Piccolo as Buu spits the opponent out, Piccolo pulls them back in, repeat with another ^X.

## Support koma (mentioned, mostly non-numeric)
- Big 3 (Trunks, Sena, Seiya 3-koma): create distance from opponent; frowned upon competitively.
- Satsuki Kitaooji 2-koma (Ichigo 100%): extra HP regen for Buu; author notes an unspecified "glitch" that makes it especially effective when combined with Buu's special (no numbers given).
- Neuro 2-koma: applies Blindness at a distance from Neuro's position (rather than around Neuro), useful for landing mid-combo ahead of where the opponent will end up; also gives Buu a health boost per author (no numeric value given).

## Help koma (mentioned)
- Neuro: prevents paralysis, grants an ally support.
- Gintoki: increases Special (SP) gauge when blocking at the last moment (perfect-block/parry-timing mechanic).
- Mr. Satan: increases SP on KO.
- Ichigo / Ishida: increase SP gain generally (no numeric rate given for either).

## Battle koma synergy (mentioned, non-numeric)
- Gintoki (any koma): boosts Buu; his 5-koma specials described as very fast and powerful, said to out-damage Buu's specials against Power-nature koma; Gintoki's 5-koma ^X also regenerates HP.

## Mechanic-revealing observations
- Forward Y explicitly supports rapid-tap input extension: base 4 hits, cap confirmed at "Max: 4" via repeated Y presses — a hit-count cap distinct from Dio's uncapped-looking rapid-tap specials.
- vY (Downward Y) is explicitly tagged as a "Guard-break" move, and the guide generalizes "guardbreak + special" as a combo pattern that "works for a great many characters" — implying guardbreak-into-special-cancel is a system-wide combo rule, not Buu-specific.
- The Henka Beam (eat-and-spit) and Kamehame Ha act as juggle-continuation tools after a launcher (^B), confirming launched opponents can be hit again by beam-type specials while airborne (juggle system).
- Support characters can be activated mid-animation during Buu's own moves (mid-Y, mid-^Y) without interrupting Buu's own attack — confirms supports run on an independent timeline from the active character's attack animation.
- "Tap Buu" (repeated basic-move tapping) into a combo is explicitly called out as beatable by blocking in the gap between hits — an explicit statement that non-true-combo strings leave a blockable window.
