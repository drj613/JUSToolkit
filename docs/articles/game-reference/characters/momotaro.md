# Momotaro — Character Guide Notes

- Source: "Jump Ultimate Stars — Momotaro Character Guide" by Christian Fontanet (TheFenikkusu, screen name Ryobit)
- Version: 1.0
- GameFAQs FAQ id: gf-50074
- Unverified community source — numbers and mechanic claims below are one player's testing, not verified against the game/emulator.

## Passive traits

- Built-in reduced damage taken from punches and kicks.
- Built-in reduced damage taken from sword/blade attacks.
- Ally boosts are described as generally weak ("terrible"), except from Edajima (author unconfirmed on numeric value).

## Support combos

- B > Side-B > Y > Shishio-2 > (run toward opponent) B > Y > Shishio-2, repeatable ("rinse and repeat until you reach the limit") — an infinite-style loop. Author notes the same loop timing also works with Gotenks-3 and Bobobo-2, and can alternate between them; Gotenks-3 variant can also ring out the enemy easily.

### 4-Koma combos
- B > Side-B > Y > Gotenks-3 > X: 108 damage (power types), 96 (laughter types), 122 (knowledge types).
- B > Side-B > Y > Kagura-3 (wait until the 5th smash) > X: 113 damage (power types), 123 (knowledge types), 93 (laughter types).

### 5-Koma combos
- B > Side-B > Y > Kenshiro-3 > X: ~76 damage (neutral types), ~113 (knowledge types).
- B > Side-B > Y > Kenshin-2 > X: only ~44 damage, but Kenshin's paralysis lasts long enough to repeat the combo; noted as great for ring-outs. Fails against paralysis-immune opponents.

### 6-Koma combos
(Same combos as above also apply, with faster X timing.)
- B > Side-B > Y > Naruto-3 > Up-X: 102 damage (neutral types), 162 damage (knowledge types). Timing note: press Up-X immediately after Naruto-3.
- B > Side-B > Y > Shishio-2 > Up-X: ~108 damage (neutral types), up to 165 damage (knowledge types, author's best result).

## Mechanic-revealing / edge-case notes

- Momotaro's Ultimate Action (Select) recovers a "decent" SP amount but drains SP slowly whenever not actively fighting due to his built-in passive — author recommends avoiding it except in FFA when opponents are fighting each other (no numeric rate given).
- Edge-hogging: only one character can hold a ledge at a time; grabbing the ledge first denies the opponent's recovery.
- While hanging on a ledge, some characters (cited: Renji, Gohan) have attacks that can hit ledge-hanging opponents, making the "wait it out" edge-guard escape unsafe against them.
- Touching Momotaro's panel repeatedly triggers repeated Side-Y ("blue tiger") shots — used as a repeated edge-guarding tool (implies panel-tap input can retrigger a ranged attack outside normal combo input).
- Support Koma synergy for combo extension/looping: Shishio-2, Gotenks-3, Bobobo-2 all reported to enable the same infinite-style combo loop with adjusted timing.
