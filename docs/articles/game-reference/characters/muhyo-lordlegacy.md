## Character/type info
- Type: Knowledge (exclusively). Grouped as a "summoner" archetype with Yugi and Taikoubou.
- Koma range: 1 through 7 (only character in the guide's claim to span this full range, with no alternate-type koma).
- Described as low J-Soul across all his forms relative to other characters, and generally low health/HP overall.

## Full moveset (damage listed as knowledge-target(power-target), per guide convention)
Ultimate Action: Muhyo disguises as an urn and "sleeps," slowly regaining SP.

- **B (Book Hit)**: 6 (9). Fast interrupt/combo starter; only combos into Y when used as an interrupt.
- **B Forward (Ghost Attacker)**: 9 (13). Small ghost dashes forward, hits and stuns briefly. Little end lag. Combos into Y (close) or Y-forward. Can be held to increase range; continues even if Muhyo is hit during startup.
- **B Up (Spikes)**: 9 (13). Spikes launch opponent upward; less range-restricted than B-forward; hits slightly behind Muhyo too. Combos into Y or Y-up. Has recovery delay after use.
- **B Down (Palm Strike)**: 7 (10). Character-change move. Steps forward after a charge moment. Only combos into Y-up.
- **B Aerial (Ghost Attacker, air)**: 10 (15). Same visual as B-forward but fixed range (can't be extended by holding). Does not spike.
- **Y (Satan's Shadow)**: 16 (24). Ground summon grabs and drags opponent forward. Slow, high end lag, but combos from B-forward/B-up at certain ranges. Holds opponent for a while (good for support combos). Cannot pass through walls; hold Y to extend range; the extending range cannot go off ledges/platforms, but once grabbed the target can be dragged off ledges/platforms. Can whiff into a "grab mode" without actually grabbing.
- **Y Forward (The Devil's Pitchfork)**: 20 (30). Author calls this Muhyo's premier combo tool. Notes: hold Y to increase range; continues even if Muhyo is hit; launch direction depends on which side of the hitbox connects (far side launches opponent away, near side launches opponent toward Muhyo).
- **Y Up (Never-Returning Owl)**: 20 (30). Anti-air: fast owl grabs anything in its path and drops for damage; leaves target "unspinning" in the air briefly, allowing support follow-ups.
- **Y Down (Satan's Iron Hammer)**: 8(12) + 10(15), two-hit Guard Break. Fast, good range, can hit multiple blockers, spikes on hit.
- **Y Aerial (Shadow Creatures)**: 7(10) per hit, launches on 3rd hit. Attack originates from the ground below Muhyo regardless of his height in the air. Keeps Muhyo stationary in the air during use (potential dodge tool).

## Passive effects
- Regains SP when fighting Knowledge-type characters (other passive triggers "unknown" per author).

## Specials / Ultimate Actions by koma
**4-Koma** (128 J-Soul):
- Special A "Hades' Dinner Punishment": 40(60). Ground-mouth trap in front of Muhyo; limited range; won't spawn if it would appear off a platform edge.
- Special B "Demonic Spellbook Punishment": 33(49) formula given as [1×hit + 20], mash X for more hits up to 13 total hits.

**5-Koma** (144 J-Soul):
- Special A "Magic Train Summon": 40(60). Hits 6 times, character-changes on the last hit, carries opponent near screen edge. 20 of the 40 total damage is dealt to the "second" (character-changed-to) character.
- Special B "Secluded Plum - Summon": 48(72). Whirlwind carries victim up before exploding. Combos out of guard break at close range and out of Y-forward. Easier to land full damage than the 6-Koma version but harder to aim.

**6-Koma** (160 J-Soul):
- Special A "The Great Demon General Summon": 50(75). Rush attack; cancels if it connects with an energy blast, a support, or a blocking character. Combos out of Y. Sends target very far. Cannot pressure guard.
- Special B "Secluded Plum - Summon (True Form)": 56(84). Bigger version of 5-Koma's Y^-summon equivalent; draws enemies up off-screen before exploding. Can no longer combo out of guard break; slightly harder to max damage. Platforms above Muhyo block the combo route into it.

**7-Koma** (176 J-Soul):
- Special A "Dark King Ruaire Summon - Douato Spear": 52(78). Very slow startup/recovery; good guard-break potential; leaves Muhyo stuck in place for a long time — advised only from a safe position.
- Special B "Dark King Ruaire Summon - Thousand Hit Tongue": 64(96), formula [2×hit + 4], mash X for more hits up to 30 total hits. Pops enemy up then whips with tongue. Platforms above Muhyo block combo route into it.

## Mechanic-revealing observations
- Hit-stun/counter mechanic: "B needs to counter for sufficient hitstun" to combo into Y — implies normal-hit B does not leave enough hitstun to link into Y, but a counter-hit does.
- Y-forward's continuation/launch direction is positionally determined (near-side vs far-side hit) — a mechanic detail relevant to hitbox-side launch angle logic.
- Several moves explicitly stated to continue/complete even if Muhyo is hit mid-startup (B-forward when held, Y-forward) — suggests certain moves have an uninterruptible "committed" projectile/summon phase independent of the character's own hit state.
- Y (Satan's Shadow) can "whiff" into a distinct grab-active state without connecting — implies the move has separate grab-detection vs. animation-active phases.
- Guard push/guard-break interactions: 6-Koma Special A cannot pressure guard; 5-Koma Special A can; Y-down is an explicit 2-hit Guard Break that can hit multiple blocking targets simultaneously.
- Platform geometry affects special summon spawning/combo routing: 4-Koma Special A won't spawn off a platform edge; 6-Koma Special B and 7-Koma specials are blocked from comboing if platforms exist above Muhyo.
