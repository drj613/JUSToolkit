## Moveset — damage given per-hit as [Power][Knowledge] then [Laughter], i.e. "Damage: X [P][K], Y [L]"

- B — "Book Smack": combo starter, fast/short range. Damage: 6 [P][K], 9 [L].
- Forward B — "Ghost Attacker": monster slashes in front; minimal recovery, safe poke; hold B to extend range. Damage: 9 [P][K], 13 [L].
- Up B — "Spike Summon": air launcher, fairly fast, starts air combos. Damage: 9 [P][K], 13 [L].
- Down B — "Palm Thrust": character-switch move; fast activation, laggy after; cannot be combo'd into. Damage: 7 [P][K], 10 [L].
- Jump B — "Aerial Ghost Attacker": hits harder than ground version; air poke. Damage: 10 [P][K], 15 [L].
- Y — "Satan's Shadow": ground monster grabs/drags opponent on hit; significant recovery lag whether it hits or misses; cannot pass through walls; can drag opponents off ledges if it grabs them near one, otherwise cannot leave platforms on its own; hold Y to extend range. Damage: 16 [P][K], 24 [L].
- Forward Y — "Giant Fork Summon": launches opponent up; if the near side of the fork connects, enemy launches toward Muhyo; if the far side connects, enemy launches away; using it directly after neutral Y produces the "away" launch; hold Y to extend range. Damage: 20 [P][K], 30 [L].
- Up Y — "Never Returning Owl": quick anti-air, best as combo filler, hard to land on its own. Damage: 20 [P][K], 30 [L].
- Down Y — "Satan's Iron Hammer": guard break, can hit multiple blockers, comes out quickly. First Hit: 8 [P][K], 12 [L]. Both Hits: 18 [P][K], 27 [L].
- Jump Y — "Shadow Blobs": three shadow projectiles emerge and explode in midair, good vs. crowds beneath Muhyo. Damage per hit: 7 [P][K], 10 [L].

### Specials (X / Up X), by koma size

4 Koma:
- X — Special A "Meiou no Bansan no Kei": ground-mouth swallow attack; leaves Muhyo open; best used after neutral Y. Damage: 40 [P][K], 60 [L].
- Up X — Special B "Mazukan no Kei": overhead spellbook, flipping pages damage enemies; hard to land full damage. Damage: 33 [P][K], 49 [L].

5 Koma:
- X — Special A "Maretsusha no Kei": train appears behind Muhyo, rushes forward. Damage: 40 [P][K], 60 [L].
- Up X — Special B "Yuurishoukan": grey monster swirls upward, small vortex, catches nearby people; control pad changes vertical direction. Damage: 48 [P][K], 72 [L].

6 Koma:
- X — Special A "Makensui no Kei": mounted knight charges forward; powerful but easily blocked by enemy supports occupying the field. Damage: ~50 [P][K], ~72 [L] (author uses "~", approximate).
- Up X — Special B "Yuurisoukan Hontou no Sugata": bigger vortex monster, larger area, can catch two fighters; control pad changes direction. Damage: ~56 [P][K], ~84 [L] (approximate, author notation).

7 Koma:
- X — Special A "Meiouruararieshoukan Touato no Yari": extremely slow but powerful, giant monster throws a lance forward; best used when opponents aren't watching. Damage: 52 [P][K], 78 [L].
- Up X — Special B "Meiouruararieshoukan Senpen no Shita": big monster throws a flurry of punches forward; has edge-guard potential if timed right; press X for more hits (mashable multi-hit). Damage: 64 [P][K], 96 [L].

## Mechanic observations

- Y ("Satan's Shadow") cannot pull enemies through walls; normally cannot pull them off platforms/ledges unless grabbed near an edge already.
- Forward Y's launch direction depends on which side of the hitbox connects (near side = pull toward Muhyo, far side = push away); chaining directly from neutral Y forces the "push away" outcome.
- Down B (character switch) cannot be integrated into combos — implies a hard-recovery/committed-animation rule for switch moves.
- Author notes most of Muhyo's moves have "lots of lag" after use (general recovery-heavy character).
