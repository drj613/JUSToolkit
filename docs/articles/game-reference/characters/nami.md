Nami has two forms: **Clima-Tact Nami** and **Perfect Clima-Tact Nami**. Basic-attack sections are given separately for each below.

Note from source: for 4-koma Laughter-nature Nami, swap the Power and Laughter damage values listed (i.e., the numbers below are written for the Knowledge/Power-nature version by default; author's parenthetical applies specifically to the 4-koma).

## Basic attacks — Clima-Tact Nami

- Neutral B (basic poke, chains into other attacks): Power/Knowledge 6, Laughter 9
- Forward B ("Thunder Tempo" — spring-loaded glove projectile forward, pushback, long recovery unless canceled into neutral Y): Power/Knowledge 6, Laughter 9
- Up B ("Rain Tempo" — hits both sides of Nami, no longer multi-hit compared to prior game): Power/Knowledge 6, Laughter 9
- Down B ("Cloudy Tempo" — fires flowers forward, forces opponent Battle Koma change on hit, short range, fast startup): Power/Knowledge 6, Laughter 9
- Aerial B (downward strike, knocks opponent through platforms, quick startup/recovery): Power/Knowledge 6, Laughter 9
- Neutral Y (spin attack, base 3 hits, holding X extends to up to 15 hits total): Power/Knowledge 1 dmg/hit, 3-15 hits, max total 15; Laughter 1-2 dmg/hit, 3-15 hits, max total 22
- Forward Y ("Cyclone Tempo" — thrown rod, up to 4 hits: 2 forward + 2 return, hits multiple lined-up opponents): Power/Knowledge 6/hit, 4 hits max, 24 total; Laughter 9/hit, 4 hits max, 36 total
- Up Y ("Fine Tempo" — 3 doves fired in a fan pattern, slow startup/recovery): Power/Knowledge 5/bird, 15 if all 3 connect; Laughter 7/bird, 22 if all 3 connect
- Down Y (waver dash, guard break, usable from ~half-screen range, fast forward movement): Power/Knowledge 12, Laughter 18
- Aerial Y ("Thunder Ball" — slow-moving electric ball projectile, up to 2 can be out at once, pushes opponent back on each hit, up to 4 hits total): Power/Knowledge 2/hit, 8 total; Laughter 3/hit, 12 total

## Basic attacks — Perfect Clima-Tact Nami

- Neutral B (same role as Clima-Tact version): Power/Knowledge 6, Laughter 9
- Forward B ("Swing Arm" — rod swing forward, pushback, long recovery unless canceled into neutral Y): Power/Knowledge 6, Laughter 9
- Up B ("Thunder Charge" — knocks opponent upward, easy chain into Up Y, noticeable recovery): Power/Knowledge 6, Laughter 9
- Down B (downward Clima-Tact swing, forces opponent Battle Koma change, short range, fast startup): Power/Knowledge 6, Laughter 9
- Aerial B (downward strike, quick startup/recovery): Power/Knowledge 6, Laughter 9
- Neutral Y (spin attack, base 3 hits, up to 15 with X held, though typically 10-12 hits unless started point-blank): Power/Knowledge 1 dmg/hit, 3-15 hits, max total 15; Laughter 1-2 dmg/hit, 3-15 hits, max total 22
- Forward Y ("Cyclone Tempo," faster rod travel than Clima-Tact version, up to 2 hits: 1 forward + 1 return): Power/Knowledge 6/hit, 2 hits max, 12 total; Laughter 9/hit, 2 hits max, 18 total
- Up Y ("Thunderbolt Tempo" — cloud + thunder ball, then a thunderbolt strike; can be blocked by thin platforms above): Power/Knowledge 1/cloud-or-ball hit, 12 for the thunderbolt; Laughter 1/cloud-or-ball hit, 12 for the thunderbolt
- Down Y (small tornado + forward charge, guard break, described as weaker guard-break than the waver move): Power/Knowledge 12, Laughter 18
- Aerial Y ("Thunder Ball," same behavior as Clima-Tact version but up to 8 hits total): Power/Knowledge 2/hit, 16 total; Laughter 3/hit, 24 total

## Help Koma

- Increases Special Gauge gain when breaking item boxes.

## Support Koma

### 2-koma (shape: `[][]`)
- Nami dashes forward ~half-screen, stealing 2 SP bars (or all available if opponent has fewer than 2) from every opponent she passes through, adding stolen SP to the user. Damage: 0.

### 3-koma (shape: `[][]` over `  []`)
- Nami appears wrapped in a towel, flashes, producing an expanding heart-shaped zone. Those hit lose 2 special bars and are afflicted with "Battle Seal" status. Damage: 0.

## Battle Koma — shared passives

- Innate air-dash.
- Gains more SP from coins.
- Gains SP from breaking treasure chests.
- Ultimate Action: taunts opponent; any non-blocking opponent close enough loses some SP and takes 1 point of damage.

## 5-koma

- Shape: `[][][]` / `[][]`
- **Health: 144**
- Neutral X — "Tornado Tempo" (Knowledge nature): rods form a horizontal connection; mechanical birds grab hit opponents, then after 1-2 seconds one rod detaches and launches the opponent backward a long distance. ## 6-koma

- Shape: `[][]` / `[][]` / `[][]`

## Mechanic-revealing observations

- SP-steal specials (2-koma support, 4-koma Neutral X) function as guaranteed-minimum resource-drain attacks that deal 0 physical damage but can combo repeatedly — implies these moves have short enough recovery/hitstun interaction to chain into themselves via support/neutral-Y resets (author describes near-infinite loops using specific support koma to interrupt/reset spacing).
- Down Y moves ("all Down Y attacks") universally have guard-break properties per the author — a cross-cast mechanic note, not Nami-specific but stated as a general engine rule.
- "All down B attacks" force a Battle Koma character switch on the opponent when they connect — another stated general engine rule.
- Up Y ("Thunderbolt Tempo," Perfect Clima-Tact) can be blocked by thin platforms positioned above the target, implying projectile/hitbox collision with stage geometry.
- Status effects referenced with gameplay impact: Battle Seal, Confusion, Blindness, Burn, Shock, Stun, Invisibility, Support Seal, Movement Seal, Freezing, Paralysis — used throughout but only qualitatively described (durations/exact effects not quantified in this source).
