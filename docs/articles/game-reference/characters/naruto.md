# Naruto

- Source guide: "Naruto FAQ"
- Author: son1cwildfire
- Version: not stated
- GameFAQs FAQ id: gf-47526
- Note: unverified community source; numbers below are as reported by the guide author and have not been independently verified against the game.

Guide's convention for the damage tables: `Damage(P/L)` = damage vs. Power/Laughter-nature targets, `Damage(K)` = damage vs. Knowledge-nature targets, reflecting the fact that Naruto's specials are Power-nature (dealing bonus damage to Knowledge).

## Koma forms

### 1 Koma (Help)
- Effect: grants wall-jump ability.

### 2 Koma (Support) — "Sexy no Jutsu"
- Requires a short start-up; stuns the enemy on connect.
- Damage: 0 (no damage dealt).

### 3 Koma (Support)
- Uses the 4-koma's Special B move, Uzumaki Rendan.
- Damage: 30 to Power/Laughter, 45 to Knowledge.

### 4 Koma (Battle)
- Two shape/nature variants: a square-shaped Power koma and a line-shaped Laughter koma (both use identical special move damage values, since both specials are Power-type regardless of the koma's own nature).
- Special A: Rasengan — requires brief start-up.
- Special B: Uzumaki Rendan — noted as stronger than the Rasengan, "surprisingly."
- Damage table (both Power and Laughter koma variants use the same special damage):

| Special | vs Power/Laughter | vs Knowledge |
|---|---|---|
| A (Rasengan) | 32 | 48 |
| B (Uzumaki Rendan) | 36 | 54 |

### 5 Koma (Battle) — Power nature only
- Special A: Rasengan, now preceded by 2 kicks during the charge-up, making it easier to combo into.
- Special B: Shadow Clone Attack — a wide-hitting move that can strike anyone in view/on screen (author unconfirmed exact hitbox but describes it as hitting anyone "within your view").
- Damage table:

| Special | vs Power/Laughter | vs Knowledge |
|---|---|---|
| A (Rasengan) | 40 | 60 |
| B (Shadow Clone Attack) | 32 | 48 |

### 6 Koma (Battle) — Power nature only
- Special A: Rasengan held by two Narutos simultaneously (implies a stronger/charged variant using a doubled character model).
- Special B: "Rasengan Combo" — kicks the opponent up, summons shadow clones to hold them down, then applies the Rasengan while they're held.
- Damage table:

| Special | vs Power/Laughter | vs Knowledge |
|---|---|---|
| A (Rasengan) | 48 | 72 |
| B (Rasengan Combo) | 44 | 66 |

Note: the last kick of the "B, B^, Y" route is said to temporarily "stun" the opponent — author recommends this combo near an edge instead of spending SP on a support combo, since the stun window covers the ring-out setup.

## Ultimate Action
- Effect: grants a single "free hit" (you can be hit once without taking the usual consequence) while an icon (described as a "wood icon") remains active in the bottom-left corner.
- Author's recommended use case: against opponents with rapid multi-hit specials (e.g., Dio, Luffy) — get hit once during their multi-hit move, then teleport behind them and counter-attack.

## Ally boosts (support characters synergizing with Naruto)

### Sakura
- Help koma: halves duration of negative status effects (except Judgement).
- 2-koma support: basic punch, 28 damage to Power/Laughter, 42 damage to Knowledge.
- 3-koma support: heals the player and removes status effects.

### Gaara
- Help koma: grants auto-guard.
- 2-koma support: forward sand attack, requires brief start-up. Damage: 22 to Power/Knowledge, 38 to Laughter.
- 3-koma support: player-targeted hit selection. Damage: 34 to Power/Knowledge, 59 to Laughter.

### Jiraiya
- Help koma: grants 1 extra SP bar.

## Deck-building notes (numeric-relevant only)
- 4-koma deck: recommends Laughter-nature Naruto 4-koma (since specials are Power-type regardless, dealing bonus damage to Knowledge opponents; Laughter koma nature helps against the common Power-character meta).
- 5-koma deck: Zoro 3-koma support recommended; author notes the support combo costs at least 3 SP bars.
- 6-koma deck: Piccolo (or any pull-effect support) recommended; author uses Josuke 2-koma personally for other reasons.
