## Support koma

**2-koma:** 0 damage; throws a brain a moderate distance, releases purple gas on landing that inflicts Blind status.

**3-koma:** 0 damage; appears in bird-head form, spits purple water that inflicts Invisible status on contact.

## Battle koma

### Passive effects (all versions)
- SP slowly drains away over time
- Immune to Confusion
- Immune to Stun
- Gains SP by attacking opponents of opposite nature, or by defending against attacks from opposite-nature opponents

### 4-koma (Knowledge nature)
- Shape: 3+1 ([][][] / [])
- Health: 152
- Neutral X Special "You, you are the killer!" (Nature: Knowledge): Yako+Neuro fire a narrow purple aura in a straight line, short range (less than half screen). Max hits 3. Power/Knowledge: 10/hit, 30 total. Laughter: 15/hit, 45 total.
- Up X Special "Ejiki" (Nature: Power): summon (Shirota) charges, Neuro dodges, monster slams ground. Damage: Power/Laughter 40; Knowledge 60. Author notes: high startup lag, poor combo utility.

### 4-koma (Power nature)
- Shape: 2x2 ([][] / [][])
- Health: 152
- Same Neutral X and Up X specials/numbers as the Knowledge 4-koma (listed identically in source).

### 5-koma (Knowledge nature)
- Shape: 2+2+1 ([][] / [][] / [])
- Health: 168
- Neutral X Special "You, you are the killer!": wider/longer aura than 4-koma version. Power/Knowledge: max hits 5, 4 hits at 5 dmg + final hit 15 dmg = 35 total. Laughter: max hits 5, two hits at 7 dmg + two hits at 8 dmg + final hit 22 dmg = 52 total.
- Up X Special "Hitorikii": Yako sings; anyone in radius around Neuro afflicted with Confusion status. 0 damage.
- Author's ranking: 5-koma is best of the three due to reach of neutral X; both 4/5-koma Up X specials considered largely useless.

## Ally boosts (character interactions, non-numeric mostly)

- Muhyo (Muhyo to Rouji) gives/receives ally boost with Neuro.
- X (Majin Tantei Nougami Neuro) help koma: reduces negative status-effect duration.
- Light Yagami help koma: gain SP from attacking/defending vs opposite nature (redundant with Neuro's own passive).
- Suggested help koma effects for Neuro: SP regen while on field (from Yusuke Urameshi or Gon Freecss help koma) to offset Neuro's passive SP drain; immunity to Speed-Down (from Johnny Joestar help koma).

## Combos (mechanic-revealing notes)

- B → Up Y: ring-out combo; second hit can fail if too close to opponent on the first hit.
- B → Y → Up Y: ring-out combo; requires close proximity on initial hit for the grab to connect.
- B → Y → X (1 SP bar): full combo into Neutral X super, works best on 5-koma; on 4-koma requires opponent near a wall or point-blank first hit.
- Forward B → Forward Y: setup combo.
- Support-based combos require 1 SP bar unless noted; a listed multi-part combo (B→Y→Kenshin 2-koma→B→Y→Up Y→Kuwabara 2-koma) requires 2 bars of special.
- Reiko 3-koma support combo (from Sasuke-adjacent note, actually Neuro-listed): B → Reiko 3-koma described elsewhere as dealing 55 (Power) / 40 (Knowledge) / 45 (Laughter) when connecting after Neutral B — these numbers describe Reiko's support koma, not Neuro's own attack.
- Forward Y → Enchyo 3-koma: opponent remains hittable during Forward Y squeeze phase, allowing support hits to land concurrently (confirms squeeze-phase does not grant invulnerability).
- Note: opponents are stated to have a few seconds of invulnerability after getting up from being knocked down (post-Forward Y combo timing note).
