# Nico Robin — Character FAQ Notes

- Source: "Nico Robin Character FAQ" by FFandMMfan

## Acquisition / Evolution

- Acquired at the end of the game, in Planet U (J-World). Help Koma unlocked via Mission 4, Objective 5 (collect all Stars in 40 seconds).
- Straight linear Support/Battle evolution chart (no branching): H -> S2 -> S3, and H -> B4 -> B5 -> B6.
- All 3 battle Koma are Knowledge nature.

## Help / Support Koma

- **Help**: Attached character spends a portion of their SP gauge to auto-guard against damage.
- **Support 2** shape `[][]`: touchscreen targeting (4-panel display), Robin copies arms onto target and applies Blind status. Usable at any range across the stage, blockable, deals **0 damage**.
- **Support 3** shape `[][][]`: arm wave spreads from both sides of Robin along the ground. Max damage: **40** vs Power/Knowledge, **60** vs Laughter. Noted for ringout/keep-away utility.

## Passive Effects (all Robin Koma)

- Can spend a small portion of SP gauge to auto-guard enemy attacks.
- Gains increased SP when attacking or blocking Laughter-nature characters.
- Nature is Knowledge for all battle Koma: 1.5x damage vs Laughter, normal vs Power/Knowledge (matches the numbers listed below, e.g. B: 8 vs P/K, 12 vs L = 1.5x).

## Combos (no Support Koma)

Any Battle Koma:
- B -> B+up -> Y+up = 30 (P/K) / 45 (L)
- Y+forward -> B -> B+up -> Y+up = 38 (P/K) / 57 (L)
- B+forward -> Y at ledge = ring-out setup, damage irrelevant (author notes hit lag prevents true "combo")
- Y on thin platform = instant-kill through floor
- B+down -> Y = 24 (P/K) / 36 (L); force-switches enemy; author notes Y+down cannot combo into Y

4 Koma only:
- B+down -> X = 43 (P/K) / 64 (L)
- B -> B+forward -> X = 49 (P/K) / 73 (L)

5 Koma only:
- Y+up -> X+up (max mashing) = 43 (P/K) / 66 (L), max hits
- B -> B+up -> Y+up -> X+up = 58 (P/K) / 87 (L), max hits
- B+down -> Y+up -> X+up = 50 (P/K) / 75 (L), max hits; must be close to foe or special won't connect

6 Koma only: author states no known unique support-less combos.

## Ultimate Action

- Produces a hand from ground, sits, reads a book. Slowly restores SP. No numeric SP-per-second given.

## Battle Koma Details

### Battle Koma 4
- J-Soul: 128
- Nature: Knowledge
- Shape: `[][][]` / ` [] ` (3 top, 1 center-bottom)
- Special A: Flower petals; if connect, multi-hit hand attack on enemy body. Robin immobile during move (and so is target). Max damage: 52 (L) / 35 (P/K).
- Special B: Tower of hands around Robin, launches all nearby foes airborne, multi-hit. Max damage: 42 (L) / 28 (P/K).
- Author notes: X+up is "painfully weak," outshined by 0-SP basic combos; risky in 4-player free-for-alls since X leaves Robin immobile/exposed.

### Battle Koma 5
- J-Soul: 144
- Nature: Knowledge
- Shape: `[][][]` / `  [][]`
- Special A: Long trail of hands along ground, sweeps forward, drags caught enemies a long distance. Does LESS damage if opponent is against a wall (opposite of most stream multi-hits — want first-hit-then-drag for full damage). Works across platforms (splits hand-stream across two platform levels; range reduced on the lower platform per author's testing). Max damage: 48 (L) / 32 (P/K).
- Special B: Circulating field of flower petals; mash X for more hits; drags enemies to center then flings them away at the end. Max damage: 45 (L) / 30 (P/K).
- Author calls this her most balanced Koma; X+up can combo from her best basic combos (unlike other Koma specials) and works as an edge-guard/shield against projectiles.

### Battle Koma 6
- J-Soul: 160
- Nature: Knowledge
- Shape: `[][][]` / `[][][]` (full 2x3)
- Special A: Wave of hands on both sides (like Support 3), spaced apart — high miss chance on individual hits. Max damage: 90 (L) / 60 (P/K).
- Special B: Spins, spreads flower petals causing body-spawned hand attacks. Blockable, telegraphed. Max damage (listed as "Damage Output"): 66 (L) / 43 (P/K).
- Author: highest J-Soul of her Koma, best suited to 4-player free-for-alls; X+up can disable/damage a whole group from an elevated platform.

## Mechanic-revealing observations

- Robin's Y attack pulls enemies through thin floors, causing an un-recoverable ring-out kill — a specific interaction with "thin floor" platform type.
- Y attack does NOT knock enemies through the Sudden Death platform specifically (special-cased platform).
- Robin's floor-spawned attacks (Y, Y+down, B+down) still connect/resolve even if Robin herself is hit after the attack is "released" — i.e., these moves are not interruptible once triggered, distinct from her body's hit state.
- Guard break (Y+down) and force-switch (B+down) similarly resolve even if Robin is hit after activation.
- Support Koma "Naruto 3" combined with Battle Koma 6's X+up was observed by the author to occasionally deal anomalously high damage (up to 131 vs a stated combo baseline) due to overlapping hitboxes from the hand spawns — described as an inconsistent/non-reliable interaction (author unconfirmed as a real mechanic vs. hitbox overlap fluke).

## Support Combos (numeric)

- Y+Forward -> Inui (Support 3 Koma) launched immediately on grab = 26 (P/K) / 51 (L); applies Support Seal, removes 2 SP bars from foe, launches them backward.
- Y+down -> Anna (Support 3 Koma) = 57 (Power) / 43 (Knowledge) / 50 (Laughter); double guard-break + Slow status.
- 4 Koma: X -> Hitsugaya Support 3 -> X -> Hitsugaya 3 -> ... described as "semi-infinite" ring-out loop (limited by SP cost of 1 SP per use of X); narrow timing window.
- 4 Koma: X -> Zoro Support 3 = 71 (Power) / 89 (Knowledge) / 88 (Laughter); strict hitbox/timing (Zoro must land before the flower hits, in a narrow window; stand ~1 "Komaman" distance from foe).
- 5 Koma: B -> B+up -> Y+up -> X+up -> Kurama Support 2 = same damage as the standard 5-Koma combo (58/87) plus Blind and an SP-bar removal.
- 6 Koma: Y+down -> Lucifer Support 3 -> X+up = 91 (P/K) / 136 (L).
- Guard break -> Lucifer Support 3 -> Battle Koma 6's X+up = 91 damage to a "neutral nature" (credited to a third party, MasterTurtle).
