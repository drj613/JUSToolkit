## Obtaining Piccolo

Dragon Ball World, Mission 3, Objective 4: collect all 7 Dragon Balls within 30 seconds.

## Koma Forms

### Help/Support Koma

- **Piccolo Help Koma**: grants slow health regeneration to the battle character it's attached to (no numeric rate given).
- **Piccolo 2 Koma [Power]** — "Nobiru ude" (Expanding Arm)
  - Effect: pulls the opponent toward the player.
  - Damage: 16 vs Power, 24 vs Knowledge, 16 vs Laughter.
  - Size: 2 panels (side by side, per ASCII diagram).
- **Piccolo 3 Koma [Power]** — "Makonkosappo" (likely Makankosappo, trademark beam)
  - Effect: fires the beam at the opponent directly ahead.
  - Damage: 5–23 vs Power, 7–34 vs Knowledge, 5–23 vs Laughter (ranged values, likely charge-dependent).
  - Size: 3 panels (vertical stack, per ASCII diagram).

### Battle Koma

- **Piccolo 4 Koma [Knowledge]**
  - Passive: gradually regains health over time; gains more SP when fighting Laughter-type opponents.
  - Ultimate Action: hold Select to slowly regain SP energy.
  - HP: 136.
  - Size: 4 panels (2x1 top row + 2 single panels stacked below, per ASCII diagram).
- **Piccolo 5 Koma [Knowledge]**
  - Passive: same as 4 koma (gradual HP regen; bonus SP vs Laughter).
  - Ultimate Action: hold Select to slowly regain SP energy.
  - HP: 152.
  - Size: 5 panels (2x2 block + 1 extra panel below, per ASCII diagram).

  - Beam that drills through (hits) multiple enemies in a line; brief charge time before firing.

  - Damage: N/A (self-heal move).

  - Damage: 3–48 vs Power/Laughter targets, 4–66 vs Knowledge targets.
  - Powered-up version of the 4-koma beam; can hold X to charge, increasing power as the aura around the hand intensifies (charge-to-damage relationship implied but not quantified).

## Mechanic-Revealing Observations

- Damage values consistently differ by opponent koma nature: Laughter-type targets take noticeably more damage than Power/Knowledge targets across nearly every move (e.g., B: 8 vs P/K, 12 vs L) — suggests a fixed nature-based damage multiplier system rather than per-move tuning.
- The Jump Y fireball is a placed/delayed projectile with a ~2-second timer or proximity trigger, and up to two can coexist on screen — implies a projectile cap of 2 for this move.
- Forward Y ("Nobiru ude") pulls the opponent toward the user — same effect name/description appears as the Piccolo 2 Support Koma's move, suggesting the support koma reuses the battle character's normal move data.
- The X special (Makankosappo) has a charge mechanic: holding X increases power before release; the 5-koma version's damage range (3–48 / 4–66) is higher than the 4-koma version's (3–27 / 4–45), consistent with the "powered up" description. The wide damage ranges likely reflect charge-time-to-damage scaling.
- Y (Bakurikimaha) causes a knockdown and has "significant lag time," i.e., high recovery/hitstun-adjacent vulnerability window if it whiffs — an anti-air/punish-window detail.
- Homing Fireballs (5-koma Up X) requires all 6 projectiles to remain on-screen to connect, implying they can be lost off-screen or blocked/destroyed individually.
