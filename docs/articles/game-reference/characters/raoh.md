# Raoh (Hokuto no Ken)

- Source: "Raoh Character FAQ" v1.3 (with v1.31 minor edit), by LifeVirus7
- GameFAQs FAQ id: gf-46626
- Unverified community source — numbers are from a fan-written guide, not verified against the game.

## Koma line / evolution

- Help koma (1H): unlocked by finishing mission 10, objective 1 in Boss Tower, J-Blackhole area of J-Universe. No support koma versions exist for Raoh.
- Evolution tree: `1H -> 6B, 7B, 8B` plus two "unlockable feature" branches (X1, X2) off the help koma.
  - 7B/8B require the second path upgrade on Yuria's evolution chart to be purchased first.

- Author notes Raoh has the highest health of any 8-block koma except Edijima (author's claim, unconfirmed against other sources).
- All koma are "large" (author's characterization of the 6/7/8 sizes as taking up more deck space).

### Type / nature interactions

Raoh is Power type in all forms:
- Deals extra damage to Knowledge-type characters.
- Takes extra damage from Laughter-type characters.

### Ally boost (adds +8 max HP if placed adjacent to Raoh)

- Toki (Hokuto no Ken)
- Yuria (Hokuto no Ken)
- Cascade (Midori no Makibaou)

### Passives

- Takes reduced damage from special attacks (innate).
- Gains special (SP) energy while guarding attacks (innate).
- Ultimate Action (hold Select): slowly recharges his special meter over time.

## Specials (X / Up+X) by koma size

### 6-koma
- X: charged energy burst to sides/above; grants Attack Boost status. Damage: 40 / 60.
- Up X: horse rears up then crashes down on enemy in front. Damage: up to 54 / up to 81.
- Author note: slower/blockable, less knockback on the horse move than the 7-koma version.

### 7-koma
- X: arm swing then energy beam forward, travels about one screen. Damage: up to 56 / up to 84.
- Up X: horse rear-and-crash, described as high knockback power. Damage: up to 68 / up to 102.

### 8-koma
- X: arm swing then larger energy beam, travels about 1.5 screens. Damage: up to 77 / up to 115. Author notes damage falls off sharply at farther distances (author unconfirmed mechanic detail — no numeric falloff curve given).
- Up X: wavy aura counterstance. If hit while active, Raoh counterattacks and briefly gains invincibility (not all attacks trigger the counter — some just knock him out of it instead). Damage: 85 / 127. Charge time same as 7-koma's X.

## Mechanic-revealing observations

- Guard blocks most damage/effects but can be broken by repeated hits while guarding (guard break threshold, no number given).
- Down+Y and the forced-swap Down+B are explicitly guard-breaking / forced-swap attacks — deal damage/effect even through guard.
- Y charges an "aura" state that buffs the damage/range of the following Forward Y or Up Y only — single-use buff, consumed on the next of those two moves.
- Midair Y can knock an opponent through certain platforms (e.g., in the Naruto stage), continuing until Raoh hits a surface or falls into a pit — described as uninterruptible once started.
- 8-koma Up X counter has a startup/vulnerability detail: "his ghost clones need to go out and come back to him before he gains invincibility," implying a delayed invincibility window (author unconfirmed exact frames).
- Beam-type specials (7/8 koma) hit for more total hits/damage when the opponent is pinned against a wall (they aren't knocked out of the attack's range).
- Two support koma interactions worth noting for SP mechanics (not Raoh-specific but numeric): Hah Brothers support "drain up to 2 special bars"; Hiei support "drains 2 special bars from anybody he passes"; Nami support drains 2 special bars from opponent and returns 2 to the user on hit; Ikki and Hyoga supports each drain 1 special bar on hit.
