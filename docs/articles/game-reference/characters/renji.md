# Renji Abarai (Bleach)

- Source guide: "Jump! Ultimate Stars Abarai Renji FAQ"
- Author: MasterTurtle (aka Hyperslasher), Version 2.0
- GameFAQs FAQ id: gf-46459
- Unverified community source — numbers are player-collected, not from game data files.

## Type / nature note

Renji is a Power-type character in all his koma forms. All of his attacks (regular and
special) deal **1.5x damage to Knowledge-type opponents**. This 1.5x power/knowledge
multiplier is stated as a general system rule, not Renji-specific.

## Acquiring Renji / evolution chart

Obtained in the Bleach world (J-Blackhole area), Mission 4, Objective 5 (get at least 2 KOs
using special attacks, in a fixed pre-made deck with Ichigo and Renji).

Evolution chart:
- H (Help) -> B4 (Battle, 4-koma) -> B5 (Battle, 5-koma) -> B6 (Battle, 6-koma)
- B4 also branches to S2 (Support, 2-koma) -> S3 (Support, 3-koma)

## Basic attacks (all koma sizes)

- **B**: sword slash. 9 damage.
- **B+side**: dashes forward slightly and slashes. 9 damage.
- **B+up**: quick upward slash; knocks enemies into the air, setting up Up+Y. 9 damage.
- **B+down**: charges ~1 tick then releases energy, forcing enemies to switch characters
  (forced-switch status effect). Range noted as shorter than it visually appears. 9 damage.
- **B aerial**: quick air slash; knocks enemies into the air, setting up Up+Y. 9 damage.
- **Y**: multi-hit Zabimaru attack, each hit only 3 damage but hits up to 6 times (max ~18
  damage total for 6 hits), and holds the opponent in place for a few seconds (hitstun/hold
  effect useful for support setups). Note: Renji steps forward slightly during the move and
  can walk off edges while using it.
- **Y+side**: extends Zabimaru in a line; has windup and recovery. Damage varies with
  distance, "usually 16-18 damage."
- **Y+up**: spins Zabimaru overhead, knocking nearby enemies up; pressing Y repeatedly
  extends the hit count. Damage range: 10–22, depending on hit duration. Setup move for the
  4-koma's Up+X special. Knockback direction on this move is described as effectively random.
  Note: can whiff against some short characters standing on the ground (example given: Don
  Patch).
- **Y+down**: charges ~1 tick, then jumps forward and slams Zabimaru into the ground. Can
  break guard (guard-break flag confirmed). Does not hit an enemy standing directly adjacent.
  18 damage.
- **Y aerial**: extends Zabimaru downward at an angle; can pass through floors (unlike some
  projectiles). Damage varies with hit count; author's max observed is 20. Useful edge-guard
  tool since it can hit people standing on ledges.
- **Select** (taunt): restores a small, unspecified amount of SP/special gauge.

## Support koma versions

- **Help koma**: Increases SP gauge when attacking or blocking with a battle character.
  Shape: single panel.
- **Support 2-koma**: Renji stabs Zabimaru into the ground; a second later it stabs up at a
  random nearby enemy. Deals **24 damage**. Requires close proximity to work; author rates
  it as not a great support due to this range limitation. Shape: 2 horizontal panels.
- **Support 3-koma**: Higa Zekkou technique — six blades hover around a (touch-screen
  selected, or auto-picked) target and converge. Deals **36 damage**. Works regardless of
  distance (unlike the 2-koma version); damage may be slightly reduced if some blades get
  blocked. Shape: 3 panels.

## Battle koma stats

All Renji battle koma have air-dash and "warp dash" (dash through targets without slowing)
abilities.

### Battle Koma 4
- J-Soul: 152
- **Special A**: ~0.5s charge, then a wide-arc Zabimaru swing. 36 damage to nearby enemies,
  24 damage to enemies further away (i.e. two damage tiers by distance). Can knock enemies
  through thin floors and hit targets standing on edges. Author rates it as not very useful
  overall — too slow for a supportless combo, and Special B outdamages it.
- **Special B**: ~0.5s charge, then Renji spins Zabimaru around himself repeatedly; hitting
  X repeatedly extends the attack. Damage: **18–42**, scaling with hit count; knocks back
  when the attack ends. Rated as excellent for a 4-koma — high damage, comboable.
- Shape: 4 panels vertical (single column).

### Battle Koma 5
- J-Soul: 168
- **Special A**: short charge, then multi-hit slash in front of Renji; hitting X repeatedly
  extends the attack; knocks back at the end. Damage: **19–43**, scaling with hit count.
  Locks opponent in place until the attack finishes — good for ring-outs or hitting multiple
  opponents. Note: enemies further away may take less than full damage.
- **Special B**: Higa Zekkou (six converging blades) — same technique as the 3-koma support,
  with two differences: (1) limited range (must be fairly close) but auto-targets, and (2)
  slightly higher damage — **42 damage if all blades hit**. Rated less useful than the 3-koma
  support version due to the range limitation.
- Shape: 5 panels — top row `[][]`, middle row `[][]`, bottom single panel `[]`.

### Battle Koma 6 (Bankai form)
- J-Soul: 184
- **Special A**: ~1.5s charge, summons Bankai (giant snake-like monster) that surges forward,
  damaging and pushing back all enemies. Damage: **50+ if an enemy is caught for the whole
  duration**. Slow to start but very strong; noted as excellent for pushing opponents off
  ledges (they get knocked down repeatedly if they try to recover).
- **Special B**: same Bankai summon (~1.5s charge) but launches upward instead of forward.
  Generally rated less useful (no ring-out utility going upward) and "tends to deal less
  damage" than Special A (no exact number given for this variant).
- Note: in both Bankai specials, the snake's trailing coils count as hits if touched, but
  deal **0 damage** (author explicit: hit-registers-but-no-damage on the coil hitbox).
- Shape: 6 panels, 3x2 rectangle (`[][]` / `[][]` / `[][]`).

## Mechanic-revealing observations

- **Wall/edge damage bonus for beam-type specials**: beam-type specials (including Renji's
  X specials) deal more damage if the opponent is backed against a wall. Author notes that
  a guarding opponent standing near a stage edge is treated the same as being against a
  wall for this purpose — meaning higher-level beam specials and Renji's Bankai can break
  guard purely through damage output when hitting a guarding opponent near an edge (normally
  guard blocks all damage; this implies a damage-threshold guard-break exists near edges,
  possibly a wall/edge-specific interaction with guard chip damage or knockback pressure).
- **Fast-fall**: pressing Down+A in the air makes Renji (and by extension, characters in
  general) drop more quickly — described as essential for blocking some aerial combos.
- Renji's Y move causes forward drift that can walk him off a ledge — a self-positioning
  hazard tied to a specific move's animation.
- Up+Y's knockback direction is stated to be effectively random, complicating support-move
  timing/positioning around it.
- Up+Y can whiff against some grounded short-statured characters (Don Patch given as
  example), implying character height affects certain hitboxes.
- Multiple specials have explicit charge-time windups (0.5s for 4-koma specials, ~1.5s for
  6-koma Bankai specials) before executing — evidence of charge-based special-move startup
  timing distinct per koma tier.

## Combos (numeric)

All regular attacks and specials are power-type, so they deal the noted 1.5x multiplier
against Knowledge-type targets; support koma damage type is called out separately where the
guide specifies it.

Basic combos (any koma):
- **B, Side B, Y**: 36 damage total. (Up B can substitute for Side B.)
- **B, Up B, Up Y**: 40 damage total. Harder/effectively impossible to combo into a support
  afterward.
- **B aerial, Y aerial**: damage varies (not quantified).

Any koma + support:
- **B, Side B, Y, Okita 3 (support)**: Okita's hit deals 40 laughter damage; combo total
  76 damage.
- **B, Side B, Y, Inui 3 (support)**: Inui deals 18 or 26 knowledge damage depending on hit
  location; combo total 62 (or 54) damage.

4-koma combos:
- **Up B, Up Y, Up X**: 73 damage. Notable weakness: misses if there's a thin floor
  overhead; support timing is unreliable since Up Y's knockback direction is random.
- **B, Up B, Up Y, Up X**: 82 damage; requires close proximity to the opponent to connect
  the initial B.
- **B, Up B, X**: 54 damage. The only reliable way to combo into the X special from regular
  attacks; used specifically for its floor-piercing knockback rather than raw damage.
  Author speculates (unconfirmed, untested) it may be possible to drop-guard through this.
- **Up B, Up Y, Up X, Okita 3 (support)**: 113 damage total. Requires calling Okita mid Up-X
  so the target gets knocked into it at the end; Okita deals 40 laughter damage within that
  total. Difficult/luck-dependent due to Up Y's random knockback direction. A leading B can
  be added (per the earlier combo) but risks failing if not close enough.
- **Up B, Up Y, Up X, Inui 3 (support)**: 99 damage total (8 less, i.e. 91, if Inui's first
  hit misses). Inui deals 26 (or 18, depending on hit location) knowledge damage within that
  total. Harder timing than the Okita version since Inui hits from farther away.

5-koma combos:
- **B, Side B, Y, [any beam support], X**: damage varies by support chosen. Recommended
  beam supports: Arale 3 (fast, laughter damage) and Train 3 (inflicts shock status).

6-koma combos:
- **B, Side B, Y, Gotenks 3 (support), X**: 113 damage total (variable depending on how much
  of the Bankai hits). Gotenks deals 32 laughter damage within that total.
- **B, Side B, Y, Shishio 2 (support), X**: 114 damage total (also variable). Timing is
  tight — Bankai must be triggered immediately after Renji's Y finishes.

Team combo:
- **"Energy whips"** (Naruto 5-koma, Gyro 2-koma support, Renji 4-koma): Start with Naruto's
  B, Up B, Y, X (Rasengan w/ kick startup); when Rasengan connects, call Gyro 2 support and
  switch to Renji; then perform Renji's Up B, Up Y, Up X. Total: **156 damage**. Author notes
  the character-switch timing is difficult to execute consistently.

## Support/ally recommendations with numeric details

- **Inui Sadaharu 3-koma (support)**: has startup time and an unusual hitbox; deals **26
  damage**, drains **2 SP bars** from the opponent, and applies a "support seal" status
  effect.
- **Kanda 2, Yusuke 2, Allen 3, Anna 3, Sanosuke 3 (supports)**: all apply guard-break (no
  damage numbers given for these specifically in this guide).
- Other supports referenced without numeric data: Josuke 2, Satsuki 2 (small healers),
  Orihime 3 (heals whole deck), Shishio 2, Okita 3, Gyro Zeppeli 2, Byakuya 2 (inflicts
  immobilize; can set up guard breaks from Renji's Bankai if the target is backed against
  something).
- Help koma effects mentioned generically (no numbers): triple jump (Eve, Devil Bat), reduce
  special-attack damage taken (Sven, Sasuke, Seiya), passive HP regen (Orochimaru, Kazuki
  Mutou), increased guard strength (Tatsuki, Miranda).

Ally-boosting battle characters mentioned (no explicit J-Soul/HP bonus numbers given, unlike
the flat-value systems seen in other guides): Bankai Ichigo (power, boosts Renji), Rukia
(any koma, gets ally boosts herself).
