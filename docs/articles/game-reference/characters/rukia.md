## General Mechanic Notes

- Damage numbers in parentheses `(X)` throughout this guide represent the 50% nature-advantage damage boost when hitting a weaker nature (i.e., base damage vs. boosted damage, consistent with a 1.5x multiplier).
- Numbers separated by `;` on a charge move represent no-charge vs full-charge damage (format: `no-charge-range(boosted); full-charge-range(boosted)`).
- Bleach characters (as a class) can Dash straight through an enemy character without being stopped/interrupted — a game-wide quirk noted specific to this character family.

## Help Koma (1 Koma)

- Equip Effect: Immunity to Freeze status effect.

## Support Koma

### 2 Koma — "Kaizoukonpaku-Kon" (Modsoul Kon)
- Nature: Knowledge
- Damage: 20 (30 boosted)
- Description: Rukia and Kon appear; after a ~0.5s wait, Rukia kicks Kon forward at Speed-Boost run speed for roughly 3/4 of the stage length. Kon stops and kicks the first battle character he contacts, dealing damage and knocking them down.

### 3 Koma — "Gikongan-Chappi" (Righteous Soul Pill-Chappy)
- Nature: Knowledge
- Damage: 0
- Description: Rukia appears, stands for ~0.5s, then grabs the enemy in front of her and leaps roughly half a screen's distance away, not releasing until she lands — noted as a strong ring-out tool.

## Battle Koma — Shared/Universal Attacks

(Applies across her Battle Koma forms; format is base(boosted).)

- B: 7 (10)
- Up+B: 7 (10)
- Down+B: 7 (10)
- Left/Right+B: 8 (12)
- Midair B: 8 (12)
- Y: 10 (15)
- Up+Y: 3-21 (4-31) no charge; 6-36 (9-54) full charge
- Down+Y: 14 (21)
- Left/Right+Y: 3-21 (4-31) no charge; 6-36 (9-54) full charge
- Midair Y: 3-21 (4-31) no charge; 6-36 (9-54) full charge
- Select (Ultimate Action, universal-listed version): breathes out, causes one hit but 0 damage, recovers 1/5 of a full SP bar.

## Battle Koma Passive Info

- Ability "Sodenoshirayuki" (Sleeve of White Snow, passive): enables Air Dash, grants immunity to Freeze effect, and recovers SP when attacking with a Support Koma.
- Compatible Koma for Health Boost (Ally Boost providers): Abarai Renji, Kuchiki Byakuya, Kon.
- Ultimate Action (Select in battle): Rukia breathes out; counts as a hit but deals 0 damage; recovers 1/5 of a full SP bar.

## Battle Koma 4 — Shape: `[]` / `[][]` / `[]` (4 cells, cross-ish)

- J-Soul: 128
- Nature: Knowledge

### Special Attack A — "Hadounosanjuusan-Soukatsui" (Destructive Teachings 31-Falling Pale Fire)
- Damage: 25 (37 boosted)
- Nature: Knowledge
- Startup: ~1/3 second
- Description: fires a large forward blast, deals damage, knocks target away and down. Range: roughly half-screen; needs only half the enemy's body onscreen to connect.
- Author evaluation (unconfirmed): practical, fast, big hitbox (can hit up to a platform just above head height), but largely superseded by 5-Koma's Up+X which is described as faster, more damaging, with better knockback.

### Special Attack B — "Sodenoshirayuki" (Sleeve of White Ice)
- Damage: 5-20 (7-30 boosted)
- Nature: Knowledge
- Effect: Freeze, duration ~1 second
- Startup: ~3/4 second before the attack executes
- Description: Rukia's Shinigami outfit appears, she draws her sword and slices sideways, spreading ice across the floor roughly 2/3 of a screen outward, freezing anything on the ground in that area; ice can spread onto other platforms at the same height/within range.
- Author evaluation (unconfirmed): long startup and low damage make it impractical as a combo piece; suggested setup pairs it with Zura & Elizabeth (3 Koma Support, Gintama) to launch opponents into the ice.

## Battle Koma 5 — Shape: `[][][]` / `[][]` (5 cells)

- J-Soul: 144
- Nature: Knowledge

### Special Attack A — "Hadounosanjuusan-Soukatsui" (Way of Destruction 31-Falling Pale Fire)
- Damage: 32 (48 boosted)
- Nature: Knowledge
- Description: Same base move as 4 Koma's, but Rukia jumps into the air when firing (allowing an Air Dash / jump-away / follow-up attack / drop afterward). It can also be charged: charging doesn't increase damage (unlike her other charge attacks) — instead she leaps forward until contacting an enemy, the ground/platform, or until release, at which point she auto-fires.
- Author evaluation (unconfirmed): considered her primary/most practical move — fast, higher damage than 4-Koma version, strong ring-out capability, though it leaves her airborne (open) afterward.

### Special Attack B — "Sodenoshirayuki" (Sleeve of White Ice)
- Damage: 8-24 (12-36 boosted)
- Nature: Knowledge
- Effect: Freeze, duration ~1 second
- Description: same as the 4-Koma version but more damage and can reach across the full screen.
- Author evaluation (unconfirmed): only slightly more practical due to increased range; standard X recommended over this move in most cases.

## Battle Koma 6 — Shape: `[][][]` / `[][][]` (6 cells)

- J-Soul: 160
- Nature: Knowledge

### Special Attack A — "Bakudouichi-Sai" (Way of Binding One-Obstruction)
- Damage: 0
- Nature: Knowledge
- Effect: Paralysis, duration ~2 seconds
- Description: Rukia dashes forward quickly and paralyzes whoever is in front of her. No damage.
- Author evaluation (unconfirmed): strong ring-out tool despite dealing no damage — a single use doesn't push far, but the dash+knockback distance is described as being tuned for repeat use (chain it repeatedly to push an opponent toward the edge). Noted synergy: activating 3 Koma Hitsugaya (Support) immediately followed by this move (before Hitsugaya's own attack lands) sends the opponent a long distance while staying close enough to dash in before the freeze wears off. Author claims combining this with enough SP-recovery-rate Help Koma enables a "semi-infinite" loop of (Forward+)B, Y, X with brief movement between reps (author unconfirmed).

### Special Attack B — "Sodenoshirayuki-Somenomai [Tsukishiro]" (Sleeve of White Ice-First Dance [White Moon])
- Damage: 3-15 (5-22) for one hit component, 4-16 (6-24) for a second hit component
- Nature: Knowledge
- Effect: Freeze, duration ~1 second
- Description: same range as 5 Koma version, but the floor ice itself no longer freezes — instead a follow-up upward ice blast does the freezing and can knock enemies upward.
- Author evaluation (unconfirmed): prettier but less reliable — after the initial floor ice, opponents can recover and block before the follow-up blast lands unless something else is holding them in hitstun; blast timing is strict but can catch falling enemies or those on a platform above Rukia.

## Deck-Building Notes (numeric items only)

- Ryotsu (Support recommendation, Battle Koma partner): 160 HP at 4 Koma (stated as one of the highest for a 4-Koma character); one of two characters noted to be able to receive a "double health boost" from a specific Help Koma (character/koma unnamed in source). His Up+X heals "a nice chunk" of his own health (no exact number given).
- Kinnikuman (7 Koma partner recommendation): standard X move heals himself by roughly 50% and heals all other Battle Koma allies by roughly 20% (author unconfirmed / approximate figures as stated).
- Renji (4/5/6 Koma partner): described as having "brilliant combo capabilities" and mutual Ally Boost synergy with Rukia (both boost each other); HP said to be "higher than most 4 Koma" but no exact number given.

## Using Rukia — Mechanic Notes

- Her 5-Koma Special A knockback sends opponents flying downward at an angle and covers a large area, making it a strong ring-out tool.
- Left/Right+Y suggested as an alternate combo-ender when SP is unavailable for the Special.
