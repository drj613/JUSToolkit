# Sakura Haruno (Naruto) — Character Guide Notes

- Source: "Sakura Haruno Character FAQ", by SMW (Carl Godon), version 2.1
- GameFAQs FAQ id: gf-46574
- Unverified community source — numbers are from a fan FAQ, not verified against game code.

## Evolution chart

Evolution tree: 1 -> 2 -> 3, and 1 -> 4 -> 5 -> 6a, with 5 -> 6b (alt path via Sai side-mission).

## Koma forms

- Help Koma: reduces duration of some status effects on Sakura.
- Support Koma 2 (shape: 2 horizontal squares): summons Sakura in front, punches forward. Needs some distance from enemy; long setup, medium-short range. Power-based. Damage: 28 to Power/Laughter types, 42 to Knowledge types.
- Support Koma 3 (shape: backwards-L, 2 top + 1 bottom-right): summons Sakura behind user, creates a blue orb that lingers ~1 second; first person to touch it is healed (auto-heals the user if they don't move).

## Battle Koma stats

- 4 Battle Koma total: three Power-type, one Laughter-type (the 6-Koma alt/6b form).
- HP values reported: 128, 144, 160 (author states these are among the lowest HP totals in the game, alongside Freiza, Rukia, Nami, Robin, Lenalee, Eve).
- Passive abilities: reduced status effect duration; can Wall Jump.
- Damage notation convention used throughout: "first/second" = base damage vs. Power&Laughter / vs. Knowledge (super-effective), EXCEPT for the alternate 6-Koma path where the first number is vs. Knowledge&Laughter and the second is vs. Power (because that koma is Laughter type).

- Y note: pushes Sakura forward on execution — can knock her off a ledge if used too close to the edge.
- Air Y note: still deals damage if only the ground-impact dust cloud hits; missing the ground on landing results in death (falling KO).

### Ultimate Action (all Battle Koma levels)

Substitution Jutsu: takes 1 second to activate; Sakura is open to attack during activation. Once active, a log replaces her if hit, lasting 2 seconds. If caught in a multi-hit attack, the first hit(s) trigger the substitution but subsequent hits in the same multi-hit can still land if not dodged. Holding a D-pad direction when hit determines which direction Sakura reappears. The UA can be re-triggered while the first substitution is still active, allowing repeated activation (effectively continuous invincibility while pressing Select). Author tip: pressing Up when hit while substitution is active + attacking with Y repositions Sakura above the enemy for a downward attack that can send them through platforms.

## Specials (X)

### 4-Koma
- X: Power type. Punches the ground; explosion also deals damage. Damage: 36/54. Breaks wall in 1 hit. Breaks guard in 2/2 hits. Delay: End.
- X Up: Power type. Creates a pink orb, dashes forward a few feet; 3-hit combo. Damage: 23/34. Inflicts Confusion. Breaks wall in 3 hits. Breaks guard in 3/4 hits. Delay: Beginning/slightly at end.

### 5-Koma
- X: Power type. Jumps and does a giant kick; explosion deals damage. Can cause self-KO (falling off/off-screen risk, "can commit Suicide" per author). Damage: 44/66. Breaks wall in 2 hits. Breaks guard in 3/2 hits. Delay: during jump.
- X Up: Knowledge type. Kneels, makes a blue orb. Effect: heals about half an SP/HP bar (ambiguous in source, likely HP). Delay: beginning.

### 6-Koma (both 6a and 6b share the same specials)
- X: Laughter type. Summons "Inner Sakura" who punches high and low; hits regardless of whether enemy is airborne or grounded. Tapping X repeatedly increases hit count and damage. Damage: 38-57 base / 57-98 effective. Breaks 2 walls (data from base/untapped X). Breaks guard in 3/3 hits (base X). Delay: End.
- X Up: Knowledge type. Makes a blue orb. Effect: heals about a quarter of an ally's HP bar. Delay: beginning.

## Ally Boost

- Sakura receives Ally Boost from: Naruto, Kakashi, Tsunade. Each contributing character adds +8 HP; if the boosting character is the deck leader, add an additional +8 HP (total +16 for leader boost per source, per character).
- Reported HP progression with boosts:
  - 4-Koma: 128 -> 136 -> 144 -> 152 -> 160
  - 5-Koma: 144 -> 152 -> 160 -> 168 -> 176
  - 6-Koma: 160 -> 168 -> 176 -> 184 -> 192
- Sakura in turn gives an Ally Boost (+8 HP) to: Naruto, Naruto Kyuubi, Kakashi.

## Mechanic-revealing notes

- Y Up is emphasized as combo-starter because of near-instant startup.
- Basic listed combos (no damage numbers given): Y Up -> B; Y Up -> Air B -> Air Y (requires opponent hit into a wall); Y Up -> Y; B -> Y Up -> [any of above]; B Down -> Y; B Down -> Up Y -> [above]; B Up -> Y Forward; B Up -> Y -> Y Forward; B Up -> Y -> Y Up -> Air Y.
- Guard-break framing: Y Down breaks guard in just 1/1 hits (fastest of her listed guard-break values), consistent with it being called a strong ranged breaker in the strategy text.
- Air attacks (Air B, Air Y) noted as effectively unblockable since guarding midair is stated to be nearly impossible.
- Author's Koma recommendation for numeric reasons: 5-Koma is the only one with a self-heal (X Up, ~half bar); 6-Koma's X Up heals only ~quarter bar but to an ally instead of self, and X deals the highest raw damage (38-57/57-98) plus a type-advantage combo (Power base type + Laughter special).
