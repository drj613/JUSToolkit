## General mechanics notes from this guide (not Sanji-specific, but numeric/mechanical)

- Deck is a 20-koma block, 4 (vertical) x 5 (horizontal) page.
- Koma affinity triangle: Power beats Knowledge, Knowledge beats Laughter, Laughter beats Power (cyclic). Damage to a weak-to type is stated here as **1.5x** the base damage (rounded down for Knowledge-type targets, per this guide's convention — differs in exact wording from other guides that give flat added-damage numbers, treat as author's own phrasing/(author unconfirmed exact rounding rule)).
- Help koma (1-koma) with no arrow: either does nothing or raises SP gauge by 1.
- Support koma (2-3 koma): cost 1 SP bar to summon; can be destroyed before their effect triggers, wasting the bar.
- Special attacks (X) cost 1 SP bar each.

## Koma types

- All of Sanji's koma are Power-type EXCEPT the Alternate 5-koma, which is Laughter-type.
- Damage tables below are given per this guide as "Damage to Power/Laughter" with "multiply by 1.5 (round down)" for Knowledge-type targets — matches the general 1.5x weakness rule above.

## Ally boost characters (adds boost if adjacent to Sanji)

- Nami (One Piece): increases special gauge when breaking item boxes.
- Zoro (One Piece): decreases damage taken from swords.
- Bianchi (Katekyo Hitman Reborn!): gain more health from food.

## Support/Help koma versions

- 1-koma (Help): "Gain more Health from Food" passive.
- 2-koma (Support): Sanji appears holding out food; heals any character that touches him (including enemies); heals a small amount of HP; heals girl characters more (no exact numbers given).
- 3-koma (Support, Power-type): Sanji dashes forward doing a 4-hit combo.
  - Hit 1: 7 dmg (Power/Laughter) / 10 dmg (Knowledge)
  - Hit 2: 7 / 11
  - Hit 3: 7 / 10
  - Hit 4: 18 / 27
  - Total: 39 / 58

## Mechanic-revealing observations

- B can cancel into Y or Forward B; Y can cancel into Forward Y (chain/cancel rules explicitly stated).
- Forward B's shoe becomes an actual projectile with hit/interaction behavior: if blocked with a support koma it may be destroyed; if it hits, target is stunned briefly; if the target jumps to avoid it, they become airborne and vulnerable until landing.
- Up X (4-koma) damage scales with height/distance: more hits the higher above ground / closer the opponent, capped by a fixed 10-damage ground-impact hit; can total 50+ damage from a 4-koma move if sustained fully airborne.
- Up X (5-koma/Alt 5-koma) has a hard timeout of about 3 seconds if it doesn't connect with an unguarded opponent, and cannot apply the ringout-style knockback to female-type targets (Mellorine branch instead of damage branch).
- 6-koma's Mellorine status (from Up X) reportedly only lasts about 10 seconds per author testing (author unconfirmed precise duration — described as "nerfed" compared to the original Jump Super Stars version).
- The guide explicitly states Forward Y has significant recovery/lag, making it punishable unless it follows a connected Y hit.
