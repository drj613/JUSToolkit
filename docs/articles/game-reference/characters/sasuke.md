## Shared/embedded abilities (all 3 Sasuke battle koma)

- Can wall jump
- Can see invisible opponents
- Takes less damage from special attacks
- Ally-boosted by: Naruto, Orochimaru, Ayame (Gintama)
- Ally-boosts: Frieza (DBZ)
- Ultimate Action (Select): Replacement jutsu — hand seal, lasts 3-4 seconds; if hit during that window, Sasuke is replaced by a log (unharmed) and reappears with a half-second (0.5s) invincibility window.

- Special A (Normal X) "Chidori": Neutral dmg 40, Dominant dmg 60 (Dominant against Laughter). Hits: 3. Special effect: hold X to charge, causes Shock status on hit. Base charge = moves ~half screen forward before striking; full charge = moves full screen forward. Rotating the D-pad while charging lengthens charge time but caps movement distance at half a screen. Charging does not increase damage, only distance. Cannot stop once executed until full travel distance is covered (risk near edges).
- Special B (Up+X) "Koukana Kisote": Neutral dmg 44, Dominant dmg 66 (Dominant against Laughter). Hits: 5. Effects: guard break + burn status. Sequence: launches opponent up (breaking guard) with a lightning effect, follows into air for 3 more hits, finishes with a fire jutsu inflicting burn. Author notes: full damage not guaranteed if a platform is above — opponent can fall out of the combo onto it before the move finishes.

Both 8-koma versions share these specials:

- Special A (Normal X) "Chidori": dmg 48, Dominant dmg 72 (Dominant against Laughter). Hits: 3. Same charge mechanic as 7-koma Chidori, but adds directional control — hold a direction during the charge/dash and Sasuke redirects the attack that way (7-koma Chidori cannot redirect).
- Special B (Normal X) "Chidori Nagashi": dmg 40, Dominant dmg 60 (Dominant against Laughter). Hits: 7. Effect: omnidirectional AoE around Sasuke, roughly half a screen wide, causes Shock status. Notes: effective as edge-guard — covers the ledge-grab area; used twice in a row is weak because the first hit knocks the opponent out of range of the second.

Author's note on 8-koma Power version: takes extra damage from Laughter characters via nature weakness, but since Sasuke's specials are all Knowledge-nature offensively, author argues Sasuke has "no real nature weakness" in practice.

## Support-based combos (character/koma names only, numeric where given)

- "Burning Chidori" (3-koma Trunks, Shishio, or Kenshin): basic 8-hit combo → summon support near the last hit → hold Chidori charge → release Chidori exactly as support's hit lands. Author states total damage "around a good 121" (author unconfirmed precise figure — described as approximate).
- Guard Breaker combo (3-koma Anna or Sanosuke): summon support during Forward+Y while holding X charge; release Chidori exactly as support punches a guarding opponent. Sanosuke variant: same effect, no slowdown status, but +10 extra damage vs. Anna (author unconfirmed exact base value, stated as relative "ten extra damage").
- "Sasuke Naruto Rendan" (3-koma Naruto): timing-based combo into Chidori/Chidori Nagashi right before opponent lands; grants ally boost.
- "Infinite Combo" (3-koma Ayame Sarutobi + Ichigo/Ishida help koma effects): basic combo, insert Ayame between Y and Y+Forward to freeze opponent, then repeat.
- "Stab Lag" (2-koma Aya): covers the recovery lag after aerial Y (14 damage, stated as "decent") by baiting opponent with Aya's support-seal.

## Deck-building notes (non-numeric, character/koma references)

- Recommended help koma: Ichigo/Ishida (SP-related), Orochimaru (ally boost + health regen), Rohan Kishibe (immune to support/attack seals), Sakura (halves status-effect duration).
- Recommended support koma: Naruto 3-koma, Ayame Sarutobi 3-koma, Trunks/Kenshin/Shishio 3-koma, Anna 3-koma, Sanosuke 3-koma, Seiya 3-koma, Luffy 3-koma, Aya 2-koma, Sakura 3-koma, Nami 2-koma.
- Recommended battle koma partners: Ichigo 7-koma, Sakura 6-koma (Vertical, Laughter), Frieza 6-koma, Train 5-koma (power version recommended).
