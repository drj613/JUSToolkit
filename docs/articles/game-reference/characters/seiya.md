## Evolution

- Evolution chart: H -> S2 -> S3 (Support branch), and H -> B4 -> B5 -> B6 -> B7 -> B8 (Battle branch). B8 additionally requires unlocking "Athena."

## Basic Moveset

No specific damage numbers given for basic attacks in this guide — descriptions only.

| Input | Description |
|---|---|
| B | Forward punch, fast, good combo starter |
| B + right/left | Steps forward, energy-wrapped punch |
| B + up | Uppercut; launches enemy a short distance into the air |
| B + down | Burst of energy around Seiya; force-changes opponent's Battle Character on hit |
| B (air) | Downward chop; spike attack |
| Y | Steps forward releasing energy bullets from fist; tapping Y yields more hits, **up to 10 hits maximum**. Good for edge-guarding and combo finishing |
| Y + right/left | Steps forward firing one large energy bullet, travels a short distance |
| Y + up | Jumps releasing energy bullets; tapping Y yields more hits, **up to 13 hits maximum**. Hits opponents above |
| Y + down | Punches the ground; guard break |
| Y (air) | 45-degree downward kick, "decent damage," fairly fast |
| Select (Ultimate Action) | Glowing blue pose; slowly recharges SP; no rate given |

## Help / Support Koma

- Help Koma: attached character takes reduced damage from special attacks (no percentage given).
- Support 2 (Shield of Athena): heals attached/target Battle Character a small amount and removes status effects.
- Support 3 (Pegasus Ryu Sei Ken): Seiya appears above the target and fires a barrage of energy bullets downward. Noted as commonly used to break combos/chains.

## Mechanic-revealing observations

- Support combo example: 7-Koma Seiya's special + Shishio Support Koma 2 — the explosion at the end of Seiya's special launches the opponent, and Shishio's Support then applies a burn status effect. Author states this combo works with any of Seiya's Koma sizes, but total damage output scales down with smaller Koma (no specific numbers given).
- 7-Koma Special 2's activation dash is stated to block frontal attacks during its dash startup — an armor/parry-like property tied to move startup.
- Guard break (Y+down) and force-switch (B+down) are both described as having "semi wide areas of effect," hitting around Seiya including behind him.

## Strategy notes (non-numeric, largely skipped per scope)

- Played as mid-range; strong edge-guard via Y+left/right, but this only connects if the opponent isn't launched too high (they can arc over Seiya's head back onto the stage otherwise).
- Vulnerable to ranged zoning characters since he needs to close distance.
