## Koma forms / evolution chart

Layout of the Taikoubou koma tree (as drawn in the guide, ASCII diagram):

- 1H (Help, 1 panel) -> 4B (Battle, 4 panels) -> 5B (Battle, 5 panels) -> 6BH (Battle, 6 panels, Horizontal)
- 1H also branches to 2S (Support, 2 panels) -> 3S (Support, 3 panels)
- 4B also branches to UP (Upgrade koma)
- 5B also branches to 6BV (Battle, 6 panels, Vertical)

Dakki koma tree: 1H -> 2S -> 3S -> JQ (J-Quiz unlock)

Outenkun koma tree: 1H -> J-Glxy (J-Galaxy unlock)

Suupuusan koma tree: 1H -> UP -> PORT (Character Portrait — unlocks Info portrait showing
Sipuxiang, and unlocks Houshin Engi manga info in Data Mode) -> CU (Character Upgrade)

No explicit J-Soul/HP or size/nature values are given for individual koma panels in this guide
(only panel counts).

## Full moveset

Damage is always given as two rows: "as knowledge based" (dealt by a knowledge-natured
Taikoubou) and "as laughter based" (dealt by a laughter-natured Taikoubou), each broken out
per target komaman nature (power/knowledge/laughter). Pattern is consistent throughout: a
knowledge-based attacker's stated {power, knowledge, laughter} damage numbers are echoed for
a laughter-based attacker but with the power/laughter figures swapped (i.e. laughter attacks
hit power targets harder and laughter targets normally — a knowledge/laughter type-advantage
system is implied; power-based numbers are never separately listed, "author unconfirmed"
whether a third profile exists).

### B-button variations (4-panel koma and up)

1. **B** — Weak Attack ("chi" attack, quick Paopei swing). Damage (knowledge-based
   attacker): 7 / 7 / 10 to power/knowledge/laughter komaman. Laughter-based: 10 / 7 / 7.
2. **Up+B** — Weak, upward attack; Taikoubou wildly swings Paopei, 5-hit combo. Damage
   (knowledge-based, total across the 5 hits): 6 / 6 / 9. Laughter-based: 9 / 6 / 6.
3. **Down+B** — Short-ranged attack; summons "apprentice" Bukichi who runs across the
   battlefield. On hit at close range, deals damage AND forces the target to switch battle
   characters. Damage (knowledge-based): 8 / 8 / 12. Laughter-based: 12 / 8 / 8.
4. **Forward+B** — Long-ranged attack; Taikoubou swings Paopei and releases an energy wave
   (projectile). Damage (knowledge-based): 7 / 7 / 10. Laughter-based: 10 / 7 / 7.
5. **Jump+B (A+B)** — Short-ranged aerial attack; rides Suupuusan to charge head-on. Damage
   (knowledge-based): 7 / 7 / 10. Laughter-based: 10 / 7 / 7.

### Y-button variations

1. **Y** — Strong attack; calls Kou Hiko to swing a two-handed sword. Damage
   (knowledge-based): 19 / 19 / 28. Laughter-based: 28 / 19 / 19.
2. **Up+Y** — Strong, upward attack; ally Youzen flies up and swings a trident Paopei.
   Damage (knowledge-based): 16 / 16 / 24. Laughter-based: 24 / 16 / 16.
3. **Down+Y** — Concentrated, short-range attack; Fugen makes a small energy explosion.
   Has a knockback effect on items and enemies, and can break guard (author-credited to †
   Genroh). Damage (knowledge-based): 14 / 14 / 21. Laughter-based: 21 / 14 / 14.
4. **Forward+Y** — Strong, long-ranged attack; Kou Tenka (Kou Hiko's son) rushes forward,
   slashes with a "light-saber" Paopei. Damage (knowledge-based): 14 / 14 / 21. Laughter-based:
   21 / 14 / 14.
5. **Jump+Y (A+Y)** — Strong, ranged, homing attack; Nataku ("Human Paopei") shoots metal
   wrist-bands at the closest enemy. Hits twice (two separate projectiles). Damage
   (knowledge-based, total across both hits): 16 / 16 / 24. Laughter-based: 24 / 16 / 16.

### X-button variations (Specials/Ultimate Actions — spoiler-tagged in source)

4-panel koma:
1. **X** — Stronger version of Forward-B. Damage (knowledge-based, total): 26 / 26 / 39.
2. **Up+X** — Vertical whirlwind that slowly advances forward; damage scales with charge
   time. Damage range (knowledge-based, min charge to max charge, averaged): power 10–30,
   knowledge 14–30, laughter 18–45. Note: author states these are averaged numbers gathered
   across a range of charge levels, not fixed min/max endpoints for each target type.

5-panel koma:
1. **X** — More powerful horizontal whirlwind. Damage (knowledge-based, total): 38 / 38 / 57.
2. **Up+X** — Six miniature whirlwinds thrown at the enemy. Damage (knowledge-based, total):
   36 / 36 / 54.

6-panel koma (both Horizontal and Vertical trees):
1. **X** — Taikoubou transforms into Fukki, barrages with a flurry of Paopei strikes, flies
   upward, then slams the enemy down (ring-out potential). Per † Genroh, this special's power
   scales up with the number of characters in the player's deck. Damage (knowledge-based,
   total): 54 / 54 / 81, with a stated max of 68 / 68 / 102 (per †, presumably at max deck
   size — exact scaling formula not given, author unconfirmed).
2. **Up+X** — Transforms into Fukki again and spreads poison onto the arena (no damage
   numbers given for this poison field).

### Other inputs / movement mechanics

- **Down+Jump or Down+Down**: fast-fall through thin platforms, or faster falling in air.
  Taikoubou cannot jump again after using this fast-fall.
- **Forward+Forward**: dash forward; can be used repeatedly near an enemy to "dash through"
  them.
- **Holding Down**: blocking stance.
- **Select**: taunt (Taikoubou sits and fishes with his Paopei — cosmetic only).
- **L & R buttons**: trigger assigned support/battle komas; can be used to execute a "dream
  combo."
- **Touch screen**: tapping a battle koma triggers a quick attack (also usable for dream
  combos); tapping a support koma activates that support character.

## Help komas (passive Help panel effects)

- **Taikoubou (Help)**: Increases SP gauge when attacking with or blocking with a Support
  Character.
- **Dakki (Help)**: Increases SP gauge when using multiple Special Attacks in a short time.
- **Outenkun (Help)**: Grants immunity from Poison.
- **Suupuusan (Help)**: Grants Triple Jump.

## Support koma versions

### Taikoubou support
1. **2-panel support**: Cures the current battle character of status ailments (poison,
   confusion, Speed-Down, etc.). No numeric duration/cost given.
2. **3-panel support**: Revives fallen battle characters in the player's deck. No numeric
   HP-restored value given.

### Dakki support
1. **2-panel support**: Summons Dakki for two quick fan slashes. Deals 24 damage total, hits
   up to 2 times, and knocks back anyone caught in the attack.
2. **3-panel support**: Dakki performs a "battle seal." Drains 1 SP bar (cost, per †). No
   damage number given for this move — described as a seal/status effect rather than direct
   damage.

## Ally boost data

Taikoubou receives ally boosts from:
1. Dakki (Houshin Engi)
2. Suupuusan (Houshin Engi)
3. Raamenman / Mongoruman (Kinnikuman)

Boost mechanic rules described in the guide:
- Ally-boost-compatible characters (as Help, Support, or Battle komas) must be placed
  adjacent to the battle character to be boosted; a sparkle appears on the boosted character,
  and a one-time dialogue line plays the first time the boost triggers.
- Each qualifying adjacent ally grants **+8 HP** to the boosted battle character, regardless
  of panel count (a 1-panel and 3-panel Dakki both give the same +8 HP).
- Boosts stack: with all 3 compatible allies adjacent, total bonus is **+24 HP**.
- Making the boosted character the deck leader adds a further **+8 HP** on top of ally boosts.
- Support-koma boosts also grant up to **+1 SP** each; stacking multiple compatible support
  komas can give up to **+3 SP** total. Author notes not all Help komas grant the SP bonus —
  only "a few" do, "at the expense of not having any other special trait" (author
  unconfirmed which specific komas beyond what's listed).

## Combo data (Character Use Advice section, credited to ‡ ChocoboMog123 unless noted)

Combo starters (damage as knowledge-based / laughter-based, power-knowledge-laughter target
order), all stated as summed hit totals rather than measured combo-scaled output:

- **B, Up+B**: knowledge-based 13 / 13 / 19; laughter-based 19 / 13 / 13.
- **B, Forward+B**: knowledge-based 16 / 16 / 21; laughter-based 21 / 14 / 14 (note: source
  table is asymmetric here — knowledge-based lists 16/16/21 but laughter-based lists
  21/14/14, not the usual clean swap; reproduced as printed, possible source typo).
- **B** (alone, restated): knowledge-based 7 / 7 / 10; laughter-based 10 / 7 / 7.

From B, Up+B chain:
- **X or Up+X** works but does not register on the combo counter. (Damage not given —
  "posted later" in source, never filled in.)
- **Forward+Y** (Y, Forward+Y) works and DOES register on the combo counter, but must be
  spaced. Knowledge-based total: 46 / 46 / 68. Laughter-based: 68 / 46 / 46. Author notes
  this combo is very hard to perform, and the numbers are individual hit damages summed, not
  measured combo output (i.e. does not account for potential damage scaling/DI).
- **Up+Y** works but not on combo counter. Knowledge-based: 16 / 16 / 24. Laughter-based:
  24 / 16 / 16.

From B, Forward+B chain:
- **Forward+Y** works, not on combo counter. Knowledge-based total: 47 / 47 / 69.
  Laughter-based: 69 / 47 / 47.

From B (alone) chain:
- **Forward+Y** works; the Forward+Y portion itself is not on the combo counter.
  Knowledge-based total: 40 / 40 / 59. Laughter-based printed as 24 / 40 / 59 (source
  inconsistency — reproduced as printed; likely a typo for 59/40/40 given the pattern
  elsewhere, author unconfirmed).
- **Up+X** works (damage "posted later," never filled in).

Corner/infinite note: author states an Up+X infinite may be possible in a stage with a full
upper-right corner; combo counter reached as high as 73 hits in testing, true max unknown
(author unconfirmed).

5-koma chains:
- **B, Up+B, Up+X**: combo works, damage not given.
- **B, Up+B, Up+Y, Up+X**: works, not on combo counter, damage not given.
- **B, Up+B, (Up+Y optional), X**: works, not on combo counter. Knowledge-based total:
  67 / 67 / 100. Author notes 100 is a summed-hits figure (including the optional Up+Y), not
  a true combo-scaled number, and the combo is hard to perform.

6-koma chains:
- **B, Up+B, X**: "seems to work, probably not though" (author uncertain it's a true combo).
  Knowledge-based total: 67 / 67 / 100. Laughter-based: 73 / 73 / 94. Author notes this
  string is not a true combo but "still works" in some sense.
- **B, Up+B, Up+Y, Up+X**: hits and applies a **Poison Effect**. Knowledge-based total:
  29 / 29 / 43 + Poison. Laughter-based: 43 / 29 / 29 + Poison. (Contributed by
  ChocoboMog123.)

## Mechanic-revealing observations

- Down+B (Bukichi) causing a forced "switch battle characters" on hit is a distinct status
  effect separate from raw damage — suggests a forced-tag/switch mechanic exists in the game.
- Down+Y (Fugen) is explicitly called out as guard-breaking and having knockback on both
  items and enemies — evidence of a guard-break flag on specific moves.
- Up+X (whirlwind, 4-panel) has charge-based damage scaling (10–30 / 14–30 / 18–45 depending
  on charge duration), evidence of a charge-attack mechanic with continuous or tiered scaling.
- The 6-panel X ultimate ("Fukki") is stated to scale in power with total deck character
  count — a "more characters in your deck = stronger special" mechanic (54→68 total damage
  example given, exact formula not specified).
- Multiple combo notes distinguish moves that register on the "combo counter" from moves that
  connect but don't count toward it, implying separate hit-connection vs. combo-counter logic
  internally.
