## General Mechanic Notes (glossary entries with mechanic-revealing content)

- **Nature triangle**: Power deals 1.5x damage to Knowledge, Knowledge deals 1.5x damage to Laughter, Laughter deals 1.5x damage to Power. (This is the clearest explicit statement of the game's damage-type-multiplier system found across the assigned guides — 1.5x, not the 2x sometimes assumed.)
- **Auto-guard** (innate or via help koma): automatically blocks any attack while in neutral position, at a cost of roughly **half an SP bar per blocked attack** (author unconfirmed precision, stated as "roughly a half bar").
- **Hit stun**: after being hit, there is a period of stun preventing movement/attack/block; all non-aerial combos are built around this stun window.
- **Push block**: pressing X on a blocked hit pushes the opponent away, interrupting their pressure.
- **Tap blocking**: tapping (rather than holding) guard lets guard meter regenerate between hits, allowing far more total blocking than holding guard.
- **Warp dash**: passes through other characters without slowing and has invincibility frames; only certain characters have it (Tsuna does not, per the "closing in" section, which says Tsuna "can't even close in fast with double airdashes" and needs help from partner characters with good dashes).

## Koma Forms

- **Help koma**: any attack dealt to or received by a support grants SP to the player. Considered weaker than SP-on-hit helps for battle-character attacks; largely redundant if using Tsuna's battle koma.
- **Support koma** (2 total):
  - **2 koma**: Reborn appears in a spiky costume and rolls around; attack is not instant (has startup); decent for combo breaking and pressure; deals **laughter** damage.
  - **3 koma**: Reborn appears and goes to bed with grenades around him; low damage, slow startup; author rates it "not a good support, avoid"; deals **power** damage.
- **Battle koma**: 3 koma forms plus one alternate. Tsuna is normally a **Laughter** nature character; his alternate is **Power** nature. All of Tsuna's specials deal **Laughter** damage regardless of which koma/alternate is active.
- Innate abilities: immune to **blindness** status; has **auto-guard**; takes reduced damage from Specials (no exact percentage given).
- Ally boosts confirmed for Tsuna's deck: Kinnikuman (reduces damage from blows/punches), Yamamoto (reduces damage from blades), Gokudera (boosts SP regained on normal-attack hits).

## Normal Attacks (no exact damage numbers given by author for normals — only qualitative range/speed/priority notes)

- **B**: fast jab, easy combo starter, cancels into almost anything, decent range, safe.
- **fB** (forward B): fast, good priority, crosses up on block at close range, can combo into Y with correct timing. Notable defensive tool: has invincibility frames "almost from start to end" — strong enough to counter Dr. Mashirito's chest projectile and beat through walls/beams the opponent thought were safe (e.g. Dio's eyebeams, Vegeta's laser blasts). After the invincibility window ends, Tsuna's hitbox stays low to the ground briefly, letting late inputs still duck under a following attack.
- **dB** (Force Change): slow, decent range, can hit characters on platforms above.
- **uB**: surprise move; on block, used to cross up (twice in one jump for a mixup); on hit, followed by another uB or a delayed Y/fY.
- **Aerial B**: recovery tool; clashes with many edge-guarding attacks; catches opponents after uB or running on platforms; becomes predictable if overused.
- **Y**: good range but slow startup; on hit, combos into Tsuna's X special regardless of koma, if opponent doesn't anticipate it; can be beaten by opponent fast-falling through it.
- **fY** ("the stumble"): Tsuna's best edge-guarding tool; can be held to produce **between 2 and 5 stumbles** before being released; high priority but easy to escape on block if opponent knows the matchup.
- **dY** (Guard Break): has range but a small hitbox; decently fast but often whiffs (opponent can jump over it).
- **uY**: throws one or two basketballs (a projectile); poor priority, destroyed by rapid multi-hit attacks.
- **Aerial Y**: less recovery lag than it visually appears, but still laggy; cannot be canceled after use (risky over gaps). Its landing lag is special-cancelable (see Tidbit #5 below).

## Specials (X = ground beam/combo attack, uX = up-special variant)

### 4 Koma
- **X**: 3-hit combo, last hit spikes downward (deadly near an edge). Fast, decent pressure, but "pretty weak": **26 damage to neutral natures**.
- **uX**: creates a splash useful in FFA/teams; otherwise weak — slow enough to be blocked/dashed/jumped away from on reaction. Stronger specifically against Power-type characters (no exact number given).

### 5 Koma
- **X**: 9-hit combo. Strong pressure tool vs. non-push-blocking opponents, especially combined with multi-hit supports near a wall. Last hit is a pseudo-spike, but earlier hits often push the opponent off-stage before the final hit can land — must be spaced so the last hit connects while sending the opponent over an edge.
- **uX**: very large range/hitbox but hard to aim since much of its reach is off-screen from the start position; strong when it lands but rarely connects. Can be used to escape corner pressure.

### 6 Koma
- **X**: "generally Tsuna's best special." Final hit spikes the opponent down — near-guaranteed KO over an edge or gap-heavy stage. If used near a wall, one of the hits misses and total damage drops noticeably (exact reduced number not given). Deals "a tremendous amount of damage" to Power characters (exact number not given, but author states it's worth spending an SP bar after landing a Y to combo into it).
- **uX**: Tsuna's most powerful special — **deals near 100 damage on Power characters**, making a decent combo into it "almost always a guaranteed KO" on Power-type opponents. Very slow startup; unimpressive range and hitbox size otherwise.

## Combo / Mechanic Observations
- **Kagura 3 (support) infinite**: Kagura's grab-and-spit attack hits **8 times** if it connects immediately, **6 hits** if she turns her head once, **4 hits** if she turns her head twice (each "turn" apparently triggered by the opponent escaping/blocking earlier hits). Tsuna's 6 Koma X should be input right as her *last* hit lands; his uX should be input right as her 7th/5th/3rd hit lands (i.e., one hit before the end of each respective hit-count variant). The sequence **B, Y, Kagura 3** is described as an *infinite combo* when spaced/timed correctly, and is also usable as a wall infinite (stumble fY then insert Kagura on roughly the 4th hit).
- **Hiei 2 koma (support)**: drains **2 SP bars** from the opponent and breaks combos; very fast, "nearly invincible."
- **Sena 3 koma (support)**: drains **2 SP bars**, breaks combos, turns opponent around, movement-seals, and stuns for "a pretty long time" — long enough to combo afterward. Author calls it "essentially the best support in the entire game."
- **Nami 2 koma (support)**: slow, but steals **2 SP bars** from the opponent and transfers them to the player.
- **Inui 3 koma (support, anti-edge-guard)**: fast waterballoon at the edge; support-seals, drains SP (amount unspecified), high knockback; can combo with Tsuna.
- Tsuna's taunt move lowers his hitbox close to the ground, allowing him to dodge some projectiles and specific moves (e.g., dodging under Jotaro's Y and dreamcombo).
- Tsuna's aerial Y landing lag is **special-cancelable** — landing lag can be canceled directly into a special move, since his specials tend to have fast startup (mechanic-revealing note about special-cancel windows).
- Warp-dash characters reportedly tend to warp to the opposite side when cornered — turning around and stumbling (fY) the other way can catch them mid-warp (author observation, situational/unconfirmed as a hard rule).

## Helps (non-numeric effect list, no exact stat values given)
- SP+ helps: adds one extra SP bar (no bar-count baseline given).
- Immunity helps: freeze and paralysis called out as the two most "direct" fatal statuses; support-seal called more indirect but still highly disruptive; author recommends Rohan (anti-support-seal) permanently on Tsuna.
- Status-duration-reduction helps: shortens most status effect durations (no numeric duration given).
- Build-SP helps: passive SP generation while attacking with the battle character; Tsuna has one help of this type that also ally-boosts him.
- Mobility helps: third jump vs. air dash; author notes Tsuna's native air dash is short/slow, so a third-jump help is recommended over an air-dash help for him specifically.

Note: this guide is almost entirely strategic/matchup prose (edge-guarding technique, support recommendations, escape habits) with sparse hard numbers. The only concrete damage/HP/SP figures found are: nature-type multiplier of **1.5x**, ~**half an SP bar** cost per auto-guard block, 4-Koma X's **26 damage to neutral natures**, 6-Koma uX's **~100 damage to Power characters**, Kagura 3's **8/6/4-hit** variants, and three separate supports draining **2 SP bars** (Hiei, Sena, Nami).
