## Character/type info
- Vegeta is primarily Power-type; his B5b koma variant is Knowledge-type instead.

## Evolution chart (koma forms)
```
       - S2 - S3
       |
H1 -  B4 - B5a - B6 - U
            |
            - B5b
```
- H (Help Koma): starting point.
- B4 (Battle 4).
- B5 (Battle 5, main path "B5a").
- B5b (alternate Battle 5 variant, Knowledge-type).
- B6 (Battle 6).
- U: unlocks Vegetto in Goku's chart.
- S2 (Support 2).
- S3 (Support 3).

## Koma effects (Help Koma / passive)
- Help Koma: increases SP gained while attacking/blocking when the user's health is low.
- All of Vegeta's battle koma have this SP-gain-when-low-health ability built in natively.

## Full moveset (base koma, no special notation for target type differences given for base moves)
- **B**: 10 damage. Basic jab; parrying/interrupt attack and combo starter.
- **Forward B**: 10 damage. Dash punch; heavy lag if guarded.
- **Down B**: 6 damage. Downward two-hand hammer strike; character-changes opponent. Fast startup.
- **Up B**: 9 damage. Upward kick, sends opponent forward slightly. Doesn't combo well into his other moves directly but allows an extended combo if the player dashes immediately after (author gives example string: B, Up B, dash, Forward B, then continue normal combo).
- **Aerial B**: 9 damage. "Knee" attack, sends opponent upward.
- **Y**: 18 damage total (two kicks that knock opponent down). Good support-comboer due to long active duration; risky if missed.
- **Forward Y**: 3 damage per blast, 6 blasts total (several Ki blasts, mash Y for more blasts). Chains from Y against a wall.
- **Down Y**: 19 damage. Guard Break — Ki explosion from an outstretched hand. Decent range.
- **Up Y**: 18 damage total = 8 (first hit) + 10 (second hit), uppercut with optional second hit on double-tap. Links into his Aerial Y if both hits land; can chain from Y against a wall.
- **Aerial Y**: 18 damage. Lunges forward and spikes opponent down through the floor.
- Ultimate Action (Select): Vegeta charges energy, slowly restoring SP.

## Specials by koma
**4-Koma**:
- Special A: large beam, 34 damage. Fast, tall hitbox.
- Special B: guard-break arm-push knockback attack, 32 damage. Author notes it is usually the strongest 4-Koma special unless opponent is wall-pinned (in which case the beam special A does "about 20 some" damage instead — author's approximation).

**B5a and B5b** (both variants share the same specials per guide):
- Special A: several Ki blasts, tap X repeatedly for more hits, 39 damage total. Author says it rarely gets more than 3 hits in before pushback separates the opponent — calls it not worth using.
- Special B: diagonal downward beam after a jump, 56 damage. Author notes 56 damage applies specifically when the opponent is on a platform above Vegeta; on equal ground level it's 48 damage instead. High risk/lag if it misses.

**B6**:
- Special A: small exploding Ki ball, 52 damage. Strong but short range, large start/end lag.
- Special B "Final Flash": 80 damage but reduces Vegeta's own HP to 1. Author advises against using it due to slowness/cost.

## Support Koma versions
- S2 (Support 2): described as "Special A of B4," 26 damage.
- S3 (Support 3): described as "Special B of B5," 36 damage.

## Combos (guide's own notation, B/Y = attack buttons, arrows = direction)
1. `B, >B, /\Y (both hits), Aerial Y` — fast, decent damage, ring-out potential (author warns it can accidentally RO the opponent in matches where that's against the rules).
2. `B, /\B, >Y (all hits if possible)` — unexpected since Up-B normally knocks opponents out of combo range; Forward-Y's multi-hit adds damage.
3. `B, >B, Y` — knocks opponent down; near an edge a follow-up Y can disrupt their recovery jump for a likely ring-out.
4. `B, /\B, dash forward, then Combo 1 or 3` — can omit the leading B of combo 1/3 since the player may already be pressing forward-B out of the dash.
5. `B, /\B, X` (Koma 6 only) — short special-ended combo; vulnerable to aerial interrupt if opponent predicts the Up-B.
6. `B, /\B, Up-X or X` (Up-X is 5-Koma only; X works on any koma) — knocks opponent up/forward then finishes with a beam special; caveat: vulnerable after the Up-X special.

## Support combos
- Allen 2, registered to R: "R, then a split second later, Combo 4." First hits of combo 4 line the opponent up in front of Allen's arm cannon; all 5 of Allen's laser-beam hits connect for 30 damage, knocking opponent back down to be comboed further.
- Trunks 3: same setup as the Allen combo, except perform Up-B before Trunks fires his Burning attack at the end. Timing described as difficult.
- Gotenks 2/3 or Hyoga 3: `Up-B, R (support), wait, then Combo 1`. Best against a wall-pinned opponent.
- Joseph 2 or Shishio 2: `B, >B, Y, R (support), Up-X or X`. Up-X works for 4 and 5 Koma; X works for 4 or 6 Koma. Timing of the R press differs since Joseph is described as coming out later than Shishio.

## Deck-building notes (numeric-relevant only)
- Vegeta's power-type koma and his B5b knowledge-variant are both weak to "laughter" characters that use punches/kicks — author recommends Luffy's or Kinnikuman's Help Koma (damage reducers for punches/kicks) as a counter.
- Sakura 3 support koma heals Vegeta by two "meats" (a healing item) — useful if the player plans to use Final Flash (B6 Special B) often, since that special drops Vegeta's HP to 1.
- Piccolo's Help Koma gives Vegeta "+8 HP" and slow health regen during battle (numeric HP boost).
- Ranking given by author: 4-Koma is Vegeta's best (only one with an easy guard-break special), then 6-Koma, then 5-Koma.
- Seiya (5 or 6) is mentioned as having a separate SP-less combo dealing 77 damage that can combo into a support (note: this is Seiya's data, not Vegeta's, included here only because it appears verbatim in the source).

## Mechanic-revealing observations
- B is explicitly called a "parrying" move that cancels an opponent's attack, used as the primary combo starter — indicates a priority/clash mechanic on jabs.
- Up-B's lack of direct combo continuation, but ability to link via a dash-cancel afterward, suggests a recovery-cancel-into-dash mechanic window.
- Forward Y and Up Y both note the ability to chain "against a wall," implying wall-bounce/wall-stun behavior distinct from open-field hitstun.
- Down Y's classification as Guard Break with "decent range" and Special B (B4) also as Guard Break — establishes multiple guard-break-flagged moves per character.
- Ring-out mechanics are explicitly tied to specific combo strings and to increasing knockback (RO potential) rather than being uniform across all combos.
