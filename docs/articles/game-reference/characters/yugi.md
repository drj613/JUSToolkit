## Evolution Chart

- 2 Support Koma slots (Support 1, Support 2)

- A Question Koma (unspecified extra unlock)

No gem costs given for Yugi's evolutions in this guide (unlike other guides in this set).

## Koma Forms

- Yugi Help Koma: shape not detailed (single tile implied).
- Yugi Support Koma 1: 2-tile shape (side by side).
- Yugi Support Koma 2: 3-tile L-shape (2 across, 1 below).
- Yugi 4-koma Battle Character: Nature = Knowledge, shape = 4 tiles stacked vertically (1x4 column).
- Yugi 5-koma Battle Character: Nature = Knowledge, shape = 1 tile top, 2x2 block below (5 total).
- Yugi 6-koma Battle Character: Nature = Knowledge, shape = 2 rows of 3 (3x2 block).

## Support/Help Koma Effects

**Yugi Help Koma**: Increases battle character's SP gauge when attacking or blocking characters of the opposite nature.

**Yugi Support Koma 1** (summons Dark Magician + Dark Magician Girl, each fires an energy blast forward):
- Power characters: 26 damage
- Knowledge characters: 26 damage
- Laughter characters: 39 damage

**Yugi Support Koma 2** (summons Monster Reborn, revives one of your KO'd characters without healing them; does not work in survival matches): 0 damage.

**Kaiba Help Koma**: Battle character's SP gauge regenerates if you don't change characters or use support characters.

**Kaiba Support Koma 1** (summons Crush Card Virus — 4 crush cards in a semi-circle in front of Kaiba): 0 damage, but applies a support-koma seal to the opponent if hit.

**Kaiba Support Koma 2** (summons Blue Eyes White Dragon on top of Kaiba, fires a straight beam from its mouth):
- Power: 20–34 damage
- Knowledge: 20–34 damage
- Laughter: 20–34 damage
- Author notes these vary because it's a ranged attack.

**Jonouchi Katsuya Help Koma**: Increases SP gauge when using multiple special attacks within a short period of time.

**Hiroto Honda Help Koma**: Increases SP gauge when hitting multiple times.

**Anzu Mazaki Help Koma**: Increases max HP by varying amounts depending on koma level (no exact numbers given).

## Specials

**4-koma Special A** — Summons The Winged Dragon of Ra, shoots a yellow energy beam forward:
- Approx. 24 damage vs Power/Knowledge (author flags as approximate)
- Approx. 36 damage vs Laughter (author flags as approximate)

**4-koma Special B** — Identical to Yugi Support Koma 2 (Monster Reborn revive, 0 damage).

**5-koma Special A** — Summons Obelisk the Tormentor; lightning from the summon also deals damage, followed by a punch:
- vs Power/Knowledge: 5 damage (lightning) + 48 damage (punch); if lightning connects with punch, combined 53 damage total. Opponent knocked into the air.
- vs Laughter: 7 damage (lightning) + 72 damage (punch); combined lightning+punch = 79 damage total. Opponent knocked into the air.

**5-koma Special B** — Summons a large sword appearing over all opponents; causes a support-seal effect:
- vs Power/Knowledge: 36 damage
- vs Laughter: 54 damage

**6-koma Special A** — Summons Slifer the Sky Dragon, fires a beam from its mouth; also deals summon damage from lightning. If the enemy is hit by the lightning, the beam will not also connect:
- Beam: approx. 32 damage vs Power/Knowledge, approx. 48 damage vs Laughter (author flags as approximate)
- Lightning: 5 damage vs Power/Knowledge, 7 damage vs Laughter

**6-koma Special B** — Plays Spellbinding Circle over all opponents, drains all their SP. 0 damage.

**Ultimate Action** — Yugi switches with another of your battle characters (no combat numbers).

## Passive Effects

- Gains SP when using support characters.
- Gains SP when fighting against Laughter-type characters.
- Can see invisible battle characters.

## Mechanic-Revealing Observations

- B+Left/Right (Dungeon Dice) is a multi-hit move (1–5 hits) that stuns the opponent for the duration of the hits — evidence of per-hit hitstun during multi-hit attacks.
- Y (air) and B+Up both apply a fixed ~0.5 second stun on hit, separate from normal hitstun — suggests a distinct "stun" status with its own duration used by certain moves.
- 5-koma Special A and 6-koma Special A: lightning-then-projectile sequences where hitting the lightning changes/prevents the follow-up hit from connecting — indicates hitbox sequencing/exclusivity where an earlier hit can consume or displace the target out of a later hitbox's path.
- B+Down (Gaia) forces the opponent to change their active battle character on hit — a recognized "forced character swap" mechanic shared with other characters' Down+B moves.
