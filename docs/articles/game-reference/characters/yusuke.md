## Koma details

### 1-koma (help)
- Equip effect: Special (SP) gauge regenerates while Yusuke is on the field (not in active battle slot, per typical help koma behavior — author doesn't clarify further).

### 2-koma (support)
- Effect: Yusuke stands for about a second, then combos into a final punch that guard-breaks.
- Damage: 26 (power/laughter), 33 (knowledge)
- Author notes it can create an opening to use a special afterward.

### 3-koma (support)
- Effect: Yusuke charges his fist for ~2 seconds, then fires a spirit shotgun forward, up, and down (up/down each at a 45-degree angle).
- No damage numbers given; author considers it too slow to be reliable (opponent likely blocks first).

### 4-koma (battle)
- J-Soul: 152
- Nature: Power
- Shape: `[][][]` over `[]` (offset, single block under middle/left)
- X: Spirit Gun (quick)
- Up X: spirit-energy punch

## Combos (mechanic-revealing notes)

- B, forward+B, Y
- B, forward+B, Y, forward+Y
- B, forward+B, up+Y
- B, up+B, (aerial)B or (aerial)Y — "may not work out every time" (implies inconsistent hit-confirm/timing window)
- B, forward+B, up+B, (aerial)Y — author notes "a short pause between forward+B and up+B," implying a linking gap/recovery window
- "The Almighty Yusuke Combo": B, Y, up+Y, then a support/demon-form finisher or spirit gun
- Most combos can end with an X (spirit gun) input as a finisher, implying cancel-into-special behavior after certain normal strings.

### Support-assisted combo damage examples

- "Dodge This!": using Hien (Sakigake!! Otokojuku) 3-koma support after a basic combo — Hien lands 4 hits, 4th hit inflicts Paralysis status, then charge a spirit gun. Total combo damage: ~91–100 to power/laughter, ~150-ish to knowledge (author estimate, unconfirmed).
- "Suppppppppper!": using Franky (One Piece) 3-koma support mid-combo (activate during Y, then use up+Y while Franky swings), dealing 76 damage to power/laughter/knowledge (all three listed equal — author unconfirmed, may be a simplification).
- "Yusuke Rendan": using Naruto (Naruto) 3-koma support, any B attacks into Y triggers Naruto follow-up. Author notes this combo is "still in testing" and inconsistent — sometimes Naruto's follow-up (Naruto Rendan) doesn't fully execute. Also notes: with 4-koma Yusuke, sometimes up+X can hit the enemy while they're still in hitstun from being knocked down.

## Deck-building notes (support/help synergy)

- Kuwabara: increases SP gauge when attacking with or blocking with a Battle Character; his 2-koma support useful for combos, 3-koma considered weak (no numbers given).
- Genkai: uses SP gauge for Auto-Guard (drains SP).
- Neuro: immunity to Paralysis; 2-koma causes Blindness effect on opponent; 3-koma grants invisibility.

## Pros/cons (author's summary, non-numeric)

Pros: UA (Ultimate Action) powers up Y attacks; ranged attacks/specials; gains more SP when health is low; strong combos; easy to pair with supports.
Cons: lag before specials come out; caps at 6-koma (no higher evolution).
