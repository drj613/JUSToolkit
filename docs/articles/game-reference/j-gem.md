## Gem colors and nature mapping

- Red Gems = "Power Gems" (Power/Strength nature)
- Green Gems = "Knowledge Gems" (Knowledge nature)
- Yellow Gems = "Laughter Gems" (Laughter nature)
- Blue, Purple, White Gems = no stated nature mapping in guide; treated as a separate, harder-to-earn tier ("B/P/W")

Abbreviations used by author: R/G/Y = Red/Green/Yellow; B/P/W = Blue/Purple/White.

## Earning gems in Story/Free battle stages

Rules as described (author does not cite exact source, presumably observed in "collect items before time limit" stage type):

- Objective items in these stages do NOT need to be collected to get gems.
- Winning the match is NOT required — only need to defeat (KO) opponents and pick up the gems they drop.
- Opponent-dropped gems can be picked up by the enemy AI too (author notes enemies tend to grab dropped gems even without needing them — a competitive pickup race exists).
- Kill method changes gem drop:
  - Normal attack (including guard break) or a Support character kill → drops **12 gems**, split into **4 pieces of 3 gems each** ("hard to get" — implies multiple separate pickups, more chance for AI to steal them).
  - Special Attack kill (X or Up+X — guide states these are equivalent for this purpose) → drops **one large gem worth 20 gems**, colored according to the killed character's nature.

### Specific farming stages (author-recommended, not necessarily unique)

- **Red/Power gems**: Eyeshield 21 planet, mission 2, J-Galaxy. Enemies: Gon, Sanji, "The Red Koma" — all Power-type, drop Power gems.
- **Green/Knowledge gems**: Ninku stage (green cap symbol), J-Space — fight "Ninkus." Noted enemy hazard: a fast rasengan-like move that interrupts specials.
- **Yellow/Laughter gems**: Gintama World (white dog-face symbol), mission 2. Enemies: Gintoki, Kagura, Ryotsu Kankichi. Hazard: support character "Honda Hayato" inflicts a Stop status effect.
- **Blue/Purple/White gems**: No farmable Story stage identified by author as efficient. Sources:
  - Win (only win counts) a Wi-fi match → 10 B/P/W gems per win (see Wi-fi table below).
  - Break boxes in mission 1 of the last world — author calls this low-value/high-variance, not recommended.
  - Beat the J-Arena Champion Match — author's top recommendation (see Ranking section).

## Deckbuilding strategy notes (author opinion, for gem farming only)

- Recommended approach: build 3 separate single-battle-character decks (one per R/G/Y target), each character strong with specials of the *opposite* nature from the enemies being farmed (nature-type advantage assumed to matter for damage/kill speed — mechanic itself not detailed numerically here).
- Character-specific recommendations (opinion, not mechanically load-bearing):
  - Power farming: Don Patch (7-koma). Up+X fits vertical stage geometry. Warning: his other special risks Ring Out on the target, and Ring Out kills do NOT drop gems (author unconfirmed mechanic, but stated as fact by author — treat as author-unconfirmed since no citation given).
  - Knowledge farming: Bankai Ichigo (8-koma), Up+X is an AOE special (saves SP by hitting multiple targets at once). Alt: Caramelman J (8-koma final form of Dr. Mashirito) — Laughter-type character but has strong Power specials.
  - Laughter farming: Sasuke (vertical 8-koma). Up+X is AOE with a chance to inflict Paralyze status on enemies (author unconfirmed exact chance/mechanism).

## J-Arena Ranking rewards

Four ranking modes, must be completed in order to unlock the last:
1. Point Match — win condition: highest point total.
2. Survival Match — win condition: KO all opponents before time runs out.
3. Event Match — win condition: collect all stars, or have the highest star count when time runs out.
4. Champion Match — unlocked only after clearing the above 3. Consists of **20 consecutive battles**.

Losing a ranking match sends the player to "Sudden Death Mode" (goal: place 1st).

Gem rewards:
- Completing Point Match, Survival Match, or Event Match: **500 gems each of Red, Green, Yellow** (author's wording implies 500 R + 500 G + 500 Y per completed mode, i.e., up to 1500 each color across the 3 modes — read as "500 of each" per mode completion).
- Completing Champion Match: **500 Red + 500 Green + 500 Yellow + 200 Blue + 200 Purple + 400 White** gems (one-time/per-completion reward).

No numeric bound given on how many times Champion Match can be repeated for repeat rewards; author notes it can take "more than 10" attempts due to difficulty/luck.

## Quiz mode

- **5 gems per correct answer**.
- Quiz only grants R/G/Y gems (no B/P/W).
- Gem color per correct answer is not specified (unclear if random, fixed, or tied to question category — gap in source).
- Wireless Quiz mode played against another player is suggested as an easy way to farm answers/gems regardless of actual Japanese-language comprehension (opinion, not a mechanic).
