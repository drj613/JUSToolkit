Treat all numbers as **author-claimed, not verified against the ROM/binary**. Several values are explicitly flagged by the author as uncertain (marked below with "(author unconfirmed)").

## Scope and measurement conventions (author's stated methodology)

- Damage values are measured **against a neutral-nature opponent**. Nature match-ups (advantage/disadvantage) are not reflected in these numbers.
- Moves covered: B, Up B, Forward B, Down B, Air B, Y, Up Y, Forward Y, Down Y, Air Y, touch-screen "tap" damage, koma X/upX specials (per koma count 4–8), and Ultimate Attack (UA) if it deals direct damage.
- If a move can be charged, only the base (uncharged) value is used for "Average Damage"; the charged value is listed separately as `base/charged`.
- For beam-type attacks, only the **maximum** possible value is recorded (often requires the opponent to have their back to a wall — see Edge Cases).
- "Average Damage" per character = average of that character's non-special moves (B-set + Y-set), using uncharged values.
- Tap damage is rounded to the nearest tenth.
- Excludes Taizo 4 (a beta/hacking-only koma with no attack moves).
- Battle characters at 7-koma tier have **no alternate/nature-swap koma**; every other koma tier (4, 5, 6, 8) has at least one alternate for some characters.

## Edge cases and mechanics that constrain implementation

- **Dio's Y move (32 vs. 20):** only reaches 32 damage if the first hit whiffs and the second hit connects (triggering an additional follow-up hit); normally deals 20. More reliable near a wall.
- **"Ledge fall" infinite-style damage stacking:** Boo, Sanji, Don Patch, and Momotaro's Up Y can deal drastically higher damage (Boo 72, Sanji 60, Don Patch 116, Momotaro 120) when used against an opponent hanging on a ledge over a wall that doesn't break easily — damage keeps accumulating as they fall. Maximum found on the Namek stage due to its unusually tall vertical drop.
- **Beam-type attacks:** generally reach maximum damage only when the opponent's back is against a wall (applies broadly, not just to specific characters).
- **Button-mash-dependent maxima:** Dr. Mashirito 6upX/7upX, Luffy 6X, and Jotaro's Y/Up Y/Air Y reach their listed maximum only through mashing the attack input.
- **Caramelman J Air B / Boo Forward Y:** only reach their max listed value (40 / 24) when the opponent is against a wall.
- **Edajima Up Y:** author unconfirmed — usually 24, but claims 26 if there is a wall directly above the opponent during the move.
- **Eve 5upX (46):** author unconfirmed/rare — typical results are 34 or 38, occasionally 42, with 46 obtained only rarely across repeated testing.
- **Kinnikuman 6upX (69 vs 2 opponents):** derived by binary-search-style trial and error in training mode with regeneration disabled; author is confident in 69 as the value when the move connects with two opponents simultaneously (vs. presumably lower for one target — see per-character entry, base 6X is 39).
- **Train Air Y (27, rare):** requires a specific distance from the opponent (who must be backed to a wall) so the fired bullet hits the opponent and the ground simultaneously and reflects for a second hit; normally only deals 15 or 12.
- **Gon's specials scale with UA charge level:** each koma's X special has 3 tiers — base, +UA level 1, +UA level 2 (e.g. 4X: 36/40/44).
- **Sanji's "Mellorine" status:** most of his moves have a drastically higher damage ceiling under this status, and the ceiling differs slightly between his 5-koma and 6-koma versions (e.g. Down B: 33 max on Sanji-5 vs 31 max on Sanji-6).
- **Hiei's "Ensatsu" status:** self-applied via Hiei's 5upX; while active, all of Hiei's normal moves (B-set, Y-set, tap) get a flat damage increase to a second listed value.
- **Franky's "cola" power-ups:** each move has 3 tiers of damage — base, +1 cola, +2 colas — with cola presumably being a stackable buff item/resource.
- **Character-relationship notes (author observation, not a rule):** Ichigo vs. Bankai Ichigo — Ichigo has higher B-set damage but lower Y-set damage; Ichigo's charged Y moves are comparable to Bankai Ichigo's uncharged Y. Luffy vs. Gear 2 Luffy — Gear 2 has higher B-set damage but the same Y-set damage. Naruto vs. Kyuubi Naruto — minimal damage change. Nami vs. PCT Nami — only 3 moves differ in damage value between forms.
